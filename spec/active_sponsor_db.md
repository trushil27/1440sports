# Active Sponsor + Activation Database

The canonical ground-truth source for who is currently sponsoring F1 and Formula E, at what level, and in what category. Used by the engine as a pre-flight check before any signal is surfaced, any brief is built, or any outreach is drafted — to prevent the engine from pitching a company into a slot that is already occupied or a category that is locked.

**Last full verification: 20 May 2026**

**Refresh cadence:** Quarterly minimum (next: 20 August 2026). Spot-update whenever a partner announcement is detected in daily signal scanning.

**Source hierarchy for entries in this database:**
1. F1.com / fiaformulae.com official partners pages (Tier 1)
2. Team-published partner pages on team domains (Tier 1)
3. Company press release announcing the partnership (Tier 1)
4. Reputable trade press confirmation (BlackBook Motorsport, SportsPro, BusinessWire) (Tier 2)
5. Aggregator sites (PitDebrief, RTRSports) where corroborated by at least one Tier-1 source (Tier 2)

**Anti-hallucination rule:** If a sponsor cannot be traced to at least one Tier-1 or one corroborated Tier-2 source in this file, the engine treats it as unconfirmed and flags the gap rather than inferring.

## SECTION 6 — ENGINE PRE-FLIGHT CHECK PROTOCOL

Before any signal is surfaced, brief is built, or outreach drafted, the engine MUST run this five-step check against this database.

```
PRE-FLIGHT CHECK — runs before scoring output / brief render

Step 1 — IDENTITY CHECK
  Question: Is the target company itself an existing F1 or FE sponsor 
            (or owned by one)?
  Action:   If YES → flag as "existing sponsor — pitch is upsell or 
            tier-upgrade, not net-new." Score adjusts accordingly.

Step 2 — CATEGORY LOCK CHECK
  Question: What industry does the target sit in? Cross-reference 
            Section 5.1 (F1) or 5.2 (FE).
  Action:   If category is LOCKED at championship → engine must pitch 
            team-level only, OR redirect to other series. The brief 
            DEAL ARCHITECTURE section must reflect this constraint.

Step 3 — TEAM SLOT CHECK
  Question: For each candidate team in the "Best Team Matches" section, 
            does the team already have a partner in the same category?
  Action:   If YES at the same tier → that team match is invalid; remove 
            from recommendation. If YES at a lower tier → note as 
            potential upgrade play, not net-new.

Step 4 — CONFLICT CHECK
  Question: Does the target compete directly with any existing sponsor 
            of the recommended team?
  Action:   If YES → flag as incompatible team match. Direct competitors 
            on the same team livery are exceptionally rare and require 
            specific carve-out.

Step 5 — CATEGORY DENSITY NOTE
  Question: How crowded is the broader category across the grid?
  Action:   If 5+ teams already have a partner in this category → note 
            in brief WHY ALPINE / WHY [TEAM] section that the team's 
            existing roster doesn't conflict and explain the slot specifically.
```

**Output of the pre-flight check** is appended to every signal report and every brief as a "Pre-Flight Status" field, with explicit pass/flag/block result for each of the 5 steps and the specific lockholders identified.

If Step 2 or Step 3 returns BLOCK, the engine does not surface the signal in Daily Top 3 without an explicit "category conflict but pursue at lower tier" framing.

## SECTION 8 — MAINTAINING THIS FILE

**Append-only convention:** Never silently remove an entry. If a partner departs, mark `DEPARTED [date]` and keep the row, so that historical context for alumni intelligence is preserved.

**Update triggers:**
1. Daily scan picks up a partner announcement → manually verify against Tier-1 source, then update relevant section.
2. Quarterly full sweep: re-verify each team's partner page on team domain + F1.com/fiaformulae.com.
3. Pre-season check: verify full grid composition + new entries (e.g. Cadillac 2026, Citroën Racing 2026).

**When uncertain — flag, don't infer.** Better to mark a slot "engine to verify per round" than to write something the brief might cite as fact. The whole purpose of this file is to be the place the engine trusts; if any entry in it is wrong, every brief built off it is wrong.
