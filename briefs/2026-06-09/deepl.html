The Case

DeepL is Europe's enterprise language-AI champion: a Cologne-based, Polish-rooted company (founded 2017) that raised $300M at a $2B valuation and is now exploring a US IPO that could value it at up to $5B, possibly in 2026. Its enterprise Language AI platform - translation and localization built with enterprise security - serves more than 200,000 businesses, governments and organizations across 228 markets. For an international championship like Formula E, a global-communication brand is an unusually clean narrative fit: European-rooted, enterprise-credible, sustainability-neutral, and used by exactly the multinational enterprises FE's audience comprises. No language or communication brand holds an FE partnership, so the lane is open, and DeepL's pre-IPO brand-building phase is the moment to plant it.

Why Now

DeepL is in a pre-IPO brand-building phase - exploring a ~$5B US listing possibly in 2026 - when enterprise credibility compounds most, and founder-CEO Jarek Kutylowski is steering a deliberate B2B scale-up. The FE enterprise/communication lane is uncontested, so moving now secures category ownership cheaply before a peer notices the open international-brand stage.

Why Jaguar TCS Racing

Jaguar TCS Racing is the fit: a British, premium, enterprise-and-sustainability-led team whose global, blue-chip audience matches DeepL's multinational enterprise and government buyers, and whose IT-services title partner (TCS) signals a roster comfortable with enterprise-software brands. DeepL's language-AI lane is distinct from Jaguar's existing partners (TCS, Chase), so it adds cleanly - and with the FE team inventory tightening as the pipeline fills, Jaguar is the cleanest open enterprise lane left.

Value to Jaguar TCS Racing  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B. DeepL's platform localizes content and communications across 228 markets - directly useful to a globally-broadcast team managing fan, sponsor and partner communications across languages, plus internal multinational operations. Jaguar gains a real localization capability and a clean enterprise co-marketing partner; DeepL gains a premium, international brand stage in front of the multinational enterprises it sells to.

Deal Architecture

Official Language AI Partner tier on Jaguar TCS Racing, minimum three years, ~$3-4M/yr, with category exclusivity for translation/language AI and a working localization deployment for the team's global content and communications.
