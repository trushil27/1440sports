The Case

Quantinuum is the highest-priority class in our mandate: a born-big spin-off (out of Honeywell) that just completed the largest quantum-computing IPO to date - pricing at $60/share for a $1.68B upsized raise and closing its 4 June 2026 Nasdaq debut at roughly $15.7B (ticker QNT), with Honeywell and Cambridge Quantum retaining ~82%. An IPO is a one-time brand-elevation moment, and a newly public deep-tech company needs exactly the global enterprise visibility and mainstream credibility an F1 association provides - which technical channels alone cannot buy. It is freshly capitalized (it raised $600M at a $10B pre-money only in September 2025, backed by NVIDIA, JPMorgan, Quanta and others), even though FY2025 revenue was a modest ~$31M - so capacity here comes from the balance sheet, not the P&L. Because no quantum brand exists anywhere on the grid, Quantinuum would define the category rather than contest it.

Why Now

The listing just happened (4 June) - the post-IPO window is precisely when a newly public company invests in institutional credibility and a differentiated brand, with fresh capital to deploy. A multi-year F1 innovation-partner deal now compounds the moment, turning the finance milestone into a sustained global brand. There is no competitor clock, so the deal can be shaped on Quantinuum's terms - but the brand-elevation window is widest in the months right after the bell.

Why Aston Martin

Aston Martin is the fit. Its premium engineering gravitas and British identity align with Quantinuum's Cambridge-and-Colorado footprint, and its hospitality audience is exactly the C-suite and government-adjacent enterprise buyer a quantum business courts. The association positions Quantinuum as 'the quantum edge in performance R&D' - a precision-and-compute halo on the grid's most boardroom-credible team - rather than as a mass-market consumer sponsor, which is the wrong register for this category.

Value to Aston Martin  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A (developing). Quantum and advanced classical optimisation increasingly touch the problems an F1 team actually faces - aerodynamic and strategy simulation, logistics and scheduling across a global calendar, and materials R&D. Positioned as an innovation and R&D partner, Quantinuum offers Aston Martin a credible compute-frontier collaboration and a powerful talent-attraction halo, with a genuine roadmap toward simulation use cases rather than an empty logo. The honest framing is a forward-looking technical partnership: real engagement on hard optimisation problems, growing into deeper integration over the multi-year term.

Deal Architecture

Official Innovation / Quantum Partner tier on Aston Martin, minimum three years, estimated $3-6M/yr, structured as an R&D-and-brand halo, with category exclusivity for quantum computing and a defined joint R&D workstream (optimisation/simulation) so the partnership has technical substance from day one.

Risks & Counters

Newly listed optics  As a freshly public stock, leadership attention and spend are calibrated to public-market optics, and securities rules constrain messaging around the listing. Counter: Position as institutional-credibility brand-building - exactly what a newly public company needs - with measurable enterprise and talent outcomes, and activate after the initial lock-up noise settles.
Deep-tech brand caution  A quantum firm may view F1 as off-strategy or too consumer-facing, and FY2025 revenue (~$31M) invites spend scrutiny. Counter: Position strictly as enterprise credibility, government/partner visibility and talent attraction with a real joint R&D workstream; the $1.68B just raised funds it comfortably - not consumer marketing.
