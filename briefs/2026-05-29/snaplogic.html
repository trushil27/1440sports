The Case

On 12 May 2026 SnapLogic appointed Molly Matthews (ex-Microsoft enterprise GTM) as President while promoting its CCO and CPO - a deliberate scale-up with new budget authority. Backed by a16z, Microsoft, Silver Lake Waterman, Sixth Street and Capital One Ventures, with $100M+ ARR (Aug 2025) and customers including AstraZeneca, Adobe, Verizon and Sony. The decisive signal: Confluent, a direct iPaaS rival, is an active partner on the Williams 2026 livery.

Why Now
