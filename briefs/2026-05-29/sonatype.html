The Case

Sonatype occupies precisely the same software-supply-chain-security whitespace as JFrog, which makes it the classic counter-narrative target. If JFrog (or Snyk) signs a team, Sonatype's urgency spikes overnight and a rival-team pitch becomes highly compelling - the exact SnapLogic-versus-Confluent playbook of turning a competitor's sponsorship into the prospect's reason to act. Capacity is more modest and ownership is private equity (Vista), so the right structure is a disciplined value tier with a clear competitive-defence rationale rather than a premium land-grab.

Why Now

Trigger-based: this prospect should be escalated to HOT and approached immediately if and when JFrog or Snyk commits to a team. Until then it is a primed, pre-researched lead to activate the day the category's first domino falls.
