The Case

Abnormal Security, an AI behavioral-security leader, raised a $250M Series D at a $5.1B valuation and has publicly stated it wants to be able to operate as a public company. It has the capacity and the brand-building motive of a pre-IPO leader, sits in the open cybersecurity category on the F1 grid, and has never entered motorsport. A useful nuance: its marketing leadership is in transition (the prior CMO, Doug Laird, is no longer listed on the current executive team, which now shows VP-level product and revenue marketing), so brand and sponsorship strategy is being re-decided - approach the CEO and help shape it.

Why Now

Pre-IPO leaders invest in enterprise brand to support a future listing, the cybersecurity category on the grid rewards a first mover, and the open CMO seat means the strategic framing set now will define the incoming hire's plan. There is no competitor clock yet, so the work here is to create the urgency rather than react to it - which favours an early, relationship-led approach.

Why Haas

Haas offers an accessible, lean entry tier with open enterprise-software and security categories - a sensible first motorsport step at a sensible price for a company making its debut in the space, with room to scale to a bigger team at the next contract if the model proves out.

Value to Haas  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. F1 teams are high-value targets for business-email compromise and social-engineering attacks aimed at finance and partnerships staff handling large sponsor flows. Abnormal's platform is a credible, deployable security partner with real protective value for the team, supporting an authentic CISO-facing 'official email-security partner' story rather than a logo placement.

Deal Architecture

Official Security Partner tier on Haas, minimum three years, ~$2.5-4M/yr, with category exclusivity and a working deployment protecting the team's email and finance functions.

Primary Decision-Maker

Evan Reiser
CEO & Co-Founder, Abnormal Security - verified (note: CMO seat in transition; current team shows VP-level marketing)
CEO and co-founder, and the owner of Abnormal's IPO-readiness narrative and top-level brand direction while the CMO role is unsettled - the right entry point today. Approach Reiser directly and offer a ready-made sponsorship thesis for the incoming marketing leader to execute.

Opening Angle

"Evan, pre-IPO is exactly when enterprise brand compounds, security is wide open on the F1 grid, and Haas is the smart, ownable first step. With the CMO seat in flux, this is your call - 25 minutes?"
