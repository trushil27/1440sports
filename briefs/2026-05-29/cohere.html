The Case

Cohere is an enterprise-focused AI company (co-headquartered in Toronto and San Francisco) recently valued at roughly $7B with about $150M in ARR and a founder who has signalled interest in a public listing. Unlike consumer-AI labs, Cohere's whole positioning is enterprise-grade, secure, deployable 'AI you can trust in production' - a register that fits Formula E's enterprise-and-sustainability audience far better than a saturated F1 fintech lane. No frontier or enterprise-AI brand currently holds a Formula E team partnership, so the category is open, and an FE entry would give Cohere a differentiated, internationally-credible brand stage as it builds toward the public markets.

Why Now

Cohere is in an active enterprise brand-building phase ahead of a likely listing, and the enterprise-AI category in Formula E is empty - a combination that rewards moving before peers (and before any of the larger AI labs) recognise the same open lane. The absence of a dated trigger argues for a relationship-led approach now, while category exclusivity is uncontested and inexpensive.

Why Nissan Formula E

Nissan is the fit: a global EV pioneer with deep Formula E pedigree and an international, technology-forward brand that pairs naturally with Cohere's Canadian-and-Californian, enterprise-AI identity. The team's electric-mobility story and Cohere's 'practical AI for real enterprises' story reinforce each other, and Nissan's audience skews to exactly the kind of large, regulated enterprises Cohere sells to.

Value to Nissan Formula E  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. AI-driven race strategy, energy-management modelling for an all-electric car, and large-scale data analysis are genuine Formula E problems where Cohere's enterprise models could be deployed as a working technical partnership, not just branding. Nissan gains a real AI collaboration and a credible innovation narrative; Cohere gains a live, demonstrable deployment it can reference with enterprise buyers and investors.

Deal Architecture

Official Enterprise AI Partner tier on Nissan Formula E, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr, with AI-category exclusivity and a defined technical workstream (strategy/energy modelling) so the partnership has substance.
