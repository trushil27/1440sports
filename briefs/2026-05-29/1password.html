The Case

1Password has raised $920M (last round at $6.8B) and is repeatedly named a 2026 IPO lock. Enterprise identity and access security is unrepresented in Formula E, and 1Password's North American brand and recent leadership expansion map cleanly onto Andretti. A pre-IPO multi-year partnership lets the company build enterprise credibility while entering a category no rival holds, on a championship whose enterprise-friendly, sustainability-forward audience matches its buyer profile.

Why Now

Pre-IPO brand investment plus an open category in a growing, enterprise-credible championship make this the moment to plant a flag - before the listing, when category exclusivity is cheapest, and while leadership is actively shaping the next-phase brand.

Why Andretti Formula E

Andretti's North American identity and Formula E growth platform fit 1Password's brand and buyer base, and Andretti's recent embrace of technology partners (including an AI partnership) shows an organisation receptive to enterprise-tech storytelling rather than pure consumer signage.

Value to Andretti Formula E  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. A racing organisation's distributed staff, drivers and partners need secure credential and access management across many systems and locations; 1Password is a real, deployable operational partner. The deal can include securing the team's own access infrastructure, giving Andretti genuine value and 1Password an authentic 'who has access' story plus enterprise hospitality.

Deal Architecture

Official Identity Security Partner tier on Andretti Formula E, minimum three years, ~$3-4M/yr, with category exclusivity and a working deployment for the team.
