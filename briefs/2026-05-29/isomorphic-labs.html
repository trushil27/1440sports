The Case

Isomorphic Labs, the DeepMind/Alphabet spin-out applying AI to drug discovery, took its first external capital ($600M) in 2025, entering unicorn territory, and has begun building out its leadership (appointing Colin Murdoch, who also serves as Google DeepMind's Chief Business Officer, as its first President). The standout attribute is low inbound crowding (~25): almost no one in motorsport has called them, so 1440 could be early by a wide margin. It is a British, precision-science brand whose talent-attraction and credibility goals could be served by a measured, tasteful Formula E association - a long-horizon relationship to build now, before the company scales its brand spend and the crowd arrives.

Why Now

This is a relationship-first play, not a deal to force this quarter. The value of acting now is positional: getting in while crowding is near zero and a newly-formed commercial leadership is open to ideas, so that when Isomorphic does turn to brand and talent marketing, 1440 is already the trusted motorsport partner rather than one of fifty inbound pitches.

Why Jaguar TCS Racing

Jaguar TCS Racing - British, premium, technology-and-sustainability-led - is the natural cultural and geographic fit for a London deep-tech brand, and its blue-chip audience suits a science-led company focused on credibility and recruitment over consumer reach.

Value to Jaguar TCS Racing  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B. This is primarily a science, innovation and talent-brand association rather than an in-car technical integration; the value sits in a shared 'precision and discovery' narrative and a recruiting halo for both organisations. Kept deliberately modest and optional-to-scale, it gives Jaguar a distinctive, prestige-science partner and Isomorphic a tasteful, high-credibility brand stage - with room to deepen only if both sides see value.

Deal Architecture

Start with a modest Official Partner tier on Jaguar TCS Racing, minimum three years, ~$2-3M/yr, structured to grow, with emphasis on talent/employer-brand activation rather than heavy consumer signage.
