The Case

Snyk is a well-funded developer-security leader (over $300M ARR) whose CEO, Peter McKay, has publicly flagged a 2026 IPO. Two signals stack up. First, the security category on the F1 grid is uncontested, so a multi-year partnership would give Snyk both enterprise credibility ahead of a listing and ownership of a lane no rival holds. Second, Snyk's brand function is mid-reset: its previous CMO, Jonaki Egenolf, departed for XBOW in November 2025, which means brand and sponsorship strategy is actively being re-decided right now - the ideal moment to plant an idea. The competitive nuance to flag internally: this is the same whitespace JFrog and Sonatype are eyeing, so the category is a race, and whoever signs first owns it.

Why Now

Pre-IPO brand-building budgets, an open security category, and a brand leadership reset all coincide. With the CMO seat in transition, the decision is currently closest to the CEO, which both shortens the chain and means the strategic framing set now will shape whoever inherits the role. The window closes the moment a DevSecOps rival commits to a team.

Why Aston Martin

Aston Martin's CISO-grade gravitas and premium, technically-credible identity place Snyk's 'secure by design' story directly in front of the enterprise security buyers it sells to. If Aston Martin's inventory is constrained, Williams (data/engineering revival) is a credible alternative with the right audience profile.

Value to Aston Martin  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. Snyk integrates security directly into developer workflows, and an F1 team's expanding in-house software estate is a real testbed and reference deployment. A deal can be built around securing the team's own development pipeline, giving the team genuine operational value and Snyk an authentic 'official developer-security partner' narrative plus CISO-level hospitality - substance on both sides, not signage.

Deal Architecture

Official Developer Security Partner tier on Aston Martin, minimum three years, ~$3-4M/yr, with security-category exclusivity and a working integration into the team's development pipeline.
