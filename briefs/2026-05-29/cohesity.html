The Case

Cohesity is the rare combination our mandate prizes: a category leader at a brand-reckoning moment. Following its 2024 merger with Veritas' data-protection business, ARR surged to ~$1.5B at a 28% adjusted margin, and the Nvidia-backed company is eyeing a 2026 IPO with a valuation aimed at its public peer Rubrik (~$17B). It has never touched motorsport, so we would not be the 100th brand in the inbox - and the data-security/resilience category is unoccupied on the F1 grid.

Why Now

An IPO is the single biggest brand-elevation moment a company gets. A multi-year F1 partnership signed around the listing converts a finance event into a global enterprise-brand statement and pre-builds the institutional credibility public investors reward.

Why Williams

Williams - the data-and-engineering revival story (Atlassian title, Confluent on data streaming) - is the natural canvas for a data-resilience brand, placing Cohesity beside complementary (not competing) data partners in front of enterprise IT buyers.

Value to Williams  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. A Williams race weekend produces and must safeguard enormous volumes of telemetry, simulation output and design IP - exactly Cohesity's domain (backup, recovery, ransomware resilience). That supports an authentic technical-partner integration and 'we protect the data that wins races' storytelling, not a sticker.

Deal Architecture

Official Data Resilience Partner tier on Williams. MINIMUM THREE YEARS (ideally four to span the IPO and post-listing cycle), estimated $4-7M/yr. Garage/cockpit branding in the data-security category, hospitality at marquee rounds, digital rights for an enterprise-IT audience, category exclusivity.

Marketing leadership / CEO (IPO sponsor)
CEO Sanjay Poonen (ex-VMware) drives Cohesity's enterprise-brand and IPO narrative; confirm the current CMO and IR lead, who co-own brand-investment decisions during the listing window.

Opening Angle

"You get one IPO. An F1 data-resilience partnership turns your listing into a global enterprise-brand moment - and data security is the one category no rival on the grid can claim. Worth 25 minutes before the roadshow locks?"
