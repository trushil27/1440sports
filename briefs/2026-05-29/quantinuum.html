The Case

Quantinuum is the highest-priority class in our mandate: a born-big spin-off (from Honeywell) heading into a ~$12.7B IPO, up 27% on its $10B September 2025 valuation. An IPO is a one-time brand-elevation moment, and an F1 association is among the fastest ways for a deep-tech company to acquire global enterprise visibility. It has never entered motorsport.

Why Now

The IPO roadshow is the window: leadership is actively investing in public-market visibility and enterprise credibility. A multi-year F1 engineering-partner deal signed around the listing compounds the narrative.

Why Aston Martin

Aston Martin's premium engineering gravitas aligns Quantinuum's Anglo-American identity with a precision/compute halo - 'the quantum edge in performance R&D' - in front of a C-suite audience.

Value to Aston Martin  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A (developing). Quantum and advanced optimisation increasingly touch race strategy, aero simulation and logistics. Positioned as an R&D/innovation partner, Quantinuum offers a credible compute-frontier story and a talent-attraction halo, with a roadmap toward genuine simulation use cases.

Deal Architecture

Official Innovation / Quantum Partner tier, minimum three years, estimated $3-6M/yr, positioned as an R&D-and-brand halo around the listing, with category exclusivity for quantum computing.

Primary Decision-Maker

Quantinuum CMO / Head of Brand (verify)
Marketing / Brand leadership
