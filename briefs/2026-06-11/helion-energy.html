The Case

Helion Energy is the fusion company turning 'limitless clean power' from physics into a construction site. In June 2026 it raised a $465M Series G at a $15.5B valuation - nearly tripling from $5.43B in January 2025 - to build Orion, its first commercial plant (Malaga, WA), which will deliver 50MW to Microsoft by 2028 under the world's first fusion power-purchase agreement. In February 2026 its Polaris machine became the first privately-funded device to run on deuterium-tritium fuel and reach 150 million degrees. For 1440 the fit is narrative, not operational: fusion is the definitive clean-energy story and Formula E is the championship built to prove clean mobility, so a Helion-FE pairing is the most on-message sustainability partnership on any grid - and Helion has never touched motorsport. The honest counterweight: it is pre-revenue and pouring capital into reactors, so its near-term sponsorship appetite is the open question - a brand/relationship play to open now, not a deal to force.

Why Now

Helion just closed a $465M round at a $15.5B valuation - a peak-visibility moment - and is moving publicly from lab milestone (Polaris) to construction (Orion for Microsoft, 2028). That arc is exactly when a deep-tech brand benefits from a global, on-narrative stage and a STEM-talent magnet. No clean-energy or fusion brand holds an FE position, so the on-theme lane is open and cheap to plant now.

Why Envision Racing

Envision Racing is the sharpest fit: a sustainability-first independent whose entire identity ('Race Against Climate Change') mirrors Helion's clean-energy mission, with a clean (greenfield) roster so Helion can own the clean-energy lane outright. A manufacturer team (e.g. Nissan or Jaguar) is the alternative if a larger stage is preferred over thematic purity.

Value to Envision Racing  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - a brand, narrative and talent partnership, not in-car tech (fusion doesn't power an FE car - batteries do). The substance is real on three fronts. (1) NARRATIVE: fusion is the definitive clean-energy story and Formula E is the championship built to prove clean mobility, so pairing 'the power source of the future' with 'the racing of the future' is the most on-message sustainability partnership available on any grid - and it supercharges Envision's own 'Race Against Climate Change' identity rather than bolting a logo onto it. (2) CREDIBILITY & COMMERCIAL: Helion's profile - the world's first fusion power-purchase agreement (Microsoft), a $15.5B valuation, a Polaris machine at 150 million degrees - lends Envision a marquee deep-tech innovation halo that materially lifts a sustainability-first independent's partner roster and helps it win and keep other backers, while Helion's high-profile ecosystem opens enterprise and policy doors. (3) TALENT & CONTENT: a 'fusion meets electric racing' content platform and a STEM/engineering recruiting halo for both - Helion is hiring physicists and engineers at speed in a fierce market. Envision gains a category-defining clean-energy story, commercial lift and talent pull; Helion gains a global, on-narrative stage as it moves from lab to grid.

Deal Architecture

Official Clean Energy / Innovation Partner tier on Envision Racing, minimum three years, ~$2-4M/yr (calibrated to a pre-revenue deep-tech's modest near-term appetite, structured to scale toward Orion's 2028 delivery), with category exclusivity for energy/fusion and a content-and-talent programme rather than heavy signage.

Note: chairman/investor Sam Altman left the board in March 2026 (OpenAI-conflict recusal), so the thesis should rest on Helion's Microsoft power deal and the Polaris milestone, not on Altman.
