The Case

ProLogium Technology, founded by Vincent Yang, is a 20-year solid-state-battery developer: Mercedes-Benz is a technology partner, investor and board member, and at CES 2026 it unveiled an all-inorganic solid-state lithium-ceramic battery (all-silicon anode). It is going public on Nasdaq as PRLG through a ~$3.8B SPAC merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp. (announced May 2026, closing H2 2026) and has broken ground on a EUR5.2B, 48 GWh gigafactory in Dunkirk, France (with EU grant approval). It has never touched motorsport. The thematic fit with Formula E - the all-electric championship - is elite: solid-state is the headline battery-future story. HONEST framing: FE runs a single spec battery supplied to all teams, so a cell maker cannot put its product in the race car. This is therefore a brand / future-battery halo (MODE B), not an in-car workstream, and it is scored accordingly. Verified decision-maker: Founder & CEO Vincent Yang (confirm the brand/commercial lead before outreach).

Why Now

ProLogium is at a capital and identity inflection - going public as PRLG via a ~$3.8B SPAC merger (closing H2 2026) and building its EUR5.2B Dunkirk gigafactory - which is exactly when a company invests in brand. Formula E is the most on-message global stage for a solid-state-battery brand, so the value of engaging now is to claim that association early; there is no dated FE trigger.

Why Mahindra Racing

Team choice is constrained by ProLogium's most important relationship: Mercedes-Benz is an investor with a board seat. Mercedes exited FE, so there is no home team via that tie - and pointing ProLogium at a direct premium Mercedes rival (Jaguar or Porsche) would create partner-politics friction at board level. Mahindra Racing is the clean answer: an Indian, mass-market EV maker and founding FE team that does NOT compete with Mercedes in its premium segments, builds its own powertrain, and carries a credible EV-innovation identity. No FE team has a battery/cell partner, so the 'battery future' lane is open (off-car, brand-led).

Value to Mahindra Racing  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B with named mechanics (not a bare logo). (1) BRAND / NARRATIVE: 'the solid-state battery of the future, on the championship that proves electric racing' is an ownable, on-message co-marketing story for a sustainability-led team, with a strong content line on next-gen energy density and safety. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: ProLogium's buyers and partners are automakers and cell/module makers (Mercedes-Benz, FEV) - exactly the manufacturers and suppliers in the FE paddock, so team hospitality becomes a working channel, while Mahindra gains a well-capitalised, newly-public battery-tech anchor. (3) TALENT / CREDIBILITY: a battery-innovation halo for the materials and powertrain engineers both sides compete for, and a credible 'future of EV' association. Honest caveat: because FE uses a spec battery, none of this is an on-car deployment - it is brand, content and ecosystem value, which is why it is scored MODE B.

Deal Architecture

Official Battery-Technology / Innovation Partner on Mahindra Racing, minimum three years, right-sized to a value tier (~$2-4M/yr given the capex focus), centred on a brand + content programme around the battery-future story and EV-supply-chain ecosystem access - explicitly not an on-car technical deal.
