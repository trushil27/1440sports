The Case

UiPath is the enterprise-automation leader pivoting hard into agentic AI - software agents that orchestrate and execute work across a company's systems. FY2026 was an inflection: its first profitable full year ($57M operating income, $282M net income), $1.9B ARR up 12%, $1.7B cash and no debt, with AI-product ARR (agentic automation, intelligent document processing, the Maestro orchestration layer) reaching ~$200M. Founder-CEO Daniel Dines is back at the helm framing the agentic-orchestration thesis, and a brand-new CMO - Michael Atalla (ex-Microsoft, ex-F5), appointed August 2025 - was hired to elevate exactly this story. No automation or agentic-AI orchestration brand sits anywhere on the F1 grid, so the category is open, and UiPath is a profitable, cash-rich company with the means to own it.

Why Now

Two clocks align. First, a new CMO (Atalla, Aug 2025) is in the window when marquee brand investments get defined - and he was hired specifically to elevate UiPath's agentic-AI leadership. Second, the agentic-AI category is in a land-grab for enterprise mindshare, and UiPath's first profitable year gives it both the confidence and the balance sheet to invest in brand. The open automation lane on the grid is cheap to plant now, before a peer notices it.

Why McLaren

McLaren is the fit: the grid's deepest enterprise-software partner roster (Dell, Salesforce, Workday, Google) means a proven hospitality machine aimed at exactly the CIO/COO/automation buyers UiPath sells to, and a premium, technology-forward brand that mirrors UiPath's 'orchestration' positioning. The automation category is uncontested there. Aston Martin (boardroom enterprise audience) and Williams (data/engineering) are documented alternatives.

Value to McLaren  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A (developing) - real substance, not signage. (1) TECHNICAL/OPERATIONAL: an F1 team runs on sprawling, repetitive back-office and engineering workflows - parts procurement and logistics across a global calendar, supplier onboarding, finance and compliance ops, and data movement between simulation, telemetry and strategy systems. That is exactly what UiPath's agentic automation and orchestration layer attacks: automating and orchestrating those processes so engineers and operations staff spend time on performance, not paperwork. A defined workstream (automating the team's logistics/finance/data ops with agentic orchestration) is genuine operational value and a flagship reference deployment. (2) COMMERCIAL: McLaren's enterprise-software partner audience - the CIOs, COOs and automation buyers UiPath sells to - is exactly its market, so race-weekend hospitality becomes a working enterprise pipeline channel, with co-marketing across UiPath's large enterprise base. (3) CONTENT & TALENT: an 'automation behind a winning team' content platform and a joint engineering/AI-talent narrative. The team gains real operational efficiency plus aligned co-marketing; UiPath gains a live, referenceable deployment and a premium enterprise stage for its agentic-AI story.

Deal Architecture

Official Automation / Agentic AI Partner tier on McLaren, minimum three years, estimated $4-6M/yr (calibrated to $1.9B ARR and profitability), with category exclusivity for automation/agentic-AI orchestration, a working automation deployment across the team's operations, and B2B hospitality + content aimed at UiPath's enterprise buyers.
