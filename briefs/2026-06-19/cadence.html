The Case

Cadence Design Systems, long the leader in chip design, has become a computational-engineering powerhouse: in Feb 2026 it completed the acquisition of Hexagon's Design & Engineering business (MSC Nastran, Adams), combining structural, acoustics and multibody dynamics with its own multiphysics and CFD portfolio, and it raised FY26 growth guidance to ~17% (an NVIDIA 'Physical AI' partner). It has never entered motorsport. The fit is literal and on-profile (deep-tech, public): F1 performance is won in simulation - aerodynamics (CFD under the cost cap), vehicle dynamics and structural analysis - which is exactly Cadence's post-Hexagon stack. Cadillac, the greenfield 2026 team, must build its entire CAE/simulation capability from zero, so the lane is cleanly open. Verified decision path: SVP Marketing & Business Development Nimish Modi (sponsorship owner) and President & CEO Anirudh Devgan (C-level sponsor), with the System Design & Analysis (multiphysics) organisation as technical counterpart.

Why Now

Cadence just expanded into the full multiphysics/CFD/structural stack and is investing in brand around 'Physical AI', so capital and appetite align now. F1's 2026 rules raise the simulation premium (lighter cars, active aero, electrical-heavy power units), and greenfield Cadillac is choosing its engineering tooling today - get in before the stack is set, not after.
