The Case

Kraken, the AI utility-software platform incubated inside Octopus Energy, has just been spun out as an independent company valued at $8.65B in a $1B raise (led by D1 Capital, with Fidelity, Durable and Ontario Teachers'), and is on track for a public listing by mid-2026. It is a clean B2B story: Kraken licenses its platform to global energy giants (EDF, E.ON, Tokyo Gas, Origin, Severn Trent), serving 70M+ utility accounts with $500M+ in contracted annual revenue, while its Kraken Flex arm runs smart-EV-charging, virtual-power-plant and grid-flexibility tech across 2GW+ of managed assets. It has never touched motorsport. For an all-electric championship, a clean-energy / smart-charging platform is an unusually on-message partner, and the energy/grid lane is open on Jaguar TCS Racing (TCS holds the IT/data/title lane, Chase holds financial services). Led by CEO Amir Orad (ex-Sisense, NICE Actimize), with CFO Tim Wan (ex-Asana) - an enterprise-software team built for an IPO and the brand-building that precedes it.

Why Now

The spin-out and $1B raise just closed and an IPO is targeted for mid-2026, so the capital and the brand mandate are live right now - and post-spin-out, pre-IPO is exactly when a platform invests in enterprise brand. A clean-energy software leader on the world's premier electric racing series is a uniquely on-message story, the category lane is open, and a founder-style enterprise-software CEO (Amir Orad) with an IPO to position will move fast on a differentiated, substance-led pitch.

Why Jaguar TCS Racing

Jaguar TCS Racing is British (matching London-based Kraken), premium, and explicitly sustainability-and-technology-led, with a long-term GEN4 commitment through 2026-2030 - so the relationship can run for the full multi-year horizon. Its energy/grid-software lane is open (TCS owns IT/data and the title, Chase owns finance), letting Kraken own a distinct, on-message 'sustainable energy tech' category rather than competing with the incumbent IT partner.

Value to Jaguar TCS Racing  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B with real, named mechanics across three fronts. (1) ENERGY / SUSTAINABILITY WORKSTREAM: Kraken Flex runs smart-EV-charging, virtual-power-plant and grid-flexibility tech (2GW+ of managed assets, 200k+ connected devices) - a credible basis to optimise and showcase the energy footprint of the team's operations and facilities (charging, battery, on-site renewables) and to co-tell a hard-data sustainability story that Formula E's audience and the FIA's net-zero agenda actually reward - substance, not a green logo. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Kraken sells to the world's energy giants (EDF, E.ON, Tokyo Gas, Origin), and Formula E's paddock and host-city programmes are dense with the utility, government and infrastructure decision-makers it courts - so team hospitality becomes a working enterprise channel, while Jaguar gains a well-capitalised ($8.65B), IPO-track technology anchor that strengthens its commercial roster in a category it currently lacks. (3) BRAND / CONTENT / TALENT: 'the software running the world's energy, powering the team that races on electricity' is an ownable co-marketing narrative, with a content series on energy/AI and a recruiting halo for the data/ML/energy engineers both sides fight for. Jaguar gets a distinctive sustainable-energy-tech partner and a real data story; Kraken gets an on-message global brand stage and a live reference ahead of its IPO.

Deal Architecture

Official Sustainable Energy / Grid-Tech Partner tier on Jaguar TCS Racing, minimum three years (aligned to the GEN4 cycle), ~$3-5M/yr, with category exclusivity in energy/grid software and a defined energy-data + sustainability activation workstream.

Risks & Counters

B2B platform, niche brand  Kraken sells to utilities, not consumers, so the sponsorship logic isn't obvious at first glance. Counter: Formula E's audience and host-city programmes are full of the utility/government/infrastructure buyers Kraken courts, and the all-electric narrative is uniquely on-message - the audience ROI is direct.
Capital focused on growth/IPO  A pre-IPO spin-out watches discretionary spend. Counter: Right-size to a value-to-mid energy-partner tier tied to a real energy-data workstream and measurable enterprise pipeline, not title-level spend.
Perception overlap with TCS  TCS is Jaguar's broad technology partner. Counter: Position Kraken precisely in the energy/grid-software vertical (a lane TCS does not hold), not as a general IT partner - the categories are distinct and the engine reads the lane as open.
