## THE CASE
Kraken, the AI utility-software platform incubated inside Octopus Energy, has just been spun out as an independent company valued at $8.65B in a $1B raise (led by D1 Capital, with Fidelity, Durable and Ontario Teachers'), and is on track for a public listing by mid-2026. It is a clean B2B story: Kraken licenses its platform to global energy giants (EDF, E.ON, Tokyo Gas, Origin, Severn Trent), serving 70M+ utility accounts with $500M+ in contracted annual revenue, while its Kraken Flex arm runs smart-EV-charging, virtual-power-plant and grid-flexibility tech across 2GW+ of managed assets. It has never touched motorsport. For an all-electric championship, a clean-energy / smart-charging platform is an unusually on-message partner, and the energy/grid lane is open on Jaguar TCS Racing (TCS holds the IT/data/title lane, Chase holds financial services).
