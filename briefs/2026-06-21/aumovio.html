The Case

Aumovio SE is the automotive-electronics group Continental spun off as an independent, Frankfurt-listed company on 18 Sep 2025 (~EUR19.6B 2024 sales, targeting EUR24B+) - ADAS, sensors, cameras, cockpit displays/HMI, and software-defined-vehicle systems, including the relaunched VDO brand. It is the born-big spin-off class 1440 prizes (the Versigent archetype, at larger scale), with a brand-new independent identity launched in April 2025 and a clear brand-build motive, and it has never entered motorsport. On-profile (electrical/electronics/automotive deep-tech). The fit is real: cockpit displays/HMI, sensor systems and software-defined-vehicle electronics are exactly the kind of electronics an F1 car and its data systems rely on. Led by CEO Philipp von Hirschheydt, with Dr Jutta Donges as CFO (by Mar 2026).

Why Now

Aumovio is a newly independent company building its brand from a standing start (listing Sep 2025, brand launched Apr 2025), which is precisely when enterprise brand investment pays off - and F1's 2026 electronics-and-software-heavy rules make an automotive-electronics partner on-message. The honest 'why now' is the spin-off inflection, not a forcing F1 event; engage while the new brand is being built.

Why Williams

Williams is the data-and-electronics-led team modernising its engineering and software stack, so a software-defined-vehicle and cockpit-electronics partner is on-narrative, and the automotive-electronics lane is distinct from its data-platform (VAST) and collaboration (Atlassian) partners. A credible stage with a real electronics/data-systems workstream.

Value to Williams  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with real mechanics. (1) TECHNICAL: cockpit displays/HMI, sensor systems and software-defined-vehicle electronics are core to a modern race car and its data pipeline - Aumovio's domain - so a co-engineering workstream on the car's electronics, displays and sensor data is genuine, not signage, and a flagship reference for the spun-off brand. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Aumovio sells to automakers and Tier-1s who populate the F1 paddock, so hospitality is a working channel, while Williams gains a large (~EUR19.6B) automotive-electronics anchor as the brand seeks visibility. (3) BRAND / TALENT: a newly independent automotive-electronics brand on the world's premier motorsport is an ideal brand-build platform, with an electronics/SDV engineering-talent halo. Williams gets real electronics/data-systems value; Aumovio gets a high-visibility stage to launch its independent brand.

Deal Architecture

Official Vehicle-Electronics / Software-Defined-Vehicle Partner on Williams, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr (right-sized to an early-stage spin-off), with a co-engineering electronics/cockpit-data workstream plus brand-build activation.

Risks & Counters

Spin-off cost discipline  A newly independent company often runs lean early. Counter: Right-size to a value-to-mid tier framed around the brand-build need (a new independent identity) plus a real electronics workstream.
Tier-1 supplier vs sponsor  Aumovio is an automotive supplier; would it sponsor rather than supply? Counter: Precedent on the grid (Tenneco, Marelli are technical partners), and a fresh spin-off has an unusual brand-visibility motive; position as technical-partner-plus-brand.
Electronics partly present  Some teams have Tier-1 electronics/PU partners. Counter: Position precisely on cockpit/HMI + software-defined-vehicle electronics, a distinct lane, on Williams where it's open.
