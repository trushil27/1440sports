The Case

Cerebras Systems, led by co-founder & CEO Andrew Feldman, builds wafer-scale AI processors (the WSE-3 is the largest chip ever made, ~4 trillion transistors) and the systems around them, and IPO'd on Nasdaq (CBRS) in a blockbuster May 2026 - ~$67B market cap, $510M 2025 revenue at a 47% net margin (profitable), with multi-billion-dollar compute agreements with OpenAI and AWS. It has never touched motorsport. The F1 fit is real: aerodynamics is the biggest lap-time lever and it is compute-bound, so wafer-scale compute lets a team extract more CFD fidelity and faster iteration - and run power-unit, strategy and ML simulation - within F1's regulated CFD/wind-tunnel allocation (the ATR). HONEST framing: the ATR caps aero-CFD volume (so this maximises value within the cap rather than removing it), CAE software incumbents (Ansys/Cadence/Siemens) already occupy the toolchain (Cerebras is the compute underneath, a distinct sub-lane), and Cerebras carries real customer concentration (G42/MBZUAI was ~62% of 2025 revenue) - all noted, and the score reflects them. Decision-maker: Co-Founder & CEO Andrew Feldman.

Why Now

Cerebras is at a live capital + identity peak (blockbuster May-2026 IPO, ~$67B cap) - exactly when a company invests in brand and lands marquee references - and F1's 2026 rules raise the simulation burden, so a founding compute relationship with a greenfield team is best built now. The honest why-now is catalyst-driven, not a dated race trigger.

Why Cadillac

Cadillac is the new American works team building its entire simulation and compute stack from zero, so an AI-supercompute partner can be the baseline it designs around (a genuine prime lane), and an American AI-compute champion alongside the American flag-bearer is a clean brand alignment - with GM behind it as a deep-pocketed, deal-capable counterpart. No AI-compute incumbent to conflict with (IFS runs ERP, TWG AI is data/analytics services, not wafer-scale HPC). It is a signal, not a placement - Cadillac's greenfield build is simply the best fit for a foundational compute partner.

Value to Cadillac  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

A TWO-WAY exchange (tech + business both directions), not a logo fee - three concrete swaps. (1) COMPUTE <-> ELITE CFD BENCHMARK: Cerebras gives the team wafer-scale AI-compute to maximise CFD fidelity and iteration within the ATR cap (plus power-unit, strategy and ML simulation); in return F1 aerodynamics - one of the hardest real-world CFD/HPC problems - becomes Cerebras's credibility-defining benchmark and showcase to industrial and scientific buyers. (2) CO-ENGINEERED WORKLOADS <-> PRODUCT VALIDATION: jointly optimising the team's CFD/simulation workloads on wafer-scale hardware advances Cerebras's real-world HPC performance and reference architecture; the team gets faster, higher-fidelity aero development that converts directly to lap time. (3) TALENT + BRAND <-> ENTERPRISE REACH: an American compute champion on the American works team is a recruiting and brand halo for the HPC/ML engineers both sides compete for; the paddock's industrial/OEM ecosystem opens the enterprise HPC buyers Cerebras is scaling into. Cadillac gets a foundational simulation-compute edge from day one; Cerebras gets the hardest benchmark, a global stage and buyers.

Deal Architecture

Official AI Compute / Simulation Partner on Cadillac, minimum three years, structured as a compute-supply + co-engineering workstream (CFD/PU/strategy simulation on wafer-scale systems, joint HPC optimisation, a shared benchmark/reference programme) - value exchanged both ways, not a pure logo fee. Cerebras is highly deal-capable, so size to a genuine technical partnership.

Risks & Counters

F1's CFD cap (ATR)  F1 regulates CFD/wind-tunnel volume per team (Aerodynamic Testing Restriction), so compute can't simply be scaled up without limit. Counter: Wafer-scale value is higher fidelity and faster iteration PER allocated unit, plus uncapped power-unit/strategy/ML simulation - maximising the regulated budget, not evading it.
CAE software incumbents  Ansys, Cadence and Siemens already sit in the CFD toolchain. Counter: Cerebras is the compute layer underneath that software - a distinct hardware sub-lane; position as accelerating the existing toolchain, not replacing it.
Customer concentration / independence  G42/MBZUAI was ~62% of 2025 revenue and G42 is Cerebras's largest backer (a concentration and geopolitical-scrutiny risk). Counter: Revenue is diversifying fast (OpenAI, AWS multi-billion deals) and the company is profitable at ~$67B - deal-capability is not in question; note the concentration honestly.
