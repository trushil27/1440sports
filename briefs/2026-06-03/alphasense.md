## THE CASE
AlphaSense is the AI platform for market intelligence and search across the financial and business world - the tool banks, asset managers, hedge funds and a majority of the S&P 500 use to find and trust insight. On 3 June 2026 it closed a $350M round at a ~$7.5B valuation (led by Vitruvian Partners, Accenture Ventures and J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners), nearly doubling its 2024 mark, on more than $600M of ARR. It has scale and momentum - the $930M Tegus acquisition in July 2024 folded in the leading expert-transcript library - and CEO Jack Kokko has said an IPO is a possibility. Two things make it a 1440 target: it has never touched motorsport, and no market- or financial-intelligence brand owns a position anywhere on the F1 grid, so the category is uncontested. Its audience - research, strategy and investment professionals - is exactly the premium enterprise crowd an F1 partnership reaches.
