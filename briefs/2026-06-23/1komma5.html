The Case

1KOMMA5°, the German home-electrification leader, has scaled to ~EUR520M revenue (debt-free) at a ~$2.4B valuation, bundling solar, storage, heat pumps and EV charging under its Heartbeat AI virtual power plant, and is on an IPO track (Nasdaq intended, timing pushed amid policy headwinds). Founded and led by Philipp Schroder (ex-Tesla Germany, ex-Sonnen), it has never entered motorsport. On-profile (electrification/energy), it offers two genuine angles for Formula E: a real-ish energy workstream (Heartbeat VPP + renewable power + EV charging to run and optimise the team's facilities and operations) and a rare audience match - FE's EV-positive, urban, European fans are exactly 1KOMMA5's customers. HONEST framing: capacity is mid-cap and the workstream is operational/brand rather than in-car, so this is a value-tier WARM. Decision-maker: CEO & co-founder Philipp Schroder.

Why Now

1KOMMA5° is in a fast organic-growth and pre-IPO brand-building phase, and Formula E reaches its target European EV-owner audience in the very cities it operates - so the audience ROI is direct. The honest 'why now' is positional (brand-build + audience), not a dated trigger; engage while crowding is low and the IPO narrative is being built.

Why Mahindra Racing

Mahindra Racing is a sustainability-and-EV-led FE team (backed by an EV-and-renewables conglomerate) with an open clean-energy/electrification lane, so a home-electrification + VPP partner is on-brand. The value centres on verified renewable energy/VPP for the team plus the European EV-owner audience, not car technology.

Value to Mahindra Racing  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A leaning B - concrete mechanics. (1) ENERGY / VPP: 1KOMMA5's Heartbeat VPP, solar+storage and EV charging can supply and optimise verified renewable power for the team's facilities and operations (and charge its road/ops fleet), with a hard-data sustainability story FE's net-zero agenda rewards - operationally real, if off-car. (2) AUDIENCE / B2C: this is the rare case where the team's fans ARE the sponsor's customers - FE's EV-positive European audience is 1KOMMA5's market - so activations (home-electrification fan offers, host-city campaigns) are a genuine acquisition channel, not decoration. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'the team that races on electricity, powered by the company electrifying European homes' is an ownable co-marketing story, plus an energy/software talent halo. Mahindra gets verified renewable energy + a relevant consumer-brand partner; 1KOMMA5 gets an on-message stage in front of its customers.

Deal Architecture

Value-tier Official Clean-Energy / Electrification Partner on Mahindra Racing, minimum three years, ~$2-4M/yr, centred on a Heartbeat VPP + renewable-power workstream for the team plus a European EV-owner fan-acquisition and content programme.
