The Case

Sila Nanotechnologies, the next-gen battery-materials leader (~$3.3B; $1.31B raised, incl. a $375M Series G, from Sutter Hill, Bessemer, Coatue), makes Titan Silicon - a silicon anode that raises lithium-ion energy density - and in Sep 2025 opened the first US auto-scale silicon-anode plant (Moses Lake, WA), now shipping to Mercedes-Benz, BMW and Panasonic. Its founder-CEO, Gene Berdichevsky, was Tesla employee #7 and led the original Roadster battery - a marquee, motorsport-adjacent story. It has never entered motorsport. Battery energy density is the single defining constraint of electric racing, so a silicon-anode leader is unusually on-message for Formula E. HONEST framing: FE's race battery is a single spec/series supplier, so Sila's anode is a series-level input rather than a team-deployable workstream - the team value is brand, a battery-innovation narrative and a talent halo (MODE B), scored WARM accordingly. Decision-maker: CEO & co-founder Gene Berdichevsky.

Why Now

Sila just brought the first auto-scale silicon-anode plant online and is ramping series production with marquee automakers, so its profile and brand are rising - and electric racing is the most on-message stage for a battery-energy-density story. The honest 'why now' is the production milestone plus a pre-IPO brand window, not a forcing FE event.

Why Nissan Formula E

Nissan's Formula E team is a works EV pioneer with an open battery-innovation/clean-energy lane (its partners are powertrain, electronics and ESG, not battery materials), so a silicon-anode energy-density story is on-brand. The value is brand + battery-innovation narrative, not a car-technology deployment.

Value to Nissan Formula E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - concrete mechanics, honestly bounded. (1) BRAND / NARRATIVE: 'the battery breakthrough that makes electric go further' on the electric racing series is an ownable, on-message co-marketing story, amplified by a Tesla-Roadster-battery founder - genuinely distinctive, not a logo. (2) CONTENT / TECH: a co-developed content series on battery energy density and the future of EVs (the exact thing FE exists to advance) is credible fan- and sponsor-facing material. (3) TALENT / ECOSYSTEM: a battery/materials-engineering recruiting halo and access to Sila's automaker network (Mercedes/BMW). Nissan gets a marquee battery-innovation brand partner and standout content; Sila gets a global stage for its energy-density story. (Scored honestly: the race battery is spec/series-supplied, so this is brand/content/talent value, not an in-car deployment.)

Deal Architecture

Brand / Innovation Partner on Nissan Formula E, minimum three years, value-tier (~$2-4M/yr), centred on a battery-energy-density co-marketing and content programme plus a talent/ecosystem tie-in - explicitly a brand partnership, not a technical supply deal.

Risks & Counters
