The Case

Nebius Group (NASDAQ: NBIS), the AI-native cloud built by Yandex founder Arkady Volozh, is one of the breakout AI-infrastructure platforms: Q1'26 revenue grew 684% YoY to $399M, 2026 guidance is $3-3.4B at ~40% EBITDA margin, it holds a ~$50B contracted backlog, and NVIDIA has taken a $2B strategic equity stake. It has never entered motorsport. The fit is real and on-profile (AI compute/data-center): under F1's cost cap, performance is increasingly decided by CFD, simulation and AI - compute efficiency converts to lap time - so a GPU-cloud partner powers the team's most decisive workstream. CoreWeave's Aston Martin deal proves AI-clouds sponsor F1; the open whitespace is at a peer team. Haas - the leanest team, which outsources design and gains the most from democratised high-end compute - is the ownable first step. Decision-maker: CEO Arkady Volozh.

Why Now

Nebius is at the crest of the AI-infrastructure supercycle (NVIDIA's $2B stake, hyper-growth, a $50B backlog), so brand investment and visibility are peaking, and F1's 2026 compute-driven era makes a GPU-cloud partner unusually on-message. CoreWeave just validated the category in F1 - move before another neocloud claims the remaining peer-team whitespace.

Why Haas

Haas is the grid's leanest team and outsources much of its design (Dallara), so cloud-based CFD/AI compute is genuinely transformative rather than incremental - and its compute/cloud lane is open. An American team plus a fast-scaling global AI-cloud is a clean story, with a clear scale-up path if it performs.

Value to Haas  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with real mechanics. (1) TECHNICAL (the core): F1 performance under the cost cap is won in CFD, vehicle-dynamics simulation and AI, and Nebius's GPU/AI cloud powers exactly those workloads - more simulation throughput per cost-cap dollar, faster design iteration, and AI/ML tooling - a genuine, deployable competitive workstream, especially for a lean team like Haas that can leapfrog on elastic compute. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Nebius sells to the enterprises and AI builders who populate the F1 paddock and its NVIDIA-anchored ecosystem, so hospitality becomes a working channel, while Haas gains a fast-scaling, deep-pocketed technology anchor. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'the compute that designs the car' is an ownable narrative on a data-driven sport, with an ML/HPC engineering-talent halo. Haas gets transformative compute and a blue-chip anchor; Nebius gets a live, visible reference and a premium B2B stage at its peak.

Deal Architecture

Official AI-Cloud / Compute Partner on Haas, minimum three years, ~$4-6M/yr, with category exclusivity in AI cloud/GPU compute and a real deployment powering the team's CFD, simulation and AI workloads.
