The Case

Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS), the MIT spinout leading the race to commercial fusion, has raised ~$3B (an $863M Series B2 in Aug 2025 from NVIDIA's NVentures, Google, Breakthrough Energy, Temasek, Eni and others). Its SPARC machine (Devens, MA) targets fusion net energy gain in Q1 2027 - a landmark scientific milestone - and Google has signed a 200MW PPA for CFS's first commercial ARC plant in Virginia. It has never entered motorsport. The fit with Formula E is a marquee brand story: 'limitless clean energy' on the world's premier electric racing series is the ultimate sustainability-and-innovation narrative. HONEST framing: fusion is not a team-deployable workstream, so this is a brand / future-energy / talent-halo play (MODE B), scored WARM accordingly - the value is co-marketing around the clean-energy breakthrough, a science-content series and a recruiting halo, not an in-car deployment. Decision-maker: CEO & co-founder Bob Mumgaard.

Why Now

CFS is approaching the sport's favourite kind of moment - a genuine breakthrough (SPARC net energy gain targeted Q1 2027) - and with the Google PPA and a fresh $863M round, its public profile is rising fast. The honest 'why now' is narrative-led: align with the clean-energy breakthrough story as it crests, not a forcing F1/FE event - and engage early while inbound interest is low (~25).

Why Andretti

Andretti is a charter, sustainability-leading, American FE team - a natural cultural home for an American fusion pioneer's clean-energy story - and the fusion/clean-power lane is open. The partnership is brand-and-content led (the breakthrough narrative), not a car-technology workstream.

Value to Andretti  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - honest mechanics, named (not a bare logo). (1) BRAND / NARRATIVE: 'the team that races on electricity, backed by the company building limitless clean energy' is a uniquely powerful, ownable co-marketing story on the first net-zero-certified series - peaking around the SPARC net-energy-gain milestone. (2) CONTENT / SCIENCE: a co-developed content series on the clean-energy future (fusion + electric racing) is genuinely distinctive fan and sponsor-facing material, not filler. (3) TALENT / ECOSYSTEM: a recruiting and innovation halo for the physics/engineering talent both fusion and motorsport compete for, plus access to CFS's marquee backer network (NVIDIA, Google). Andretti gets a marquee innovation-brand partner and standout content; CFS gets a global stage for its clean-energy story. (Scored honestly: this is brand/content/talent value - there is no in-car or operational deployment.)

Deal Architecture

Brand / Innovation Partner on Andretti Formula E, minimum three years, value-tier (~$2-4M/yr), centred on clean-energy co-marketing, a science-content series timed to the SPARC milestone, and a talent/ecosystem programme - explicitly a brand partnership, not a technical deployment.

Risks & Counters

Halo, not a workstream  Fusion is not deployable to a race team; the value is brand/content/talent only. Counter: Scored and pitched honestly as a brand partnership timed to the SPARC breakthrough - name the concrete content/talent mechanics, don't dress it as a technical deal.
R&D-stage spend discipline  CFS is pouring capital into building SPARC/ARC, not marketing. Counter: Right-size to a value tier with a clear narrative-moment rationale (the net-energy-gain milestone); treat as a relationship to build, not a marquee spend.
Sponsorship appetite unproven  A fusion lab has never done anything like this. Counter: Lead with the once-in-history breakthrough story and a low-commitment, content-led structure; let the narrative carry it.
