The Case

Watershed is the leading enterprise carbon-management platform - measuring, reporting and reducing emissions for customers like Airbnb, FedEx, General Mills and Walmart, tracking a gigaton of emissions - valued at $1.8B after a $100M Series C led by Greenoaks (with Sequoia and Kleiner Perkins; $185M raised), and named a category leader by Verdantix in 2026. Founded in 2019 by ex-Stripe/Lyft/Google operators (co-founder Taylor Francis is the external-facing leader), it has never touched motorsport. The fit is unusually literal: Formula E is the first motorsport certified to a net-zero pathway, runs verified Scope 1/2/3 carbon accounting, and its teams face tightening FIA requirements and EU CSRD reporting - so carbon management is a real team workstream, not a halo. One honest caveat: because sustainability IS Formula E's identity, the category is contested - Envision's net-zero claims run on its own technology plus Earthly, and Nissan has Coral (ESG-AI) - so the clean opening is team-level at Mahindra Racing (an EV-sustainability conglomerate whose carbon-software lane is open: Umicore covers battery materials, Renesas chips, Tech Mahindra broad IT).

Why Now

Tightening FIA net-zero requirements and EU CSRD reporting are forcing teams to put auditable numbers behind their sustainability claims, which is exactly Watershed's product - so the team-workstream value is live now. Watershed is scaling post-Series C and investing in brand, and inbound motorsport interest is low (~30), so the value of moving now is positional: claim the carbon-software lane on a sustainability-credible team before the category - already filling across the grid - closes.

Why Mahindra Racing

Mahindra Racing is a founding FE team backed by an Indian EV-and-renewables conglomerate with real net-zero commitments, so a verified-carbon partner is on-brand - and its carbon-software lane is open (Umicore covers battery materials, Renesas chips, Tech Mahindra general IT), unlike Envision (own net-zero tech + Earthly) or Nissan (Coral). It lets Watershed own a distinct 'verified net-zero' position and gives Mahindra hard data behind its sustainability story.

Value to Mahindra Racing  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with concrete, named mechanics. (1) CARBON WORKSTREAM (real and deployable): Watershed measures, reports and helps cut Mahindra's full Scope 1/2/3 footprint - freight and intercontinental travel, factory and race-operations energy, car production and supply chain - turning the team's sustainability claims into auditable, FIA- and CSRD-grade numbers, identifying reduction levers (logistics routing, energy sourcing, supplier engagement) and producing the verified disclosures regulators and sponsors increasingly demand. That is substance a sustainability-led team can actually use, not a green logo. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Watershed sells to the chief-sustainability-officers and CFOs of global enterprises (Airbnb, FedEx, Walmart), and the Formula E paddock and its corporate-sustainability programmes are dense with exactly those buyers - so team hospitality becomes a working enterprise pipeline, while Mahindra gains a category-leading credibility partner. (3) BRAND / CONTENT / TALENT: 'don't just claim it, prove it' - a co-developed verified-net-zero content story (the hardest, most credible sustainability narrative in a sport built on it) plus a recruiting halo for the climate/data talent both sides want. Mahindra gets audited carbon leadership and a real data workstream; Watershed gets an on-message global stage and a flagship live deployment.

Deal Architecture

Value-tier Official Carbon-Management / Net-Zero Partner on Mahindra Racing, minimum three years, ~$1.5-2.5M/yr, with category exclusivity in carbon/ESG software and a defined Scope 1/2/3 measurement-and-reduction workstream plus verified-disclosure content.

Risks & Counters

Carbon is FE's most contested category  Sustainability is the series' identity, so several teams already have carbon/sustainability partners (Envision, Nissan). Counter: Claim a team-level position at Mahindra, where the carbon-software lane is genuinely open - not grid-wide exclusivity; lead with the verified-data workstream as the wedge.
Mid-cap, value-tier capacity  At $1.8B, Watershed funds a value-tier deal, not a marquee spend. Counter: Right-size to a value tier tied to a real measurement-and-reduction workstream and measurable enterprise pipeline - marketing with a return.
Unproven sponsorship appetite  An enterprise-SaaS company has never done a motorsport deal. Counter: Anchor on the literal product-to-need fit (FIA/CSRD reporting) and a low-commitment, measurable structure; let the workstream, not a hard sell, carry it.
