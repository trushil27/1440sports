The Case

Vertiv (NYSE: VRT) is the critical-digital-infrastructure leader - power continuity, precision cooling/thermal management and edge systems - and the clearest pick-and-shovel winner of the AI-data-centre buildout: Q1 2026 net sales of $2.65B (up 30%), a backlog of $15.0B (up 109% year-on-year), a stock up 102% year-to-date, an NVIDIA partner, and a just-completed acquisition of thermal specialist ThermoKey (12 Jun 2026) led by CEO Giordano Albertazzi. It has never touched motorsport. The fit matches the recalibrated 1440 profile precisely - a public, electrification/power-backbone company (the Versigent/nVent thread) with a genuine technical workstream, not a halo: a modern F1 team runs on compute (CFD, simulation, factory and trackside data), and Vertiv builds exactly the power and cooling that compute depends on. The why-now is sharp: F1's 2026 power units are ~50% electric and the sport's data/compute arms race is intensifying, while Cadillac - the brand-new 2026 entrant - is building its entire digital backbone from zero, with an open critical-infrastructure lane (IFS runs its ERP, TWG its AI, Tenneco its powertrain - none is power/cooling).

Why Now

Vertiv is at the crest of the AI-power supercycle - record sales, a $15.0B backlog and a stock up 102% YTD - so the capital and the appetite to build enterprise brand are peaking together, exactly the inflection moment 1440 targets. F1's 2026 electrification regs and the sport's escalating compute/data demands make a power-and-cooling partner unusually on-message right now, and Cadillac's greenfield build means the infrastructure decisions are being made today - get in before the backbone is architected, not after.

Why Cadillac F1 Team

Cadillac is the 2026 grid's only greenfield team - building its factory, data and trackside infrastructure from scratch - so Vertiv can architect the power-and-cooling backbone from day one rather than retrofit, a uniquely concrete workstream. The critical-infrastructure lane is open (IFS = ERP, TWG = AI, Tenneco = powertrain), and an American critical-infrastructure champion on the American flag-bearer team is a clean brand story for Vertiv's US-heavy investor and customer base.

Value to Cadillac F1 Team  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with real, named mechanics. (1) TECHNICAL / OPS (the core): Vertiv designs and operates the power continuity (UPS/switchgear), precision cooling and thermal management, and edge/data-centre infrastructure that a modern F1 team's compute runs on - the factory CFD/simulation clusters and data centre, plus the trackside 'garage' edge where race-weekend power conditioning and cooling for compute and comms simply cannot drop mid-session. For Cadillac, a brand-new team, Vertiv can architect that entire digital backbone from day one (factory + trackside + edge) as a flagship 'the power and cooling behind the newest team on the grid' reference, with its fresh ThermoKey thermal expertise mapping onto the team's own cooling-engineering culture. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Vertiv sells to the hyperscalers, enterprises and data-centre operators building AI infrastructure, and the F1 paddock is dense with exactly those CIO/CTO/infrastructure buyers and the AI/cloud ecosystem (NVIDIA among them) - so team hospitality becomes a working enterprise channel, while Cadillac gains a marquee, deep-pocketed technology anchor at the company's peak-visibility moment. (3) BRAND / CONTENT / TALENT: 'the power and cooling behind the data that wins races' ties Vertiv to the hottest tech story of 2026 (AI power) on a data-driven sport, with a co-marketing content series and a power/thermal/electrical engineering-talent halo both sides compete for. Cadillac gets its digital backbone built and a blue-chip anchor partner; Vertiv gets a live, visible reference deployment and a premium B2B stage at its peak.

Counter: F1 is now full of exactly this play (Oracle, AWS, Dell, HP, Cisco) for B2B reach, the paddock IS Vertiv's buyer base, and the real power/cooling workstream carries the logic beyond a logo.
Large-cap, many priorities  A company at peak demand may not prioritise a single-team sponsorship. Counter: Lead with the flagship greenfield deployment (build the newest team's backbone) as a marquee, visible proof point at the company's peak-attention moment - a reference, not just spend.
