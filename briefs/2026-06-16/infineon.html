The Case

Infineon is the world's #1 automotive/power-semiconductor company (~$17.2B revenue, ~$120B market cap) and a silicon-carbide (SiC) leader - the power electronics at the heart of every EV traction inverter, where it just set new benchmarks (a 1300V module to 205C). It just raised FY26 guidance on the AI boom and improving automotive orders, and has never entered motorsport. The fit is literal and on-profile (electrification/deep-tech, public co's in scope): FE powertrains are manufacturer-developed, and the traction inverter - which turns battery DC into the AC that drives the motor, the biggest lever on efficiency, thermal and regen - runs on exactly Infineon's SiC. Jaguar TCS Racing is a works manufacturer with an open power-semiconductor lane (TCS = IT, Schaeffler = mechanical, Chase = finance). SiC has a prior FE association (onsemi powered the now-departed Mercedes-EQ team), so not pristine, but Infineon is the uncommitted global leader. Verified decision path: CMO Andreas Urschitz, CEO Jochen Hanebeck, Automotive President Peter Schiefer.

Why Now

Infineon just raised FY26 guidance on the AI-power and automotive up-cycle and is investing in brand around SiC leadership, so the capital and the appetite to tell its electrification story are aligned now. Electric racing is the sharpest possible proof point for SiC - the 2026 motorsport-wide electrification push (F1 ~50% electric, FE Gen4) makes the narrative timely - and the power-semiconductor lane is open on a works team (Jaguar), so a co-engineering-plus-brand partnership can be claimed before a rival semiconductor maker re-enters the space onsemi vacated.

Why Jaguar TCS Racing

Jaguar TCS Racing is a works manufacturer that develops its own powertrain, so Infineon's SiC can genuinely sit in the inverter - a real co-engineering workstream, not a logo - and its power-semiconductor lane is open (TCS = IT, Schaeffler = mechanical, Chase = finance). It is a premium engineering brand whose efficiency-and-thermal performance story aligns with SiC's value, and a long-horizon works programme supports a multi-year deal. (Per signals-not-placements, another 1440 signal aimed at Jaguar in a different category does not occupy the power-semi lane.)

Value to Jaguar TCS Racing  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with real, named mechanics. (1) TECHNICAL (the core): the traction inverter is the biggest single lever on an FE car's efficiency, thermal behaviour and energy recovery, and it runs on power semiconductors - so a partnership puts Infineon's SiC (its new 1300V/205C class) into Jaguar's inverter development: a genuine co-engineering workstream for efficiency/thermal/regen gains that translate into lap time and energy management, plus access to the world SiC leader's applications engineering. 'The silicon that powers the win' - a flagship reference, not signage. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Infineon sells to every automaker and Tier-1, and the FE paddock is dense with exactly those OEM/powertrain decision-makers - so hospitality becomes a working enterprise channel, while Jaguar gains a category-leading, deep-pocketed (~$120B) technology anchor in a lane it lacks. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'decarbonisation runs on semiconductors' is an ownable co-marketing narrative on the electric racing series, with an SiC/electrification content series and a power-electronics talent halo. Jaguar gets real inverter-development value; Infineon gets a live SiC reference and a premium B2B stage.

Deal Architecture

Official Power-Semiconductor / Traction-Inverter Partner on Jaguar TCS Racing, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr, with category exclusivity in power semiconductors and a co-engineering SiC inverter + content workstream.

Risks & Counters

Prior SiC association in FE  onsemi powered the Mercedes-EQ FE team's inverter, so the SiC-in-FE story has been told before. Counter: That team has left the grid and the lane is open; Infineon is the global SiC leader and uncommitted - this is a chance to own the category on a current works team.
Would a semiconductor giant sponsor a team?  Infineon is a B2B chipmaker, not a consumer brand. Counter: Infineon has motorsport-brand history (the former Infineon Raceway) and FE is perfectly on-message for 'decarbonisation via semiconductors'; the real co-engineering workstream and OEM-buyer audience carry the logic.
Incumbent inverter supply  Jaguar already sources its current inverter semiconductors from somewhere. Counter: Frame this as the named, co-engineering power-semiconductor partnership opportunity (and brand lane), not a claim that Infineon is already in the car - a forward workstream to build.
