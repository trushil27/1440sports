The Case

SambaNova Systems, the Palo Alto AI-chip company, completed the first close of a $1B Series F at an $11B valuation (8 Jul 2026), led by General Atlantic with T. Rowe Price, Capital Group, Intel Capital, Vista Equity, BlackRock and QIA; JPMorgan named SambaNova an on-premise inference-infrastructure partner the same day, and CEO Rodrigo Liang is publicly weighing a 2027 IPO. IMPORTANT (desk honesty): this prospect came from an external 1440 intelligence brief that scored it 87 (HOT TOP TIER) and recommended Cadillac - but that duplicates our Cerebras signal (07-03), which already holds the AI-compute recommendation at Cadillac; Cerebras and SambaNova are direct competitors for one slot, and Cerebras (public, ~$67B, profitable) is the stronger of the two. So this is re-scored honestly to 77 and re-pointed to Haas, on a genuinely distinct angle: SambaNova's edge is sovereign, on-premise, low-latency INFERENCE - which maps to a team's real-time pit-wall race-strategy computation and secure trackside telemetry, distinct from Cerebras's factory-CFD training compute. HONEST framing: it is compute infrastructure (off-car), F1's AI lane is crowded, and the round is a first close - scored accordingly. Verified decision-maker: CEO & Co-Founder Rodrigo Liang.

Why Haas

Haas is the American privateer that runs lean and lives on smart real-time strategy, so on-prem, low-latency inference (fast, secure race-strategy and telemetry AI) is a genuine fit, and the American-sovereign-AI story lands on a US team - without duplicating our Cerebras-at-Cadillac AI-compute recommendation. Haas's AI-compute lane is open (Mphasis is a services integrator, not a chip/compute-infrastructure product). Cadillac was the external brief's pick but is taken in our book by Cerebras (same category); re-pointing avoids double-pitching one team two rival AI-chip firms.

Value to Haas  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

A TWO-WAY exchange (tech + business both directions), not a logo fee - three concrete swaps. (1) ON-PREM INFERENCE <-> REAL-TIME PROVING GROUND: SambaNova powers the team's low-latency pit-wall race-strategy inference and secure on-prem telemetry AI (the sovereign JPMorgan use case, transferred to the garage); the team gives SambaNova the ultimate real-time, high-stakes inference showcase. (2) CO-ENGINEERED INFERENCE WORKLOADS <-> PRODUCT VALIDATION: jointly tuning race-strategy and telemetry inference on SambaNova's stack advances its edge/on-prem reference; the team gets a faster, sharper real-time decision edge for a lean budget. (3) US IPO STAGE + BUYERS <-> ENGINEERING CREDIBILITY: SambaNova (pre-IPO, American-sovereign) gets the US-race institutional-investor visibility (Austin/Vegas) it wants ahead of 2027, and the paddock's enterprise ecosystem is its buyer base; the team gets a well-funded frontier-AI partner and an innovation story. Haas gets a real-time inference edge + a funded partner; SambaNova gets a showcase, buyers and an IPO stage.

Deal Architecture

Official AI Inference / Compute Partner on Haas, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr with an IPO-trigger scale-up clause, structured as an on-prem inference workstream (real-time race-strategy + secure telemetry) plus US-race brand activation - value exchanged both ways, not a pure livery fee.
