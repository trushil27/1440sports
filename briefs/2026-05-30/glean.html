The Case

Glean is one of enterprise AI's fastest risers: a 'Work AI' platform that raised a $150M Series F at a ~$7.2B valuation (led by Wellington Management) and has reached ~$300M ARR, whose users executed more than 250 million agentic actions by January 2026, and which acquired agentic-document company Aryn in March 2026. It is on a clear IPO trajectory, has never entered motorsport, and sits in the same open enterprise-AI category as Cohere - meaning Formula E currently has room for a Work-AI brand to own a lane outright. Glean's product story (AI that makes large organisations radically more productive) is a natural fit for the operational, engineering-led narrative of an FE team, and its growth-stage brand would benefit from the international, enterprise-credible stage FE provides. Founder-CEO Arvind Jain - a Google Distinguished Engineer and a co-founder of Rubrik - gives the brand unusual technical credibility.

Why Now

Glean is scaling brand and go-to-market hard ahead of a likely listing, and the enterprise-AI category in FE is uncontested. Moving now secures category exclusivity cheaply and gives Glean a differentiated stage before larger AI names crowd in - the same first-mover logic that makes the AI lane valuable applies in reverse the moment a peer signs.

Why Mahindra Racing

Mahindra Racing is a strong fit: a founding Formula E manufacturer committed through the Gen4 era to 2030, with an Indian-and-global identity and a large enterprise parent ecosystem (the Mahindra Group) that mirrors the kind of complex, multi-business organisation Glean sells into. The long manufacturer commitment also de-risks a multi-year deal, and Mahindra's audience and parent-group relationships open enterprise doors for Glean.

Value to Mahindra Racing  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A. A racing team and its parent run on dispersed institutional knowledge - engineering documentation, supplier data, sponsor and operations information - which is exactly what Glean's Work-AI platform indexes and makes instantly usable. A deployment for the team and selected parent-group functions gives Mahindra real productivity value and Glean a flagship, referenceable enterprise deployment, plus a co-marketing story across a global enterprise group.

Deal Architecture

Official Work AI Partner tier on Mahindra Racing, minimum three years (aligned to the manufacturer's Gen4 commitment), ~$3-4M/yr, with AI-category exclusivity and a working deployment for the team and selected Mahindra Group functions.
