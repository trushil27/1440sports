## THE CASE
Redwood Materials, founded by Tesla co-founder and former CTO JB Straubel, is North America's battery-circularity leader - recycling end-of-life batteries and producing sustainable cathode and anode materials for automakers and cell makers. It expanded its Series E to $425M at a $6B+ valuation (Jan 2026, as Google/Alphabet joined existing backers including Nvidia's NVentures, Capricorn and Goldman Sachs), on top of ~$2.3B in equity raised and a $2B Department of Energy loan commitment, and it has added a fast-growing, profitable second-life arm (Redwood Energy) that redeploys retired EV batteries as grid storage for AI data centres. It has never touched motorsport. For an all-electric championship whose Gen4 era (2026-2030) is built on circular design and recyclable materials, a battery-circularity leader is an unusually on-message partner. Battery-materials is crowded grid-wide (Umicore partners Mahindra; Envision's parent makes cells), but the lane is open on Andretti - its materials partner NAGASE works in carbon-fibre/composite recycling, a distinct stream - and Andretti is the American, sustainability-leading, charter FE team that fits an American circularity champion. Led by founder-CEO Straubel, the right and only real entry point.

## WHY NOW
