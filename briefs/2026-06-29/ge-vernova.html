The Case

GE Vernova, the electrification/power/grid pure-play that GE spun out as an independent company in 2024, is now ~$290B in market cap with FY26 revenue guidance raised to ~$45B and a $163B backlog, riding the energy-transition and AI-data-centre power supercycle (Q1'26 revenue $9.3B, +16%; backlog up ~$13B quarter-on-quarter; guidance raised). It has never entered motorsport. It is the Versigent born-big-spin-off archetype at marquee scale, and on-profile (electrification, public). For Formula E, 'the company electrifying the world' on the world's electric racing series is a near-perfect thematic match. Honest framing: GE Vernova doesn't build race-car technology, so the team-level workstream is clean and temporary event power / microgrids for FE's city races plus a hard sustainability story - real, but more brand than in-car - and it is scored accordingly. Verified decision path: Chief Commercial & Operations Officer Pablo Koziner (the partnership owner) and CEO Scott Strazik (C-level sponsor).

Why Now

GE Vernova is at the crest of the power/electrification cycle - Q1'26 revenue +16% and FY26 guidance raised to ~$45B on the AI-data-centre power supercycle - so brand investment is live, and Formula E (the first net-zero-certified series) is the most on-message stage for an electrification leader during the energy transition.

Why Porsche

Porsche is the reigning FE champion and the grid's premium electrification-performance brand, with an open electrification/clean-power lane - so 'the company electrifying the world with the team that wins on electricity' is a clean, on-message pairing. The workstream centres on clean event/paddock power and the sustainability narrative rather than car technology.

Value to Porsche  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B with concrete mechanics (not a bare logo). (1) ENERGY / EVENT POWER: FE races in city centres on temporary infrastructure, and GE Vernova's grid, microgrid and clean-power capability can supply and certify cleaner event and paddock power and a hard-data sustainability story that FE's net-zero agenda rewards - a real, if off-car, operational contribution. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: GE Vernova sells to utilities, governments and data-centre operators, many of whom engage FE through its host-city and sustainability programmes - team hospitality becomes a channel, while Porsche gains a marquee, deep-pocketed electrification anchor. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'electrifying the world, racing on electricity' is an ownable co-marketing narrative pairing the electrification leader with the champion electric team, plus an energy/electrical engineering-talent halo. Porsche gets cleaner event power and a blue-chip anchor; GE Vernova gets an on-message global stage.

Deal Architecture

Official Electrification / Clean-Power Partner on the Porsche Formula E Team, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr, centred on clean event/paddock power and a sustainability-data and co-marketing programme.

Risks & Counters

Counter: Score and pitch it honestly as event-power + sustainability + brand; the thematic match and capacity are the strengths, named with concrete mechanics rather than a logo.
Utility-scale company, niche sponsorship  A power giant may see a race team as off-mission. Counter: Frame as the most on-message stage for the energy transition during its brand-build, right-sized and tied to event-power + sustainability outcomes.
