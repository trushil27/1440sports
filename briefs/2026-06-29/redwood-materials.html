The Case

Redwood Materials, founded by Tesla co-founder and former CTO JB Straubel, is North America's battery-circularity leader - recycling end-of-life batteries and producing sustainable cathode and anode materials for automakers and cell makers. It expanded its Series E to $425M at a $6B+ valuation (Jan 2026, as Google/Alphabet joined existing backers including Nvidia's NVentures, Capricorn and Goldman Sachs), on top of ~$2.3B in equity raised and a $2B Department of Energy loan commitment, and it has added a fast-growing, profitable second-life arm (Redwood Energy) that redeploys retired EV batteries as grid storage for AI data centres. It has never touched motorsport. For an all-electric championship whose Gen4 era (2026-2030) is built on circular design and recyclable materials, a battery-circularity leader is an unusually on-message partner. Battery-materials is crowded grid-wide (Umicore partners Mahindra; Envision's parent makes cells), but the lane is open on Andretti - its materials partner NAGASE works in carbon-fibre/composite recycling, a distinct stream - and Andretti is the American, sustainability-leading, charter FE team that fits an American circularity champion. Led by founder-CEO Straubel, the right and only real entry point.

Why Now

Post-Series E, Redwood has fresh capital and is scaling commercially (including a profitable new energy-storage line), which is when companies invest in brand and category leadership. There is no hard external clock, so the value of moving now is positional: claim the open battery-circularity lane on the right team before the category - already filling at Mahindra and Envision - consolidates across the grid, and build the founder relationship while inbound interest is still low (~30).

Why Andretti

Andretti is the American, charter, sustainability-leading FE team - a clean cultural match for an American battery-circularity champion - and its battery-materials/recycling lane is open (NAGASE covers composites, TWG AI covers AI, Porsche the powertrain). It lets Redwood own a distinct, on-message 'circular battery' category rather than competing with an incumbent, with a relationship that can run the full Gen4 cycle.

Value to Andretti  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B with real, named mechanics across three fronts. (1) SUSTAINABILITY / CIRCULARITY WORKSTREAM: Redwood recycles batteries and makes sustainable cathode materials, and its second-life arm redeploys retired packs as grid storage - a credible basis to build and certify a genuine closed-loop battery story for the team (end-of-life cell recovery, recycled-content sourcing, second-life energy at the team's facilities) that Formula E's audience and the FIA's net-zero/Gen4 circular-design agenda actually reward - hard substance, not a green logo. (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Redwood's customers and targets are automakers and cell makers, exactly the manufacturers and suppliers who populate the Formula E paddock - so team hospitality becomes a working channel into the EV-supply-chain decision-makers Redwood needs, while Andretti gains a well-capitalised ($6B+) circularity anchor in a category it currently lacks. (3) BRAND / CONTENT / TALENT: JB Straubel's Tesla pedigree and 'the company closing the loop on the batteries that race' is a powerful, ownable co-marketing narrative, with a content series on battery circularity and a recruiting halo for the battery/materials/energy engineers both sides compete for. Andretti gets a distinctive circularity partner and a real sustainability-data story; Redwood gets an on-message global brand stage and access to its exact buyers.

Deal Architecture

Official Battery Circularity / Sustainable Materials Partner tier on Andretti, minimum three years (aligned to the Gen4 cycle), ~$3-5M/yr, with category exclusivity in battery recycling/materials and a defined closed-loop + content activation workstream.

Risks & Counters

Category crowded grid-wide  Battery-materials partners already sit at Mahindra (Umicore) and Envision (AESC cells). Counter: Claim a team-level position on Andretti, where the lane is genuinely open - not grid-wide exclusivity; the differentiated American-team + circularity-leadership narrative is the wedge.
Capital-intensive, mission-focused  A heavy-industry company building gigafactories may see motorsport as off-mission. Counter: Frame as access to EV-supply-chain buyers, a certified closed-loop sustainability story, and a talent magnet - kept value-to-mid and tied to measurable outcomes.
Overlap perception with NAGASE  Andretti already has a materials/recycling partner (NAGASE). Counter: NAGASE works in carbon-fibre/composite recycling - a distinct material stream from battery circularity; position Redwood precisely in batteries, where the lane is open and complementary.
