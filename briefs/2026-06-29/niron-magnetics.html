The Case

Niron Magnetics (University-of-Minnesota-born) makes the Clean Earth Magnet - the first powerful permanent magnet built from iron and nitrogen, with no rare-earth elements and so no exposure to China's rare-earth supply chain. Backed by GM Ventures, Stellantis Ventures and Volvo Cars Tech Fund, it has raised ~$214M, is reportedly raising ~$500M at a ~$2-3B valuation (Axios, Mar 2026), and is opening the world's first commercial-scale iron-nitride plant (Sartell, MN; 1,500 t/yr) in 2026. It has never touched motorsport. The FE fit is real: the permanent magnet is the heart of the permanent-magnet synchronous motor FE manufacturers develop in-house, and Niron already integrates its magnets into EV motors (the MATTER variable-flux motor, Jan 2026). The why-now is sharp - China's rare-earth export controls have made magnet-supply independence a board-level priority for every automaker on the grid. Honest framing: adopting Niron in a race motor is a multi-year co-engineering commitment, not an off-the-shelf swap, so the near-term team value is a credible innovation + supply-resilience narrative and an R&D relationship - scored accordingly. Verified decision-maker: CEO Jonathan Rowntree (confirm the commercial/BD lead before outreach).

Why Now

China's 2025-26 rare-earth export controls turned magnet-supply independence into a board-level priority for every automaker, and Niron - the only commercial rare-earth-free magnet maker - is scaling fast (first commercial-scale plant online 2026; a reported ~$500M raise in progress), so brand and partnership investment is live. Formula E, whose manufacturers build permanent-magnet motors, is the most on-message proving stage for that story.

Why Nissan Formula E

Nissan is a Japanese EV pioneer that develops its own Formula E powertrain (motor included) and has an open 'EV tech' lane, so a rare-earth-free magnet is a genuinely relevant motor-materials story, not a logo - and magnet-supply independence is a direct strategic concern for a major OEM. No FE team has a magnet or motor-materials partner, so the lane is open for Niron to own.

Value to Nissan Formula E  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with honest, named mechanics. (1) MOTOR-MATERIALS / TECHNICAL: the permanent magnet is the core of the PM synchronous motor an FE manufacturer develops; Niron's iron-nitride magnet is a credible co-engineering thread for motor efficiency, thermal behaviour and rare-earth-free supply security, and Niron already integrates into EV motors (MATTER) - so the workstream is real, with the honest caveat that race-motor adoption is a multi-year R&D programme. (2) SUPPLY-CHAIN / STRATEGIC: for a global OEM like Nissan, a rare-earth-free magnet partner is a board-level supply-resilience story amid China's export controls - the FE deal is a public proof-point for it. (3) BRAND / CONTENT / TALENT: 'the magnet that frees the electric motor from rare earths, on the all-electric championship' is an ownable co-marketing narrative, a strong independence/sustainability content line, and a recruiting halo for the materials and motor engineers both sides compete for. Nissan gets a distinctive deep-tech motor-materials partner; Niron gets a flagship motorsport reference and access to its exact OEM buyers.

Deal Architecture

Official Magnet-Technology / Motor-Materials Partner on the Nissan Formula E Team, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr, with category exclusivity in magnets/motor-materials and a defined rare-earth-free co-engineering and content workstream tied to the motor and the supply-resilience story.
