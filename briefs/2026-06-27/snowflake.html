The Case

Snowflake (NYSE: SNOW), the AI Data Cloud, is at a reignited moment - FY26 revenue $4.68B (+33%), the strongest sequential dollar growth in its history, with its Cortex and Snowflake Intelligence (agentic AI) layer embedded across 13,600+ accounts. Notably, Snowflake already proved the F1 use-case: it partnered Aston Martin Red Bull Racing in 2019-20 to drive data-based race decisions, then left after 2020. It is NOT currently on a grid, so this is a clean re-entry - and the AI-data/Cortex era is exactly the reason to return, bigger. The workstream is real and on-profile (data/AI deep-tech): ingesting race-weekend telemetry, running strategy and simulation analytics, and applying Cortex AI on a team's data - the kind of data brain that, under the cost cap, increasingly decides results. Decision-maker: CEO Sridhar Ramaswamy (ex-Neeva, ex-Google Ads SVP). HONEST: data/cloud is a saturated F1 category (VAST, Oracle, TWG, Mphasis, Extreme Networks), so this is positioned as a distinct, complementary AI-Data-Cloud lane at a team where it doesn't clash (Haas), not as clean whitespace.

Why Now

Snowflake's AI-data thesis has reignited (record growth, Cortex/Intelligence adoption), so brand investment and visibility are rising, and F1's cost-cap era rewards the team with the best data/analytics brain. Snowflake left F1 in 2020 before its AI-data products existed - returning now, with Cortex, is a stronger story than the original; engage while the re-entry narrative is fresh.

Why Haas

Haas is the leanest team and gains the most from a turnkey AI Data Cloud (more analytics per cost-cap dollar). HONEST: Haas has an IT-services partner (Mphasis) and data/cloud is a contested category grid-wide - but Mphasis is apps/digital services, distinct from a data-warehouse/Cortex AI platform, so Snowflake's specific lane is defensibly open and complementary (verify against the current roster before any exclusivity claim).

Value to Haas  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A with real mechanics. (1) TECHNICAL (the core): a team's edge under the cost cap is how fast it turns race-weekend telemetry and historical data into decisions - Snowflake's AI Data Cloud ingests and unifies that data, runs strategy/simulation analytics, and applies Cortex AI/agents for natural-language insight, a genuine, deployable data-brain workstream (one Snowflake has run in F1 before, with Red Bull). (2) COMMERCIAL / B2B: Snowflake sells to the enterprise data and analytics buyers who fill the F1 paddock, so hospitality becomes a working pipeline channel, while Haas gains a marquee, deep-pocketed data anchor. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'the data cloud that makes the calls' is an ownable narrative on a data-driven sport, with a data/ML engineering-talent halo. Haas gets a transformative data platform and a blue-chip anchor; Snowflake gets a flagship live reference and a premium B2B stage for its AI-data story.

Deal Architecture

Official Data / AI-Cloud Partner on Haas, minimum three years, ~$4-6M/yr, with category exclusivity in data cloud/analytics and a working deployment running the team's telemetry analytics, race-strategy data and Cortex AI.
