The Case

ElevenLabs, the AI-voice and conversational-audio leader, raised a $500M Series D at an $11B valuation in Feb 2026 (Sequoia-led, with ICONIQ tripling down and a16z; NVIDIA-backed), closed 2025 with $330M+ ARR (Deutsche Telekom, Square, Revolut, the Ukrainian Government), and is building toward an IPO behind its ElevenAgents conversational-AI platform. It surfaced through the MD's ICONIQ-investment lens, and it has never entered motorsport. The fit with F1 is fan-and-content-led: F1's audience is global, young and multilingual, and ElevenLabs' AI dubbing, voice and conversational agents can localise and scale a team's content, social and fan experiences. HONEST framing: this is a brand/content/fan-engagement play (MODE B), not a performance or in-car workstream, so it is scored WARM, not HOT - and AI is broadly present on the grid, so the open lane is voice/audio specifically. CEO & co-founder Mati Staniszewski (with CTO Piotr Dabkowski).

Why Now

ElevenLabs is in a fast-scaling, pre-IPO brand-building phase (tripled valuation, $330M+ ARR), so it has the capital and the motive to invest in a global brand stage, and F1's multilingual global audience is an on-message fit for AI voice/content. The honest 'why now' is the brand/IPO window, not a forcing F1 event.

Why McLaren

McLaren has one of the largest, most global and most digitally-engaged fanbases in F1 and a content-leading brand, so AI multilingual voice/content and conversational fan experiences map onto a real audience-growth need. The voice/audio lane is distinct from McLaren's enterprise-software partners; the value is fan content and engagement, not car technology.

Value to McLaren  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B with concrete, named mechanics. (1) FAN CONTENT / LOCALISATION: ElevenLabs' AI dubbing and voice can localise the team's content, social and video across F1's many languages at scale - reaching global fans in their own language - a genuine, measurable content-and-audience workstream (not signage). (2) CONVERSATIONAL EXPERIENCES: ElevenAgents can power fan-facing conversational voice experiences (team app, driver/race Q&A, sponsor activations) - a distinctive, ownable fan product. (3) BRAND / TALENT: 'the voice of the team, in every language' is an on-message co-marketing story for an IPO-track AI brand, with an AI-engineering talent halo. McLaren gets scaled multilingual fan content and a marquee AI partner; ElevenLabs gets a global brand stage in front of a massive audience. (Scored honestly: fan-content/brand value, not performance.)

Deal Architecture

Official AI Voice / Fan-Content Partner on McLaren, minimum three years, ~$3-5M/yr, centred on AI multilingual content/dubbing and conversational fan experiences (ElevenAgents) - a brand/content partnership, not a technical deployment.
