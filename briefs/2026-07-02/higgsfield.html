The Case

Higgsfield AI, founded in 2023 by ex-Snap generative-AI head Alex Mashrabov, is the breakout generative-AI video platform - 'Cinema Studio' turns prompts into cinematic, camera-controlled, character-consistent video for creators and marketers, with ~20M users. It has scaled from $0 to a ~$500M revenue run-rate in ~14 months and is reportedly raising $300-500M at a ~$5B valuation (Jun 2026), up from a $1.3B valuation in January (Accel-led $130M Series A). It has never touched motorsport. This is deliberately NOT an in-car play: Higgsfield builds no car technology, so it is a CONTENT / fan-engagement partnership (MODE B). The fit is with Visa Cash App Racing Bulls - the grid's explicitly youngest, most social-media-native team - where AI cinematic social video is a genuine, named content workstream and the audiences overlap almost exactly. HONEST framing: it is a content/marketing exchange, not performance tech, and F1's AI lane is crowded - so it is positioned in the distinct video-content sub-lane on an open team, and scored as a strong-but-MODE-B signal (its deal-capability and momentum are the real edge). Decision-maker: Co-Founder & CEO Alex Mashrabov.

Why Now

Higgsfield is at a live capital + momentum peak (reportedly raising at ~$5B this quarter, ~$500M run-rate), which is exactly when a company invests in brand and distribution, and the AI-content lane in F1 is starting to fill (ElevenLabs-Audi) - so the value of claiming the video-content position with the right social-first team is now. The honest why-now is catalyst- and land-grab-driven, not a dated race trigger.

Why Visa Cash App Racing Bulls

Racing Bulls is the grid's youngest, most social-media-first team (the Red Bull sister team, built for a younger demographic) with no AI or content partner - so a generative-AI video engine is a real, on-narrative content workstream, and its fanbase is almost exactly Higgsfield's creator/consumer demographic. Chosen over the engine's greenfield default (Cadillac/Alpine) on that narrative fit, not just an open slot. No conflict (Higgsfield's backers - Accel etc. - are diversified funds, not team rivals).

Value to Visa Cash App Racing Bulls  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

A TWO-WAY exchange (business + tech both directions), not a logo fee - three concrete swaps. (1) AI CONTENT ENGINE <-> CINEMATIC CANVAS: Higgsfield gives the team a cinematic AI-video production engine (broadcast-quality social/fan content at a fraction of the cost and time of a traditional studio); in return the most cinematic, globally-watched sport becomes Higgsfield's flagship showcase and IP to prove and market its product to brands. (2) YOUNG AUDIENCE <-> CREATOR ECOSYSTEM: the team's young, social-native fanbase is exactly Higgsfield's customer demographic (a direct acquisition channel), while Higgsfield's ~20M-creator ecosystem and tools amplify the team's content reach and volume. (3) CO-MARKETING INNOVATION <-> BRAND CREDIBILITY: joint 'AI-made F1 content' campaigns position Racing Bulls as the innovative youth team and give Higgsfield a marquee brand association and case study. RB gets a scaled, low-cost content machine + an innovation story; Higgsfield gets a global stage, its exact buyers, and customer acquisition.

Deal Architecture

Official AI Content / Creative Partner on Visa Cash App Racing Bulls, minimum three years, mid-tier (Higgsfield is highly deal-capable), structured as a content-production + co-marketing workstream (cinematic social/broadcast video at scale, joint AI-content campaigns, creator-ecosystem amplification) - value exchanged both ways, not a pure logo fee.
