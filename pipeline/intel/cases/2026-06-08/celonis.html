The Case

On 12 May 2026 Celonis launched the Celonis Context Model, a real-time digital twin of a company's operations built from process data, and signed a definitive agreement to acquire Ikigai Labs, whose forecasting and simulation software rests on patents licensed from MIT; MIT becomes a Celonis shareholder and Ikigai co-founder Devavrat Shah joins as Chief Scientist, Enterprise AI. Celonis last priced at about $13B in an August 2022 extension led by Qatar Investment Authority, after a $1B Series D at $11B in June 2021; it has raised roughly $2.4B in total. Revenue is not disclosed.

Formula E's technology lanes are held by integrators, not software: TCS titles the Jaguar, Tech Mahindra sits on the Mahindra, Sand Technologies on the Envision and TWG AI on the Andretti. No process-intelligence or operations-software brand is on the grid. Porsche AG already runs Celonis across supply chain and production, and Porsche is doubling its Formula E presence to two works teams for the first GEN4 season.

Why Now

The Context Model launched on 12 May and the Ikigai deal is closing; the marketing push runs through the autumn. Season 12 ends with the London double-header on 15-16 August, and Season 13 opens the GEN4 era with two Porsche entries, so the partner slate for the new cars is set this summer.

What holds it back: no fresh capital since 2022 and no public revenue.

WHY PORSCHE FORMULA E TEAM

Porsche runs Celonis inside its own operations: with MHP, its consulting subsidiary, it has used Celonis process mining across more than 25 projects from inbound supply chain to production and finance. The Formula E team is the sharpest public expression of that culture, and from Season 13 it doubles to two works entries that need partners. Its Season 12 roster is reported to include TDK, NetApp, Synopsys and Cato Networks; none is an operations-software brand.

VALUE TO PORSCHE FORMULA E TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Celonis maps the team's race operations end to end: freight and parts flow across a global season, garage build and tear-down, spares approvals and supplier lead times, with the Context Model as a live digital twin the operations director can query and Ikigai's forecasting flagging bottlenecks before a race weekend. In return: Official Process Intelligence Partner status, livery on both cars, London hospitality.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Process Intelligence Partner across both Porsche works entries. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, the first three GEN4 seasons. Estimated $2-3M a year, part-payable in software and services. Scope: livery on both cars, naming of the team's operations twin, hospitality at Berlin and London.

Risks & Counters

NO NEW CAPITAL SINCE 2022  The $13B mark is four years old and revenue is undisclosed; appetite for a first motorsport budget is unproven. Counter: Lead with the Ikigai launch budget and a services-heavy structure; confirm the marketing envelope on the first call.
CUSTOMER, NOT SPONSOR  Porsche buys Celonis software; turning a supplier into a paying partner inverts the relationship. Counter: Position it as a joint showcase of Porsche's own process programme, co-funded, with Celosphere as the stage.
