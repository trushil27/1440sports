The Case

On 20 May Mercury announced a $200M Series D at a $5.2B valuation led by TCV, with Andreessen Horowitz, Coatue, CRV, Sapphire, Sequoia and Spark returning; CNBC puts the valuation up 49% in 14 months. Sequoia led the $300M Series C at $3.5B in March 2025, when Mercury reported $500M revenue for 2024 and ten straight profitable quarters. It reached $650M annualised revenue in Q3 2025 and serves more than 300,000 customers, one in three US startups. On 27 April the OCC granted conditional approval for Mercury Bank, N.A., headquartered in Utah under Jon Auxier, formerly CFO of SoFi Bank.

This is a capital and identity inflection at once: fresh money and a company about to become a bank in its own name. Fintech has already found the grid, with Airwallex at McLaren, Revolut as Audi's title partner, Cash App at Racing Bulls and Nu at Mercedes. Mercury's customers are American founders, the audience Cadillac was built to reach, and no bank or fintech has taken the seat at the new American team.

Why Now

The round was announced on 20 May and the bank charter is in its organisation phase; a brand about to become Mercury Bank sets its launch budget now. Canadian GP (24 May), Monaco (7 June) and the British GP (5 July) fall inside the window; Austin and Las Vegas follow in the autumn.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is the first new American team since 2016 and its inaugural roster, TWG AI, Claro, Core Scientific, IFS, Jim Beam, Tenneco and Tommy Hilfiger, carries no bank, fintech or payments partner: Official Banking Partner is a clean category on the American story of the season. Its US rounds, Austin and Las Vegas, are where Mercury's founders, investors and bankers sit. The team is closing its 2027 roster in its first year, so the category is open now and unlikely to stay so.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, with concrete mechanics. No engineering workstream: the value is audience and commerce. Mercury brings 300,000 business customers, one in three US startups, as a guest list and a marketing channel; founder hospitality at Austin and Las Vegas; banking, cards and treasury for the team's US supplier base; launch-year content as Mercury becomes a bank. In return: Official Banking Partner designation, livery presence and driver access for Mercury's founders.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Banking Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the bank launch and a probable listing window. Estimated $4-6M a year. Scope: sidepod or rear-wing placement, founder hospitality at Austin and Las Vegas, a Mercury Bank launch activation at the US rounds, co-branded content with the team's leadership.
