The Case

Form announced the $750M Series G on 12 August, led by T. Rowe Price, with Sequoia Capital, Janus Henderson, Franklin Templeton and PEAK6 new to the register. Total equity passes $2B; proceeds scale Form Factory 1 in Weirton, West Virginia. Axios reports the round priced at $1.75B pre-money, down from about $3B in October 2024. The order book explains the investors' patience: an announced backlog of 80 GWh, led by the 300 MW / 30 GWh Google and Xcel Energy system in Minnesota, and 12 GWh for Crusoe's AI data centres.

Weirton is the story a sponsor buys: a 550,000-square-foot battery plant on a former steel-mill site, around 400 people and a planned 500 MW a year by 2028, shipping 100-hour batteries to utilities and hyperscalers. Axios and TechCrunch report a listing being prepared for 2027. A down round is a reason to build the brand, not to hide: the next four quarters decide whether Form is priced as a manufacturer or a technology bet.

Why Now

The Series G closed on 12 August, and a reported 2027 listing puts brand-building inside the next four quarters. Formula E's GEN4 era opens in Jeddah on 18 December; the new Austin E-Prix on 6 February 2027 and Miami on 20 February give an American storage brand two home rounds before any roadshow.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti is the American team on the grid, run from Indianapolis; parent TWG Motorsports also operates the Cadillac F1 team. The roster is TWG AI, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo; the sponsor table shows no energy or storage brand, so the lane is open. Google Cloud is the championship's technology partner and Google is Form's anchor customer: the ecosystem already overlaps. For a utility-and-hyperscaler brand, an American car at Austin and Miami is the right stage.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, said plainly: a 100-hour iron-air battery has no race-day role. The mechanics are commercial. Official Energy Storage Partner status and sidepod placement on the American car; Austin and Miami hospitality for utility executives, regulators and hyperscaler energy buyers; Weirton factory content pairing a steel-town rebuild with the team's GEN4 debut; and pre-listing visibility to the capital-markets audience already in the Formula E paddock.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Energy Storage Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15 (2026/27 to 2028/29), bridging the reported 2027 listing and the first Google and Crusoe deliveries. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year. Scope: sidepod branding, Austin and Miami hospitality, factory and grid content, driver visits to Weirton, and a utility-customer programme through TWG Motorsports.
