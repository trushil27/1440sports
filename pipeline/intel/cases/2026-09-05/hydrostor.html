The Case

On 12 August Hydrostor closed a $230M equity investment: the conversion of the note committed by Canada Growth Fund, Goldman Sachs Alternatives and CPP Investments in February 2025, plus a strategic round from Baker Hughes, Realize Capital Partners and Hatch. The new-money share is not broken out. It follows US$55M from Export Development Canada in September 2025 and Baker Hughes' January agreement pairing equity with up to 1.4 GW of equipment orders. The money has a stated job: a 7 GW pipeline and Willow Rock, 500 MW in Kern County, to financial close this year.

This is the half-year in which a developer becomes a builder. Willow Rock holds a California licence granted on 19 December 2025 and a 25-year offtake with Central Coast Community Energy worth about $1B; Silver City in Broken Hill won grid-connection approval on 2 September and is due to start construction. Goderich in Ontario has run commercially since 2020. A company about to pour concrete in two countries needs a public identity it lacks.

Why Now

Financial close on Willow Rock and first construction at Silver City both fall in the fourth quarter, the window in which Formula E teams finalise Season 13 rosters before the Jeddah opener on 18 December. Austin follows on 6 February and Miami on 20 February 2027.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti's roster is TWG AI as primary partner, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo, with Nissan power from Season 13; there is no energy, storage, utility or grid brand on it. It is the American team for a company headquartered in Denver for the US with its first US plant in California; Austin and Miami arrive within weeks of Willow Rock's financial close. Envision Racing, the obvious energy fit, is titled by a rival grid-battery maker.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, honestly stated: a cavern-scale storage developer has no workstream on a race car. The mechanics are commercial. Formula E sells itself as the championship of electrification; a partner that stores electricity for eight hours gives Andretti an energy story its roster lacks. Hydrostor brings utilities, community-choice buyers, regulators and project financiers to Austin and Miami, and a technology narrative for the team's content.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Energy Storage Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, spanning Willow Rock construction and the first Silver City operations. Estimated $1-2M a season at partner level. Scope: car and suit placement, Austin and Miami hospitality, co-produced content on long-duration storage, and a Willow Rock site activation.
