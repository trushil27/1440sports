The Case

Base Power announced on 3 August that it had raised a $1B Series D at a $13B post-money valuation, led by Ribbit, Addition, Valor Equity Partners and JPMorganChase's Strategic Investment Group, with Altimeter, Coatue and others alongside. It follows a $1B Series C led by Addition in October 2025 at $4B and a $200M Series B in April 2025; the company says it has raised more than $2.5B. The same release launched Base Core, a 39.2 kWh home battery in production at Base Factory 1 in Austin, with proceeds going to national expansion.

The model is the story: Base sells homeowners a cheap battery and cheap electricity, then dispatches thousands of batteries into the ERCOT market as a virtual power plant. Utilities now buy it wholesale, with CoServ signing for 100 MW across North Texas in March 2026, and Illinois opened on 24 June in ComEd territory. The all-electric championship arrives in Base's own city in February, with the money for a national launch already in the bank.

Why Now

The round closed on 3 August and the money is earmarked for expansion beyond Texas and Illinois. Formula E's GEN4 era opens in Jeddah on 18 December, and the Austin E-Prix on 6 February 2027 is a home race in Base's own city; teams finalise Season 13 rosters this autumn.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti Formula E is the American team, owned by TWG Motorsports, whose parent TWG Global also fields the Cadillac F1 Team. Its roster is TWG AI as primary partner, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo, with Nissan supplying the GEN4 powertrain from Season 13; there is no energy, battery or utility partner. Austin on 6 February and Miami on 20 February 2027 give Base two home-market weekends in a fortnight, and a route to F1 later under the same owner.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with concrete mechanics. Base Core units powering the team's Austin and Miami hospitality and fan zone; a member-acquisition offer for ERCOT and ComEd households carried on the car and in team content; a grid-resilience story Andretti can tell on camera in Texas. In return Andretti gains an American consumer brand at the first US rounds, a utility and policy guest list, and a partner in the grid's emptiest category.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Energy Partner. THREE YEARS, Season 13 to Season 15, covering the national roll-out and a probable listing window. Estimated $2-3M a year, with the Austin and Miami activations budgeted separately. Scope: livery and race-suit placement, naming of the home-race hospitality, Base Core demonstrations, driver content for the Texas and Illinois markets, a route to the Cadillac F1 Team through TWG.
