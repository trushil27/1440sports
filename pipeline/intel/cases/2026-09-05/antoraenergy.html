The Case

Antora announced on 30 July that it had closed $550M of Series C funding, co-led by G2 Venture Partners and Eclipse, with Ribbit Capital, Salesforce Ventures, StepStone and John Doerr joining Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Lowercarbon and Decarbonization Partners. Total funding stands at $770M; Crunchbase News reports a $2.47B valuation, which the company has not stated. The money funds a second US manufacturing hub and large projects. The proof point landed in May: a 50 MW / 5 GWh thermal battery at POET's Big Stone City plant, built in under twelve months and now delivering energy.

Bloomberg framed the raise around the data-centre boom: Antora stores cheap electricity as heat in solid carbon blocks and returns it around the clock as heat or power, the load-shifting story hyperscalers and utilities are buying. The company says it has signed agreements with hyperscale operators but has not named them. A brand that sells to industrial CFOs, utilities and data-centre developers now needs a national stage on which to say 'reindustrialise America' out loud.

Why Now

The round closed on 30 July; the second-factory site is the next announcement. Formula E's GEN4 era opens in Jeddah on 18 December, and Season 13 adds a new Austin E-Prix on 6 February 2027 with Miami a fortnight later: two US rounds in Antora's first year at scale.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti Formula E is the American team on the grid, run from Indianapolis by TWG Motorsports, which also operates the Cadillac F1 team. Its roster is TWG AI, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo, and the sponsor table shows no energy or storage brand: the lane is open. GEN4 brings Nissan powertrains, Jake Dennis and Felipe Drugovich. For Antora the audience matters more than the podium: industrial buyers, utilities and hyperscalers at Austin and Miami, on an American car.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, stated honestly: a 50 MW thermal battery has no place in a garage. The mechanics are commercial. Official Energy Storage Partner designation and sidepod placement; hospitality at Austin and Miami for utility, hyperscaler and industrial buyers and state policymakers; co-produced content setting stored heat against the team's race-day energy management; and a step-up path to F1 through the same owner as Antora scales.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Energy Storage Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15 (2026/27 to 2028/29), covering the second-factory build and the first hyperscaler projects. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year. Scope: sidepod branding, Austin and Miami hospitality, engineering content, an industrial-customer programme through TWG Motorsports, and an option to step up to the Cadillac F1 team from 2028.
