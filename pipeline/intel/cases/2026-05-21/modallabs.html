The Case

On 21 May Modal announced $355M at a $4.65B post-money valuation, led by General Catalyst and Redpoint with Menlo, Bain Capital Ventures and Accel joining. Reuters reports the round closed in two tranches, the first at $2.5B, as AI-coding demand surged. Annualised revenue has passed $300M, fivefold the roughly $60M of September 2025, when Lux Capital led an $87M Series B at $1.1B. Modal runs over a billion isolated sandboxes for DoorDash, Cognition, Decagon, Suno and Physical Intelligence, and scaled Lovable to a million sandboxes in a weekend. Founded 2021; New York, San Francisco and Stockholm.

The grid has already priced this archetype: CoreWeave became Aston Martin Aramco's Official AI Cloud Computing Partner in May 2025, Core Scientific sits on Cadillac's first roster, and Modal's own customer Cognition joined Aston Martin this season. Modal is the developer-native version of the same story, with revenue to match, and it has not chosen a stage. Its buyers are the engineers and AI labs an F1 technology partnership reaches best.

Why Now

The round was announced on 21 May; brand budgets reset in the quarter after a raise. Canadian GP (24 May), Monaco (7 June) and the British GP (5 July) fall inside the window, and teams close 2027 partner rosters in the autumn.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams runs the most data-native roster on the grid: Atlassian as title partner, VAST Data for the data platform, Keeper for identity, and since this season Claude as Official Thinking Partner inside engineering and strategy. There is no cloud or GPU-compute partner. Modal's product is Python-native compute that scales from zero, the shape of a race team's bursty simulation load; its New York base gives Austin and Las Vegas a US story.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Race-team compute is bursty: machine-learning surrogates, driver-in-loop and strategy simulation, telemetry and video analytics between sessions. Modal turns any Python function into an autoscaling GPU job with cold starts in seconds, so Williams pays for seconds of compute rather than idle clusters, outside cost-cap capital. In return: Official AI Compute Partner designation, livery presence and Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for Modal's developer audience.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Compute Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the post-raise growth run and a probable listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in compute credits. Scope: engine-cover or sidepod placement, naming of the team's simulation compute environment, US-race hospitality, engineering content.
