The Case

Glow's release states $180M in funding at a $1.2B valuation, led by Sequoia, Cyberstarts, Greenoaks and Redpoint Ventures, with Index, Swish, Lux, Operator Collective and Holly Ventures participating; CTech reports it as three rounds in about a year, a $20M seed, a $60M Series A at $400M and a $100M Series B. The product is prevention-first: it decides which software, AI agents and developer tools may run on employee devices rather than detecting them afterwards. Paying customers span healthcare, retail and financial services; the money goes to a US go-to-market team and Glow Labs.

Cybersecurity is the grid's most-sold technology category, and every vendor bought the same thing: CrowdStrike at Mercedes, Bitdefender at Ferrari, Keeper at Williams, Cato Networks at Alpine, a paddock full of the CISOs they sell to. Glow's leadership has run that playbook from inside: COO Emily Heath sat on Wiz's board through its $32B sale to Google and was a Cyberstarts partner. A stealth exit with $180M is when a company buys its stage.

Why Now

The stealth exit was 22 July and the stated use of funds is a US go-to-market team; the first brand budget follows the first sales hires. The United States GP in October and the Las Vegas GP in November sit inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY TGR HAAS F1 TEAM

Haas lists cybersecurity as an open product category: Toyota Gazoo Racing as title and technical partner, Mphasis for digital services, CommScope and Ruckus for connectivity, and no security vendor. Mphasis sells cyber services, a channel for a deployment rather than a clash. The lean American privateer prices an entry a 100-person company can carry, Kannapolis sits in Glow's chosen sales market, and three US races a year put Glow in front of the CISOs it is hiring to reach.

VALUE TO TGR HAAS F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A race team's engineers run exactly the tools Glow polices: developer environments, package managers, AI agents and copilots on laptops that travel to 24 circuits. Glow maps what runs on every device, blocks risky packages and unsanctioned agents before they execute, and flags devices whose detection tools have gone quiet. Deployment across Kannapolis, Banbury and the trackside estate, a published case study, Glow Labs research on the team's threat surface.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Endpoint Security Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the US go-to-market build and the next raise. Estimated $2-4M a year, part-payable in product. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, deployment across the team's devices, US-race hospitality for CISO prospects, Glow Labs content.
