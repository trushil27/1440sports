The Case

Qumra Capital joined the new investors; Vertex Ventures, Third Point Ventures, DTCP and Intel Capital returned. The $38M Series B, co-led by Third Point and DTCP with Microsoft's M12, closed in October 2024. In April Gartner named Zenity 'the company to beat' in AI agent governance. Founders Ben Kliger and Michael Bargury built security products together at Microsoft; the company now employs more than 230 people, about 150 in Israel, runs sales, marketing and operations from New York, and counts Fortune 500 and Global 2000 enterprises, SoftBank Corp among them, as customers.

Every AI agent an enterprise deploys, from Microsoft Copilot to custom builds, acts with someone's permissions; Zenity inventories them, limits what they can reach and detects when their intent drifts. The grid has bought defence in depth, CrowdStrike at Mercedes, Bitdefender at Ferrari, Keeper at Williams, Rubrik at McLaren, but nobody has bought the agent layer. The Norwest-led Series C funds exactly the global expansion a sponsorship serves.

Why Now

The Series C closed on 3 August with a global-expansion mandate, and Gartner's April verdict gives the brand a window to own its category before rivals catch up. The United States GP in Austin (late October) and Las Vegas (November) sit inside the horizon; teams settle 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac's debut roster has TWG AI as exclusive AI partner, Core Scientific for the data centre and IFS for enterprise software, and no cybersecurity partner, so the agent-security lane is clean. A team assembling its estate in 2026 is adopting agents inside Copilot and its own AI programme at the same moment, with no legacy controls to retrofit. Zenity's New York commercial base and Fortune 500 customer list match an American flag-bearer selling to enterprise CIOs.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Zenity inventories every AI agent in the team's estate and what each can reach; enforces least privilege before an agent touches design, supplier or strategy data; and watches agent behaviour at runtime for prompt injection and intent drift. For a first-season team standing up Copilot beside an exclusive AI programme, it is the control layer that lets engineers use agents without exposing the car.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Security Partner, in the cybersecurity category. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the expansion the Series C funds. Estimated $2-4M a year, part in platform licences. Scope: garage and car placement, an 'agents in the garage' content series, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for CISOs and CIOs, a Cadillac reference deployment.
