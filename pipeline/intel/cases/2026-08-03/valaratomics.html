The Case

The round is company-announced: $1B of Series B equity led by Sequoia, whose Shaun Maguire joins the board, with Atreides, Point72, Snowpoint and Valor Equity Partners participating, plus a $200M credit facility led by Erebor Bank with J.P. Morgan, Crescent Cove and Hercules Capital. Bloomberg and TechCrunch report a $6B valuation, about three times the prior round; the company has not disclosed it. The engineering is real: Ward 250 reached criticality on 18 June at the Utah San Rafael Energy Lab under the DOE Reactor Pilot Program, the first DOE-authorised reactor built outside a national laboratory.

The identity moment is public and presidential: on 1 July Ward 250 powered an NVIDIA Blackwell chip live on stage, the first US advanced reactor to run an AI chip, and the two companies announced a study for a 30 MW water-free AI facility in Utah. On 24 July Taylor stood in the Oval Office as one of four reactor CEOs the President honoured. A company selling firm power to hyperscalers needs a brand beyond the trade press.

Why Now

The round closed on 3 August and teams settle Season 13 rosters this autumn before the Jeddah opener on 18 December; the Austin E-Prix on 6 February and the Miami E-Prix on 20 February 2027 put two US rounds in front of the utilities, hyperscalers and regulators Valar sells to.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti is the American team in an electric championship, and its roster already carries TWG AI, an AI-infrastructure brand in the market Valar's reactors serve. The sponsor table shows no energy, fuel or power partner at Andretti and no nuclear or firm-power brand anywhere in Formula E, while TotalEnergies holds the energy lane at DS Penske, Shell at Lola Yamaha ABT and Castrol at Jaguar. An electric series asked where its power comes from meets an American reactor company with an answer.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with mechanics. A reactor is not a race-team input, and the brief says so. The value is commercial: Official Clean Firm Power Partner, an American energy story alongside TWG AI on the car, and Austin and Miami hospitality for utilities, hyperscalers, governors and federal officials no other Andretti partner brings. Valar gets a public stage; the team gets livery, content and a partner with $1B to spend on being known.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Clean Firm Power Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, spanning the Utah facility study and first commercial orders. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year. Scope: sidepod placement, Austin and Miami hospitality, a power-behind-the-grid content series, driver visits to Utah.

Risks & Counters

SERIES OPTICS  Formula E markets itself on sustainability; a fission brand may draw objection from the promoter, the team's owner or NGOs. Counter: Clear it with team and series early; frame as clean firm power for electrification.
PROOF OF CONCEPT ONLY  Ward 250 ran at about 100 kW thermal for a demo; commercial power is years away, and the $6B is Bloomberg's figure, not the company's. Counter: Anchor on the DOE milestone and the Oval Office; confirm the valuation on the first call.
