The Case

The oversubscribed round brought in Acrew, Blue Cloud Ventures, Demeter, Singapore's EDBI, PSG, SAIC and Sapphire, with Craft, Prosperity7, Qualcomm Ventures, Ridge and SignalFire returning. The company reports 120% year-on-year ARR growth and more than 7,000 organisations protected, four of them Fortune 10; TechCrunch puts ARR near $100M (reported). Founders Snehal Antani and Anthony Pillitiere met at Joint Special Operations Command; Antani was later CTO of Splunk. The money funds international expansion, with offices opened in Amsterdam in June, Australia and Singapore, and a partner network.

Cybersecurity is the most-sold technology category on the grid: CrowdStrike at Mercedes, Bitdefender at Ferrari, Keeper at Williams, Okta and Rubrik at McLaren, Cato Networks at Alpine, and Red Bull's 1Password. Every one sells defence. Horizon3 sells the attack, continuously, and its new CMO Andres Botero ran marketing at Rubrik in the period its McLaren partnership was being built. A category leader with no seat, and a marketer who has watched one taken.

Why Now

The round closed on 3 August, a CMO arrived in January with a category-leadership mandate, and offices are opening on three continents. The United States GP in Austin (late October) and Las Vegas (November) fall inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is the American debutant building its technology roster from scratch under a stated 'fewer, bigger, better' partner policy: IFS runs the enterprise stack, Core Scientific the data centre, TWG AI the exclusive AI programme. It carries no cybersecurity partner, so Official Cybersecurity Partner is cleanly ownable. A first-season estate across Fishers, Charlotte and Silverstone has never been tested in anger, and Horizon3's defence-rooted American story suits a GM-backed flag-bearer.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. NodeZero runs autonomous penetration tests continuously across the team's estate: the Fishers and Silverstone factory networks, the garage network rebuilt at every circuit, supplier and partner integrations, and the Core Scientific and TWG AI environments. Findings are ranked by exploitability and re-tested after each fix, so a small security staff works on what an attacker would actually use.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Cybersecurity Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the international build-out. Estimated $4-6M a year, part-payable in NodeZero licences. Scope: car and garage placement, naming of the team's security-validation programme, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for CISOs and federal buyers, content on how a new team defends itself.
