The Case

Fundomo led the round; Arm, Hudson River Trading and Reed Hastings joined, and existing backers Hummingbird, Plural, Creandum, Crane, Phoenix Court and Transition increased their commitments. Six months earlier Hummingbird had led a $220M Series A at just over $1B. OLIX builds specialised inference silicon connected by an optical network, with first systems due to customers in the second half of 2027. It is recruiting engineers across London, Bristol, Austin, Toronto and San Francisco, has hired former Wise CFO Matt Briers as CFO and put Stanford's Nick McKeown on the board.

The grid has already priced AI silicon: Groq became an Official Partner of McLaren in September 2025 with its inference chips on the car from Singapore, and Arm, now an OLIX investor, sits on Aston Martin Aramco's roster. OLIX is the British entrant without a seat. A company that must recruit against Nvidia and sell to hyperscalers before its first chip ships has a brand to build, and now the money to build it.

Why Now

The round closed on 3 August and the first systems ship in the second half of 2027, so a three-season deal from 2027 straddles launch. Teams settle 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter, and the United States GP in Austin, where OLIX is hiring, falls in late October.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams runs a data-native roster: Atlassian as title partner, VAST for the data platform, Keeper for identity, Airia and Brillio, and Claude as Official Thinking Partner. It carries no semiconductor or AI-compute partner, so Official AI Silicon Partner is a clean category. Grove is a British engineering revival to match a British chip champion, and the team's own arc, a rebuilt technical base chasing far larger incumbents, is OLIX's arc.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with concrete mechanics. OLIX has nothing to deploy before the second half of 2027, so year one is brand and talent: the Williams engineering-recruitment platform for a company hiring in five cities; sovereign-AI storytelling with government backing at Silverstone; Austin hospitality for hyperscaler and capital-markets buyers. From 2027, a defined evaluation of OLIX inference systems on Williams' machine-learning workloads, as an early reference deployment.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Silicon Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, bridging first shipments and a probable listing. Estimated $3-5M a year, stepping up with product delivery; a technical-evaluation clause from 2027. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, engineering-recruitment content, British and United States GP hospitality, an early-access reference programme.
