The Case

On 6 July EnergyX announced a $225M equity investment from Eni for a minority stake in Project Black Giant, a lithium brine and refining project in Chile's Antofagasta region designed for 52,500 tonnes a year of lithium carbonate across two phases, with Eni taking rights to up to 25% of output. It sits on top of a $690M debt letter of intent from the US EXIM Bank; total project cost is put just below $1B. General Motors led the $50M Series B in April 2023; a $75M Regulation A+ raise built about 40,000 retail shareholders (reported).

Formula E is the world championship built around the electric car, and the lithium that goes into it has no name on the grid. Energy majors do: TotalEnergies at DS Penske, Shell at Lola Yamaha ABT, Castrol at Jaguar. EnergyX now carries an energy major's endorsement, an American home base and a Chilean flagship, and its first US plant, Project Lonestar in the Smackover, came online in March.

Why Now

Eni's money arrived on 6 July; Season 12 ends with the London E-Prix in mid-August and Season 13 opens in Jeddah in December with the GEN4 car. The Austin E-Prix on 6 February 2027 is a home race for an Austin company, and a partner signed before the launch is on the car for it.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti is the American team, owned by TWG Motorsports, with TWG AI as primary partner, Nissan GEN4 powertrains from Season 13 and Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo alongside. There is no energy, battery or materials partner, and the roster is being rebuilt for the new car. The ownership link is real: General Motors, EnergyX's Series B lead, is TWG Motorsports' partner in the Cadillac F1 Team. Austin and Miami in February 2027 are home rounds for the team and for EnergyX.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, stated honestly: Formula E runs a spec battery, so no EnergyX lithium enters the car. The mechanics are concrete: a lithium-to-lap supply-chain story told from the Smackover plant to the Austin grid; hospitality at Austin and Miami for the company's retail shareholders and strategic investors; a US critical-minerals narrative the team can carry into policy and OEM conversations; driver content on where a battery begins.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Lithium and Critical-Minerals Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, spanning Black Giant's build and a probable listing. Estimated $1.5-3M a year. Scope: rear-wing or sidepod placement on the GEN4 car, Austin and Miami hospitality with a shareholder allocation, plant-to-track content.

Risks & Counters

PROJECT MONEY, NOT BRAND MONEY  Eni's $225M goes into Project Black Giant, not the corporate budget; the company is pre-revenue and its filings report losses. Counter: Size the entry small, tie it to Austin and the shareholder programme, and let year one earn year two.
RETAIL-FUNDED PROFILE  A company that raises from 40,000 small investors invites scrutiny of its promotional spend. Counter: Frame it as shareholder engagement and OEM-facing storytelling with a stated budget, never a vanity buy.
