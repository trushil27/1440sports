The Case

Emerald AI raised a $150M oversubscribed Series A on 25 Aug 2026, valuing the grid-flexibility software company at $1.05B and lifting total capital raised past $220M. Energize Capital and DCVC co-led the round, joined by NVIDIA, Siemens, GE Vernova, RWE, Aramco Ventures and Samsung Ventures — twelve Fortune Global 500 companies now hold equity. The raise marks a BRAND RECKONING moment: Emerald moves from demonstration pilots to multi-megawatt, full data-center-scale commercial deployment, and needs a visibility platform to match its new enterprise-trust ambitions.

No F1 or Formula E grid-flexibility sponsor exists on either grid, leaving the energy-management category entirely open. As hyperscalers and utilities scrutinize Emerald's enterprise credentials, a global motorsport platform offers scale-validation no single-utility pilot can match. The slot that matches Emerald's grid-flex narrative architecturally is open at Nissan Formula E Team.

Why Now

Emerald's Series A closes as Formula E teams open 2026-27 season sponsorship renewals, an 8-12 week window before grid-partner commitments lock for pre-season. No rival grid-flexibility platform has claimed the lane; a first-mover conversation now secures placement before category competitors reach the paddock.

WHY NISSAN

Nissan Formula E Team carries no grid-flexibility or energy-management partner today, and its electrification-first positioning gives Emerald's load-shedding narrative direct thematic alignment no combustion-era team can offer. Formula E's entire proposition — cars drawing grid power, racing on street circuits embedded in urban grids — mirrors Emerald Conductor's real-time demand-response story. Nissan's global EV manufacturing scale and utility relationships give the team credibility to co-present Emerald's technology to the same hyperscaler and utility audiences it is courting.

VALUE TO NISSAN FORMULA E TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

Emerald Conductor's real-time grid-flex and load-shedding engine maps directly onto Nissan's factory energy operations and race-weekend power draw — EV battery charging, simulator compute loads, and trackside power management all become live demonstration surfaces. Deployment surface: garage energy dashboards, factory telemetry walls, broadcast graphics showing real-time grid interaction during ePrix sessions, and pit-wall power-allocation feeds during qualifying and race format changes.

Deal Architecture

A three-year entry-tier grid-flexibility partnership, in the $2-4M annual range, makes Emerald the team's energy-management partner across the 2026-27 season, with activation anchored at flagship street-circuit race weekends. THREE YEARS secures renewal leverage as Emerald scales from pilot utilities toward full hyperscaler contracts.
