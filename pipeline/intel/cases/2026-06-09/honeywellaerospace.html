The Case

Honeywell announced on 5 June that its board has set 15 June 2026 as the record date for the spin-off of Honeywell Aerospace, with distribution on 29 June on a one-for-two basis; the shares trade when-issued as HONAV from around 15 June and regular-way on Nasdaq as HONA from 29 June. The business reported 2025 net sales of $17.4B, up 12% organically, led by commercial aftermarket and defence. Headquartered in Phoenix, it is led by Jim Currier as President and CEO with Josh Jepsen as CFO, chaired by former Eaton chief Craig Arnold.

Aerospace is already on the grid: Boeing sits on the Alpine, Atlas Air on the Aston Martin, and Bombardier, a Honeywell customer, has left the Aston Martin roster. The engines-and-avionics tier has never taken a seat. That leaves a supplier-grade engineering story, from megawatt electric machines to thermal management, without a grid home just as Formula 1's new hybrid era begins and a British team starts its Honda power-unit years.

Why Now

Regular-way trading starts 29 June, the day after the Austrian GP; the British GP on 5 July is the company's first race weekend as an independent listing. A spin-off's first hundred days set its brand, and the marketing budget is now Honeywell Aerospace's own to spend.

WHY ASTON MARTIN ARAMCO F1 TEAM

Aston Martin is the one team where an aerospace supplier's story is already on the car: Aramco as title partner, Honda supplying the power unit from 2026, Atlas Air in the cargo-aviation lane, Arm and CoreWeave on compute. Bombardier's business-jet slot has departed, so the aerospace-engineering lane at Silverstone is open. Honeywell's 1-megawatt generator, about 97% efficient and usable as a motor, speaks to the hybrid era better than any consumer brand could.

VALUE TO ASTON MARTIN ARAMCO F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - brand launch with an engineering exchange. The car carries the new company's name through its first hundred days as a listing; hospitality at the British GP and the US rounds hosts airline, defence and investor guests. Outside the regulated power unit, Honeywell's electrical-machine and thermal-management engineers work with the Silverstone campus on cooling and test rigs: aerospace-grade know-how for the team, a technical story for Honeywell.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Aerospace Technology Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, the company's first three years as a listed business. Estimated $5-8M a year. Scope: sidepod placement, the engineering exchange above, hospitality at the British GP and the US rounds for airline, defence and investor guests.

Risks & Counters
