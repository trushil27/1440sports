The Case

On 9 July Oxylabs announced a $130M investment from the Warburg Pincus Capital Solutions Founders Fund; the release does not print a valuation, and the $3.6B figure is reported by SiliconANGLE, TNW and Dealroom. It is the company's first external funding since 2015. The business serves over 350,000 customers across cybersecurity, e-commerce and finance, and Dealroom and IBTimes report $350M of annual recurring revenue, citing the company. Vytautas Savickas took over as chief executive in the second quarter, with founder Julius Černiauskas moving to chairman.

The grid has learned to sell data infrastructure: VAST at Williams, Confluent and Dynatrace at Racing Bulls, Alteryx at McLaren, TWG AI at Cadillac. None of them touches the public web, where the grounding data for AI agents comes from and where Oxylabs sits. A bootstrapped European company that just took growth capital from a New York investor has one job now: be known by the enterprises and AI builders that buy this.

Why Now

First outside money and a new CEO arrive together, which is when a company decides what it wants to be known for. The Dutch GP and Italian GP fall inside the window, Austin and Las Vegas close the US season, and 2027 rosters are set in the fourth quarter.

WHY AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM

Audi's first season carries a technology roster still being written: Revolut as title partner, ElevenLabs, NinjaOne, Extreme Networks, JigSpace and Libertex. There is no data-platform, web-data or AI-data partner, and the profile lists enterprise software as open. The introduction is unusually warm: Revolut runs its European bank from Vilnius, licensed by the Bank of Lithuania, the city where Oxylabs was founded. A European scale-up going to America suits a German works team with a fintech on the car.

VALUE TO AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with a working edge. Oxylabs' web intelligence is what brands use for counterfeit and grey-market monitoring, price tracking and ticket-resale surveillance across marketplaces; a new team's licensing and merchandise operation can run exactly that. On the brand side: an AI-infrastructure name for the team's technology story, developer-community activation through Oxylabs' channels, and hospitality at Zandvoort, Monza and the US rounds.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Web Intelligence Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the US build-out and a plausible listing window. Estimated $2-4M a year, part in data services. Scope: sidepod or mirror placement, brand-protection workstream for the licensing programme, European and US-race hospitality, developer content.
