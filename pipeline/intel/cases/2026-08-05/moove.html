The Case

The round is announced by the lead investor and the company: $250M at $2.1B, led by Mubadala, co-led by Woven Capital and Ion Pacific, with BlueCrest and Sona Capital new alongside BlackRock, MUFG, Franklin Templeton and Uber. Moove reports about $420M of annual recurring revenue from roughly 42,000 vehicles in 29 cities across 13 countries. Proceeds go to owning autonomous fleets outright and to 'Nests', robotics-first depots where robotaxis are charged, serviced and orchestrated, with the autonomous workforce growing from about 150 to about 500 by year-end.

The identity moment is the pivot from financing drivers in Lagos to owning the operating layer beneath robotaxis. Since Waymo contracted Moove for Phoenix and Miami in December 2024, every vehicle it runs there has been a Jaguar I-PACE; London, Waymo's first European city, is next, with Moove as fleet partner and service pending regulators. A company about to put its name on driverless Jaguars in three cities needs a brand its riders, regulators and partners recognise.

Why Now

The round closed on 5 August and teams close Season 13 rosters this autumn, before the Jeddah opener on 18 December; Moove's two Waymo cities then host the Miami E-Prix on 20 February and the London E-Prix on 29-30 May 2027, and London's driverless launch is due the same season.

WHY JAGUAR TCS RACING

Jaguar TCS Racing is the only team whose road car Moove already runs: the Waymo fleets it operates in Phoenix and Miami, and will operate in London, are Jaguar I-PACEs. Jaguar has committed to the GEN4 era through 2030, so the platform outlives a three-year deal. The sponsor table shows TCS as title partner, Castrol and Jaguar itself, and no fleet, mobility or ride-hail partner at Jaguar or on any Formula E roster; the lane is clean and the London E-Prix is the home round.

VALUE TO JAGUAR TCS RACING  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with mechanics. Moove's product, depots and charging orchestration for EV fleets, is not a race-car input, so the value is commercial: Official Fleet Partner, a Jaguar-branded robotaxi story in race weeks at Miami and London, rider-to-race activations, and a demonstrable road-EV use of the I-PACE that the racing programme was built to promote. Moove brings Mubadala, Toyota, Uber and Waymo into the Jaguar suite; the team gives livery, hospitality and driver content.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Fleet Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, spanning the London launch and the fleet-ownership build. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year. Scope: sidepod placement, Miami and London hospitality, co-branded I-PACE fleet livery on race weeks, driver appearances at Nest openings.

Risks & Counters

NOT A RACE INPUT  Depots and charging orchestration give the team nothing to use; a halo at a manufacturer team can read as a logo. Counter: Anchor the deal on the I-PACE fleets and the two matching rounds; keep the activation about cars, not depots.
CAPITAL GOES TO FLEETS  The round funds owning autonomous vehicles outright, a capital-intensive plan that competes with marketing spend. Counter: Size the entry small and value-in-kind heavy: fleet livery and rider activations cost Moove little.
