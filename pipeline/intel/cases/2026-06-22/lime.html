The Case

Lime announced the roadshow on 22 June with pricing expected the following week; Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan lead the books and Uber, holder of 24.4% pre-IPO, has indicated interest in up to $20M of shares. The business on sale is no longer a scooter story: FY2025 revenue was $886.7M, up 29%, with adjusted EBITDA of $218.1M and free cash flow of $103.8M, a third positive year, against a $59.3M net loss driven by debt costs. Proceeds repay a $115M term loan at 10% due in September 2026, which frees the cash flow behind it.

A newly listed consumer brand needs a public-market story about scale and cities, and Formula E races in Lime's cities. London, where Lime has served more than 400,000 customers, hosts the season finale on 15-16 August; Berlin and Madrid follow in the GEN4 season. No shared-mobility brand has ever raced on the grid, so the first one defines the category.

Why Now

Pricing lands next week and the first public-company brand budget is set in July, with the London E-Prix on 15-16 August eight weeks out. Season 13 opens in Jeddah in December with a GEN4 car and a 21-race calendar.

Bottom Line

WHY ENVISION RACING

Envision Racing carries Envision Group as title partner with Sand Technologies, Teijin and Genpact alongside; there is no mobility or consumer-transport partner, so the lane is clean. The team is British-based and built its identity on the Race Against Climate Change, the same car-replacement argument Lime makes to city halls. It is an independent, not an OEM works entry, so a two-wheel brand is a fit rather than a conflict. London is its home race and Lime's largest European market.

VALUE TO ENVISION RACING  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with hard mechanics. Lime-liveried rider zones and ride credits for ticket holders at the London, Berlin and Madrid E-Prix, team-branded e-bikes in each host city during race week, and a rider-app audience in the tens of millions for Envision's climate content. For the team: a consumer brand that turns a city race into a city-wide campaign, and Uber-backed distribution the roster does not yet have.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Micromobility Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, the whole first GEN4 cycle. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year plus in-kind fleet and ride credits. Scope: car and suit placement, host-city activation in Lime cities, app and rider communications, hospitality at London and Berlin.

Risks & Counters

A SCOOTER BRAND IN A B2B PADDOCK  Lime is a consumer brand with thin margins after debt; a sponsorship line competes with city-licence costs and fleet capex. Counter: Size the entry at the low end and weight it to in-kind fleet and ride credits, which cost Lime little.
LISTING FIRST, BRAND SECOND  Pricing and the quiet period come before any marketing decision; the window may not open until the first public quarter. Counter: Approach after pricing with a London-finale package that needs only a July decision.
