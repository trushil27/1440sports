The Case

On 6 May 2026 Infineon reported second-quarter revenue of EUR 3.81B, up 6%, and raised fiscal 2026 guidance: revenue growing significantly, a segment result margin of about 20% and adjusted free cash flow of about EUR 1.65B, up from EUR 1.4B. AI data-centre revenue is expected at about EUR 1.5B this year and EUR 2.5B next. On 8 June it announced with Siemens that its silicon-carbide modules will sit inside Siemens' semiconductor circuit breakers. This is a EUR 14.7B, 57,000-person company at a demand peak, with a board-level chief marketing officer.

Silicon carbide lets an inverter switch faster and waste less, which in an electric race car is range and lap time. Jaguar first ran it in 2017 and made its US supplier Official Power Semiconductor Partner in 2023; that supplier went through Chapter 11 last year and is absent from the 2025/26 roster. GEN4 arrives in December with 600 kW, permanent all-wheel drive and 700 kW regeneration: the series' biggest power-electronics step.

Why Now

GEN4 debuts at the Jeddah E-Prix on 18-19 December 2026, the first season of a four-season era Jaguar has signed for. Season 13 partner rosters close in the autumn; a partner agreed before the London E-Prix in August is on the car for the first 600 kW race.

WHY JAGUAR TCS RACING

Jaguar TCS Racing carries TCS as title and technology partner, Castrol on fluids and Jaguar's own powertrain. There is no semiconductor or electronics brand on the roster. It is the team that proved silicon carbide in Formula E, has signed for the GEN4 era through Season 16, and whose parent JLR sells the road cars the technology transfers into. For a German company with an Americas base in California, a British works team racing in Austin and Miami is the stage.

VALUE TO JAGUAR TCS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. GEN4 doubles the electrical workload: 600 kW across two motors, 700 kW regeneration, permanent all-wheel drive. Infineon supplies CoolSiC power modules and gate drivers for the traction inverters, application engineering on switching losses and cooling, and silicon carbide for the pit equipment. In return: Official Power Semiconductor Partner, inverter-cover and halo branding, Austin and Miami hospitality for Infineon's automotive and data-centre customers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Power Semiconductor Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15 (2026-2029), inside the GEN4 era. Estimated EUR 3-5M a year, part in components and engineering. Scope: inverter modules and support, halo and rear-wing branding, Austin, Miami and Berlin hospitality, technical content.

Risks & Counters

ROAD-CAR SUPPLIER TENSION  JLR contracted its road-car silicon carbide to Wolfspeed in 2022, and Infineon buys wafers from Wolfspeed. Counter: Position Infineon as the GEN4 racing partner, separate from road-car supply; the wafer link makes it familiar, not a rival.
NO SPORT SINCE 2012  Infineon has not bought a sports property since the raceway naming rights lapsed; appetite is unproven. Counter: Anchor on the engineering workstream and the CMO's board mandate; a component-level deal is cheaper than a circuit name.
