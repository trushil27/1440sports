The Case

Odyssey announced on 17 June a $310M Series B at a $1.45B valuation, led by Natural Capital with Amazon, AMD Ventures, GV, EQT and IQT participating; total funding stands at $337M (Crunchbase). NVentures had backed the Series A only in February. Founded in 2023 by Oliver Cameron, who founded Voyage and ran product at Cruise, and Jeff Hawke, formerly of Wayve, the company ships physics-accurate world models, Odyssey-2 Max and the real-time Starchild-1, through an API. AWS becomes its preferred cloud provider, with Trainium chips in the mix.

A world model is a simulator that learns physics from video and runs in real time. That is a tool for a race team's simulation group, not a chatbot, and it arrives in the year the grid has to learn a new car from scratch. Odyssey has a Zurich office on Hinwil's doorstep, a founding team fluent in vehicles, and a fresh $310M that will be spent on being known to enterprise buyers.

Why Now

The round closed on 17 June; a lab that has just taken $310M from Amazon, AMD and GV spends the next two quarters becoming visible to buyers. Audi's first season is the one moment a new technical partner gets an origin story, and 2027 rosters close in the fourth quarter.

WHY AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM

Audi enters Formula 1 in 2026 as a works team, chassis in Hinwil and power unit in Neuburg, with Revolut as title partner and a roster built from scratch: Extreme Networks, JigSpace, ElevenLabs and NinjaOne on the technology side. Nothing covers vehicle-dynamics or world simulation, a lane almost no team has branded. Additive Industries and Hewlett Packard Enterprise left within twelve months, so engineering slots are being re-let. A Zurich office suits a Hinwil team.

VALUE TO AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Real-time, physics-accurate world models generate interactive track environments from video: new or resurfaced circuits for the driver-in-the-loop simulator before a laser scan exists, wet and low-light conditions on demand, synthetic footage to train the team's vision analytics. Audi's Hinwil simulation group trials the API under engineering supervision; Odyssey gets a named Formula 1 engineering reference for its enterprise sales.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official World Simulation Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning Audi's first regulation cycle. Estimated $2-4M a year, part-payable in model access and compute. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, naming of the simulation programme, Hinwil engineering content, hospitality at Monza, Austin and Las Vegas.

Risks & Counters
