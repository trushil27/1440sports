The Case

Intercontinental Exchange committed up to $2B in October 2025, making the owner of the New York Stock Exchange the distributor of Polymarket's event data; an April raise at $15B then brought in DE Shaw and G Squared (reported). Volume crossed $10B in a month for the first time in March 2026, and trading fees arrived on 30 March. Ari Borod, Fanatics' former chief business officer, joined in February as President of Sports Business Development; Warren Jenson, once Amazon's finance chief, became the first CFO on 10 September.

Rightsholders now sell this category on its own. MLB made Polymarket its exclusive prediction-market exchange in March, a deal reported at up to $300M; the NHL took both Polymarket and Kalshi; the Yankees and Rangers signed at club level; Kalshi became the US Open's prediction-market partner on 31 August. Formula 1 sold betting-operator rights to Betway and FanDuel this year and left prediction markets unsold.

Why Now

The money landed on 31 August and the first CFO on 10 September; the NFL season and the November midterms are the volume events the marketing rebuild was hired for. Teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter, and the United States GP in Austin in late October is the announcement stage.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac F1 Team is the one roster with no betting, casino, exchange or trading brand: Williams carries Betway and Jackpot City, Alpine Modo Casino, McLaren OKX and Allwyn, Aston Martin Coinbase and Public. Its owner group, TWG Global, also controls the Los Angeles Dodgers, whose league made Polymarket its exclusive exchange. American, debut season, and a stated preference for fewer, bigger partners.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B, halo with mechanics. Commercially: a $21B partner at the reported premium tier, a young US audience, Austin and Miami hospitality. Nothing reaches the car.

Risks & Counters

THE OWNER AND THE INVESTOR  TWG Global's Mark Walter is under a DOJ and SEC investigation he denies; 1789 Capital, co-founded by Donald Trump Jr., leads the round while a House Oversight insider-trading probe is open. Counter: Put both on the table in the first call and contract reputational exit rights both ways.
REGULATORS AND THE CATEGORY  Polymarket US is enjoined in Nevada with cease-and-desists in four more states, and F1 holds Betway and FanDuel as Official Betting Operators: is a prediction market a separable category? Counter: F1's betting deals are regional and non-exclusive; MLB and the NHL run both categories side by side. Clear the definition with Cadillac's counsel first.
