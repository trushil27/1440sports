The Case

TechCrunch reported on 11 May that Helsing was in the final close of a $1.2B round led by Dragoneer and co-led by Lightspeed at an $18B valuation, up from the ~€12B set by its €600M Series D under Daniel Ek's Prima Materia in June 2025. Order flow has arrived to match the capital: on 25 February the Bundestag budget committee approved a €269M initial HX-2 contract with a framework of up to €1.46B over seven years, inside a 4,300-drone first tranche. The round later closed at $1.8B on 13 July.

Defence is not new to the paddock: Boeing sits on Alpine's roster and BAE Systems shares fast-jet technology with Williams. What is new is a European defence company with venture capital at this scale and a hiring problem, competing with the primes and the hyperscalers for the same engineers. Formula 1 is Europe's most-watched engineering shop floor, and no defence-technology company has a seat in it.

Why Now

The round was reported in final close on 11 May and closed at $1.8B in July; the HX-2 contract was approved on 25 February. Budget and brand motive crest in the same year, and Audi's first Formula 1 season leaves a German partner roster still being written.

WHY AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM

Audi's 2026 entry is a German works programme: power units from Neuburg an der Donau in Bavaria, chassis from Hinwil and a technology centre in Bicester. Its partner roster carries Revolut as title partner and Extreme Networks, NinjaOne, ElevenLabs and JigSpace on the technology side; there is no defence, aerospace or security partner. A Bavarian engineering pairing in Audi's debut season is a story both boards can tell.

VALUE TO AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with concrete mechanics. Talent: Helsing and Audi's Neuburg, Hinwil and Bicester sites recruit the same software, autonomy and hardware engineers; a joint graduate and secondment programme is a real, fundable workstream. Access: paddock hospitality for ministry and NATO-market delegations at the European rounds. Brand: a made-in-Europe engineering narrative on a German car in its first season.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Defence Technology Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the HX-2 production ramp and a likely listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year. Scope: sidepod or engine-cover placement, a joint engineering-talent programme across Neuburg, Hinwil and Bicester, European-round hospitality for government customers, and a recruitment content series.

Risks & Counters

CATEGORY SENSITIVITY  A munitions maker on a car is a board-level decision for Audi and the VW Group; the Boeing and BAE precedents are aerospace, not weapons. Counter: Frame it around software, autonomy and talent; agree branding guidelines before the term sheet.
