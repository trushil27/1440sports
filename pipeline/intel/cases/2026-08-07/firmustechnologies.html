The Case

The round is company-announced: $2B of equity from Coatue, NVIDIA, funds managed by Blackstone and Jane Street, taking new equity raised in the past year past $3B and the valuation from $5.5B in April to more than $10.5B. Beneath it sits a $10B debt facility led by Blackstone and Coatue, signed in February, for Project Southgate: liquid-cooled AI Factories in Tasmania, 36,000 NVIDIA GPUs at the Launceston site, and a 170,000-GPU campus in Batam, Indonesia, from the first quarter of 2027, against which Firmus reports up to $30B of committed revenue over six years.

The identity moment is the listing. Firmus and its advisers are working towards an ASX float in the final quarter of 2026 (Wilson Asset Management, Capital Brief), which at the reported size would rank among the largest technology listings in Australian history. A company about to sell itself to Australian institutions and retail investors needs a brand beyond data-centre engineers, and the local precedent is set: Atlassian, Airwallex and Airtasker all bought Formula 1 for that reason.

Why Now

The round closed on 7 August and the float is targeted for the fourth quarter, so the listing's brand budget is being set now. The Singapore GP in October sits an hour from Batam, and the 2027 season opens with the Australian GP in Melbourne, the home market for the offer.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams is the Australian car on the grid: Atlassian, founded in Sydney, became title partner in 2025 in the largest deal in the team's history, and the roster around it is data-native: VAST for the data platform, Keeper for identity, Claude as Official Thinking Partner. The sponsor table shows no cloud, GPU or AI-compute partner at Williams; CoreWeave holds that lane at Aston Martin Aramco, Core Scientific and TWG AI at Cadillac. Two Sydney companies on one car is a story the ASX will read.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Williams' development outside the FIA aerodynamic-testing limits is GPU-bound: machine-learning surrogates, driver-in-loop and strategy simulation, telemetry analytics between sessions. Firmus supplies that capacity from liquid-cooled NVIDIA factories it says use 33% less energy and 99% less water, on demand and outside cost-cap capital. In return: Official AI Factory Partner designation, livery presence, Singapore and Melbourne hospitality for its investor and customer audience.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Factory Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the listing and the Batam ramp. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in compute. Scope: sidepod placement, naming of the team's compute environment, hospitality at the Australian and Singapore rounds, joint engineering content.
