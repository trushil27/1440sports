The Case

On 10 June 2026 Eaton signed definitive agreements to separate its Mobility business and combine it with Dana in a Reverse Morris Trust valued at about $5.1B. Eaton shareholders keep at least 50.1% of the combined company, Eaton receives roughly $1.1B in cash, and closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027. The vehicle heritage leaves; intelligent power stays. First-quarter sales were a record $7.5B, up 17%, Electrical Americas reached $3.6B, up 20%, data-centre orders rose about 240% and organic growth guidance went to 10%.

The identity reset arrives with money and a story: 2025 sales were a record $27.4B, and in March Eaton and NVIDIA unveiled the Beam Rubin DSX platform for 800-volt AI factories. Every F1 factory, wind tunnel and garage is a power problem, and from 2026 the cars draw about half their output electrically. The electrical-equipment category on the grid is held by two European rivals, Siemens and Schneider Electric; the American incumbent is absent.

Why Now

The Dana agreement was signed on 10 June and closes in the first quarter of 2027: the window in which Eaton decides what the remaining company stands for. Teams close 2027 rosters in Q4, and the United States GP (Austin) and Las Vegas GP sit inside it.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is the first American works entry in a generation: General Motors and TWG Motorsports, a $200M, 400,000 sq ft headquarters under construction in Fishers, Indiana, a power-unit facility in Concord, North Carolina, and a European base at Silverstone. Its inaugural roster is Claro, Core Scientific, IFS, Jim Beam, Tenneco, Tommy Hilfiger and TWG AI: no electrical, power or energy brand. Eaton is the US incumbent in switchgear and UPS; a new campus is specified once.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Three Cadillac sites are being fitted out at once. Eaton supplies the medium-voltage switchgear, power distribution, UPS and circuit protection for Fishers, Concord and Silverstone, with data-centre-grade monitoring across dyno rooms and simulators. Trackside, Eaton power-quality and backup kit runs the garage and pit wall. In return: Official Power Management Partner, engine-cover or halo placement, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for Eaton's utility and data-centre buyers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Power Management Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, bridging the Dana closing and the first seasons of the pure-play Eaton. Estimated $5-8M a year, part in equipment and engineering. Scope: campus and trackside power infrastructure, engine-cover or halo branding, US-race hospitality, engineering content from the Fishers build.

Risks & Counters
