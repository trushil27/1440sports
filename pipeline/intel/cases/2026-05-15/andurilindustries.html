The Case

On 13 May 2026 Anduril announced a $5B Series H led by Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz at a $61B valuation, double the $30.5B set in June 2025, taking total funding past $11B over eight rounds. The demand behind it is public: the US Army awarded a ten-year enterprise contract with a $20B ceiling on 13 March 2026, and Arsenal-1, a $1B factory in Columbus, Ohio, targets production from July. Anduril has bought motorsport already: Official Defense Partner of NASCAR, title sponsor of June's San Diego weekend at Naval Base Coronado, and Hendrick's William Byron through 2028.

That NASCAR programme is the tell. A company that put a Cup Series event on a naval base to recruit engineers and tell a rebuild-the-arsenal story has a template Formula 1 can scale: three US rounds, a global engineering audience and a listing expected in 2027. General Motors is the bridge. GM Defense signed a teaming agreement with Anduril in October 2023 on autonomy and electrification, and Cadillac is GM's works team.

Why Now

The round closed on 13 May; the NASCAR debut at Coronado is in June and Arsenal-1 starts production in July, so budget, motorsport template and manufacturing story crest together. The United States GP in Austin (October) and the Las Vegas GP (November) fall inside the window; 2027 rosters close in the fourth quarter.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is General Motors' American works entry, in its first season with a deliberately small roster: TWG AI, Core Scientific, IFS, Tenneco, Jim Beam, Tommy Hilfiger, Claro. No aerospace, defence or autonomy partner is on it, and the parent already works with Anduril through GM Defense, so the introduction is warm on both sides. Three US rounds give a Costa Mesa brand home stages, and a debut team prices entry below the established grid.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with concrete mechanics. TWG AI holds Cadillac's exclusive AI lane, so this is not a software workstream. It is: livery and garage presence at Miami, Austin and Las Vegas; engineering-recruitment content, the job Anduril's marketing chief says marketing does, filmed at Arsenal-1 and the team's Indiana headquarters; warfighter-family programmes carried over from the Hendrick deal; hospitality for Pentagon, Congressional and allied guests at the US rounds.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Defence Technology Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, bridging the Hendrick term and a probable listing. Estimated $8-12M a year, the top of Cadillac's partner tier. Scope: engine-cover or rear-wing placement, US-round hospitality, recruitment and manufacturing content, a joint GM Defense and Cadillac engineering story.

Risks & Counters
