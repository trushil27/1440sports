The Case

Hightouch announced on 29 April that it had raised $150M in a Series D led by Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Bain Capital Ventures at a $2.75B valuation, with Iconiq, Sapphire Ventures, Amplify, Y Combinator and TD7, The Trade Desk's venture arm, participating; total funding is about $322M. Hightouch says revenue has grown more than 100% in each of the past two years, and names Domino's, PetSmart, DraftKings, Ramp and Whoop among customers. Its Series C, $80M at $1.2B led by Sapphire, closed in February 2025.

The product is a marketing data layer that reads a company's warehouse and pushes audiences to advertising, email and CRM tools, with AI agents deciding what each customer sees next. It is bought by marketing leaders, which makes the paddock a sales floor, not a billboard. Gartner named Hightouch a Leader in its 2025 Magic Quadrant for customer data platforms in January, and no team on the grid carries a partner in the category.

Why Now

The round was announced on 29 April; budgets reset in the quarter after a raise, and Hightouch is spending on category leadership. The European rounds from Monaco, then the US GP and Las Vegas, fall inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY MCLAREN MASTERCARD F1 TEAM

McLaren carries the longest technology roster in the sponsor table, and its fan-data lane has just opened: Salesforce, whose platform ran McLaren's fan-relationship programme, has departed, while Google Cloud has joined. Alteryx and Splunk cover analytics and observability; nothing on the roster activates fan data into media, email and the store. Hightouch fills that slot, with a warehouse-side partner already in place.

VALUE TO MCLAREN MASTERCARD F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Hightouch as the activation layer on McLaren's fan data: audiences built from ticketing, store, app and content behaviour in the team's warehouse, pushed to paid media, email and the McLaren store, with AI decisioning choosing the next offer for each fan. The team gets a measured lift in fan revenue; Hightouch gets a reference customer and a paddock full of chief marketing officers at the US GP and Las Vegas.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Marketing Data Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the next raise and a probable listing window. Estimated $2-4M a year, part in kind via platform licence and services. Scope: mirror or engine-cover placement, a fan-data activation programme with McLaren's digital team, CMO hospitality at the US rounds, and a co-authored annual fan-data report.
