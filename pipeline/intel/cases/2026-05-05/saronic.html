The Case

On 31 March 2026 Saronic announced a $1.75B Series D led by Kleiner Perkins at a $9.25B post-money valuation, with Advent International, Bessemer Venture Partners, DFJ Growth and BAM Elevate joining 8VC, Caffeinated Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Elad Gil and Franklin Templeton; a $600M Series C had valued it at $4B in 2025. In December the US Navy awarded a $392M production contract for its 24-foot Corsair, prototype to production in under 12 months. Franklin, Louisiana is adding 300,000 square feet and 1,500 jobs; Port Alpha targets 20-plus vessels a year by 2027. AMERICAN SHIPBUILDING, REBUILT FROM TEXAS.

No defence, maritime-autonomy or shipbuilding brand sits on the Formula 1 grid; the nearest adjacencies are Boeing at Alpine and Zoox at Williams, and neither conflicts. The question is not a category clash but which team's identity a US Navy supplier can stand behind. Two teams are American: one a privateer built around machine tools, the other a General Motors works entry in its first season with a manufacturing campus rising in Indiana.

Why Now

The Series D closed on 31 March and the Port Alpha site decision, with its governors and senators, is the next public act. Cadillac settles its 2027 roster between the summer break and the United States GP in Austin, Saronic's home city; a deal agreed by summer is announced at home.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is the American works team, in its first season with an inaugural roster: TWG AI, Claro, Core Scientific, IFS, Jim Beam, Tenneco, Tommy Hilfiger, Pirelli and Alpinestars. It has no industrial-technology, defence or autonomy partner. Its cars are engineered at Silverstone while a campus rises in Fishers, Indiana; Saronic is building Port Alpha on the same reindustrialisation story. Austin is the team's home race and Saronic's home city, and a Navy supplier belongs beside the American flag-bearer.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with mechanics. Saronic's constraint is people: 1,500 hires in Louisiana, 10,000 planned at Port Alpha, and autonomy engineers who could work anywhere. A Formula 1 partnership is a recruiting platform: engineer exchanges between Cadillac's simulation group and Saronic's autonomy team, campus activations around Austin, the team's manufacturing story told beside Saronic's. Cadillac gains an American industrial partner with a national-security audience for its US hospitality, and content from a shipyard building at Formula 1 pace.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Autonomy and Shipbuilding Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, through the Port Alpha build-out. Estimated $4-6M a year. Scope: sidepod placement, Austin, Miami and Las Vegas hospitality for Navy, congressional and state guests, an engineering-exchange programme, and co-produced content on building at speed.
