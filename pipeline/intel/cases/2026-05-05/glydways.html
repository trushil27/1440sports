The Case

On 14 April 2026 Glydways announced a $170M Series C co-led by Suzuki Motor Corporation, ACS Group and Khosla Ventures, with Mitsui Chemicals, Gates Frontier and new investor Obayashi Corporation; total funding exceeds $250M, and Bloomberg reported the round at almost $700M with talks for $250M more at $1B or above. The money is for going public in the literal sense: the South Metro Atlanta system broke ground in February and opens in December 2026, Dubai's RTA signed for a 2.8km pilot route, and Abu Dhabi's investment office is assessing a deployment. THE FIRST PASSENGERS RIDE THIS YEAR.

No autonomous-transit or mobility-network brand sits anywhere on the Formula E grid; the only autonomous-vehicle partner in either series is Zoox, at Williams in Formula 1. Formula E's works teams belong to carmakers, which matters when Suzuki co-led the round: Nissan, Porsche, Jaguar, Citroën, Cupra, DS and Mahindra each carry a rival badge. That leaves the independents, and one of them is American, with Miami and Austin on its next season's calendar.

Why Now

The Series C closed on 14 April and public passengers board in Atlanta in December. Between those dates sit the Tokyo E-Prix on 25-26 July, home race for Suzuki, Obayashi and Mitsui Chemicals, and Formula E's Season 13 roster window. The GEN4 era opens in December, with Miami and Austin following in February 2027.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti is the American team in Formula E, and its roster has no mobility or autonomy partner: Andretti Global, TWG AI, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo, with Porsche power until Season 12 ends and Nissan from Season 13. As an independent it carries no carmaker badge to clash with Suzuki's. Its next season brings the Austin E-Prix and Miami in February 2027, the closest Formula E gets to Glydways' Atlanta and New York riders, and the paddock is where city transport authorities are hosted.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with mechanics. Glydways sells to cities, so the deliverable is the audience: transport authorities and mayors hosted at Miami, Austin and Tokyo, where Formula E races on their streets. A branded pod on the E-Prix fan concourse is a working demonstration, not a logo. Andretti gains a technology partner whose story, driverless electric transit, is Formula E's own pitch to host cities, plus content from the Atlanta line's first public rides.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Mobility Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, tracking public operations from 2027. Estimated $1-2M a year, scaled to a Series C business. Scope: livery and driver-suit placement, a pod demonstration at Miami and Austin, city-official hospitality at Tokyo and Jeddah, and joint content around the Atlanta opening.
