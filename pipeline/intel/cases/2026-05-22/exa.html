The Case

On 20 May Exa announced a $250M Series C led by Andreessen Horowitz at a $2.2B valuation, confirmed by Bloomberg the same day. It follows an $85M Series B led by Benchmark at $700M in September 2025 and a $22M Series A led by Lightspeed in July 2024. Exa says more than 5,000 companies and 400,000 developers use its search API, Cursor, Cognition and HubSpot among them. The money funds next-generation models, infrastructure for hundreds of thousands of searches a second, and a go-to-market build-out under Marcus Holm, joining as CRO from LaunchDarkly.

AI brands have moved onto the grid quickly: Claude races on the Williams, Cognition joined Aston Martin Aramco, Meta AI sits at Mercedes and TWG AI at Cadillac. Exa is the search layer beneath those agents, and the companies it positions against are already on cars, Google Cloud at McLaren and Microsoft at Mercedes and Alpine. A challenger with a $250M war chest and a new revenue chief has a story to tell against them.

Why Now

The round was announced on 20 May; a first sports partnership is a decision for the quarter after a raise, when a new CRO is setting the brand budget. Monaco GP (7 June) and the British GP (5 July) fall inside the window, and 2027 rosters close in the autumn.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams runs the most software-native roster on the grid: Atlassian as title partner, VAST Data for the data platform, Keeper for identity, Airia and Brillio, and since February Claude as Official Thinking Partner inside engineering and strategy. No search or retrieval partner sits on the car. Exa is a native Claude connector, so the introduction runs through a partnership Williams already trusts, and the product plugs into a workflow the team already uses.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Williams engineers, strategists and commercial staff already work with Claude; Exa gives those agents live web retrieval: regulation and technical-directive monitoring, supplier and parts discovery, rival upgrade tracking from open sources, and media and prospect research through Websets. It is a lighter workstream than compute. In return: Official AI Search Partner designation, livery presence and British GP hospitality for Exa's developer audience.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Search Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the go-to-market build-out and any listing window. Estimated $2-4M a year, part-payable in API credits for the team's agents. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, naming of the team's research-agent stack, British GP hospitality, developer content with the Claude programme.
