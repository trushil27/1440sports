The Case

Also spun out of Rivian in March 2025 with $105M from Rivian and Eclipse after three years inside the carmaker as Project Inder. Bloomberg reported Greenoaks' $200M at a $1B valuation in July 2025; the Series C was announced closed on 31 March 2026 with DoorDash joining alongside Prysm Capital, a multi-year agreement to build autonomous last-mile delivery vehicles, and DoorDash co-founder Stanley Tang as board observer. Total funding is $305M. The first product, the $4,500 TM-B e-bike revealed in October 2025, starts US deliveries this spring; about 350 staff work in Palo Alto, Seattle and Ghent.

The category precedent landed this week: Specialized became Audi Revolut F1 Team's official bicycle and e-bike partner for 2026 and 2027, supplying the fleet the team rides for daily mobility, training and recovery. Chris Yu spent more than ten years at Specialized as chief product and technology officer before co-founding Also. The template exists, the man who built the incumbent's product now runs the challenger, and the challenger has a fresh $200M to spend.

Why Now

Specialized's Audi deal was announced on 21 May, the day before this brief, and makes e-bikes a live F1 category. Also's US deliveries begin this spring on a fresh $200M. Monaco GP (7 June) and the British GP (5 July) fall inside the window; the US GP and Las Vegas follow in the autumn.

WHY VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS

Racing Bulls carries Visa and Cash App at the front, with Yugo, Student.com, Airtasker and Hugo behind them: a roster built for a younger consumer audience, the buyer of a $4,500 design-led e-bike. No bicycle, e-bike or mobility partner sits on the team. Ford supplies the power unit from 2026, a supply agreement rather than a brand exclusivity, so a Rivian-born partner is a conversation, not a bar. It is the grid's natural first-timer stage: a team-partner price and 24 rounds of paddock use.

VALUE TO VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. The Specialized template, run by the team that built it: a TM-B fleet for track rides, paddock commutes and driver recovery sessions at every round, the removable battery doubling as a 240W power bank for trackside crews, and the cargo quad for paddock logistics once in production. In return: Official Mobility Partner designation, livery and kit presence, Monaco and Las Vegas hospitality, and the drivers on the bikes on camera.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Mobility Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the TM-B ramp, the DoorDash delivery programme and a probable further raise. Estimated $1-2M a year plus product, with the fleet supplied in kind. Scope: rear-wing or halo placement, a team-liveried TM-B edition, Monaco and Las Vegas hospitality, driver content on the bikes.

Risks & Counters
