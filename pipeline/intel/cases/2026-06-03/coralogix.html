The Case

Coralogix announced the $200M Series F on 3 June 2026: co-led by Advent, CPPIB and Greenfield, with Brighton Park Capital participating, taking total funding to $550M. TechCrunch and Calcalist put the valuation at $1.6B, up from the $1B-plus mark of the $115M Series E led by NewView Capital in June 2025. TechCrunch reports more than 5,000 customers, revenue growing over 60% year on year and around 30 accounts above $1M a year, with over half of enterprise customers running its AI agent, Olly. Founded in Israel in 2015, it is headquartered in Boston.

Observability is already priced on the grid: Splunk sits on McLaren through Cisco and Dynatrace on Visa Cash App Racing Bulls, both selling monitoring of the kind of data estate a race team runs. Coralogix is the challenger those incumbents are measured against in analyst comparisons, and a round of this size is when a developer-known brand goes looking for a wider enterprise audience: the CIOs and platform leaders who fill F1 hospitality suites.

Why Now

The Series F closed on 3 June; the quarter after a raise is when brand budgets are set. The British GP at Silverstone on 5 July, Williams' home race, falls inside the six-to-ten-week window, and teams shape their 2027 partner rosters through the summer break.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams carries the most data-led roster in the paddock: Atlassian as title partner, VAST on the data platform, Keeper on identity and Claude as Official Thinking Partner. No partner monitors the estate itself, so the observability lane at Grove is open. McLaren holds Splunk through Cisco, Racing Bulls hold Dynatrace, and IBM at Ferrari and ServiceNow at Aston Martin sell observability products. Boston adds an American story for Miami, Austin and Las Vegas.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A Formula 1 team is a distributed data business: trackside telemetry streamed to the factory, simulation and CFD queues, pit-wall applications and a fan-facing digital estate, all producing logs, metrics and traces around the clock. Coralogix's in-stream analytics monitors that pipeline without indexing every byte; its agent, Olly, investigates incidents in plain language. In return: a named partnership, livery presence and race-weekend hospitality for its enterprise buyers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Observability Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the post-raise growth push and a possible listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in platform credits. Scope: sidepod or engine-cover placement, naming of the team's monitoring stack, Silverstone, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality, engineering content with the data partners.
