The Case

Eos, headquartered in Edison, New Jersey, makes Znyth aqueous-zinc and Z3 zinc-bromine batteries in Pennsylvania for three-to-twelve-hour storage. Second-quarter revenue was $68.8M, up 351% on 207% more cube deliveries; backlog rose 25% in the quarter to a record $807M; full-year guidance was tightened to $300M-$350M. The company is loss-making: the quarter's net loss was $275.7M and the gross loss $48.8M. A $303.5M loan guaranteed by the US Department of Energy, closed in December 2024, funds expansion to 8 GWh of capacity by 2027, and Cerberus is a strategic investor.

The trigger is a person, not a round. On 24 August the company put its Chief Administration Officer in charge of the whole commercial path, from first customer engagement to revenue, and moved marketing and communications under her; the previous commercial chief leaves on 20 October. A company that has just centralised its market voice, with shareholders watching the price fall, has a reason to be seen. Formula E is the electric stage that fits.

Why Now

The new Chief Commercial Officer owns marketing from 24 August and the transition completes on 20 October; the 2027 plan is being written now. Season 13 opens with the Jeddah E-Prix on 18-19 December, and the Austin E-Prix on 6 February and the Miami E-Prix on 20 February 2027 are the American rounds.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti Formula E is the American team on the grid, owned by TWG Motorsports, running Nissan powertrains from Season 13 with TWG AI, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo as partners and no energy or storage brand on the car. An American-made storage company on the American team gives both a home story at Austin and Miami, and the energy lane is open in the sponsor table. Envision, the other natural fit, is ruled out by its parent's own energy business.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Energy Storage Partner at associate level. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, sized to the balance sheet: estimated $1-2M a year with a review clause tied to the company's gross-margin turn. Scope: rear-wing placement, Austin and Miami hospitality for utility and developer customers, and a trackside storage showcase built from the company's own cubes.

Primary Decision-Maker

Michelle Buczkowski
Chief Commercial Officer, Eos Energy Enterprises · verified
Buczkowski became Chief Commercial Officer on 24 August 2026, moving from Chief Administration Officer; sales, business development, government affairs, marketing and communications report to her. Path: Joe Mastrangelo, CEO since 2019; Eric Javidi, CFO. No chief marketing officer is listed; marketing sits under her.

Opening Angle

Lead with the home rounds.
“Michelle, marketing now reports to you, and Formula E's American rounds land in Austin and Miami in February. Andretti is the American team with no storage partner. 25 minutes to see whether a small three-year entry belongs in your 2027 plan?”
