The Case

Druva was founded in 2008, is headquartered in Santa Clara with R&D in Pune, and has raised $475M, the last $147M in a Series H led by CDPQ in April 2021 at a valuation above $2B. It passed $200M of SaaS annual recurring revenue in August 2023, with more than 5,000 customers and over six billion backups a year. On 28 July 2026 it hired Anthony Anzevino from Kaseya, where he was Chief Revenue Officer, as Chief Commercial and Partner Officer; his career runs through Commvault, Veeam, AWS, VMware and Dell EMC.

A new commercial owner writes his plan in the first quarter, and Druva's category has already been priced on the grid: Rubrik, its direct rival, joined McLaren for 2026. A sponsorship at Druva is now a two-seat conversation, the commercial chief who arrived in July and a chief marketing officer who came up through Commvault and Dell EMC. The audience is American enterprise IT, and the two United States races close the season.

Why Now

Anzevino started on 28 July; the first hundred days of a new commercial chief set the partner and marketing plan for 2027. The United States GP in Austin (late October) and the Las Vegas GP (November) are the two American rounds left in 2026, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac's inaugural roster is greenfield: IFS runs enterprise software, TWG AI is the exclusive AI partner, Core Scientific builds the team's primary US data hub in Indianapolis, and no partner covers backup, recovery or cyber-resilience; the lane is open in the team table. A new team building a data estate from scratch, with an American audience at Austin and Las Vegas, is where a US data-resilience company protects something real rather than lending a logo.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Druva's platform backs up and recovers Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce and cloud workloads with immutable copies; a new team's estate, from the Indianapolis data hub to the factory's engineering data, needs exactly that, with ransomware-recovery drills before the first race weekend. In return: Official Data Resilience Partner, engineering-content rights, and Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for Druva's enterprise buyers and channel partners.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Data Resilience Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the new commercial chief's plan and the team's build-out. Estimated $2-4M a year, part-payable in platform services. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, naming of the team's recovery environment, US-race hospitality, and content on protecting a race team's data.
