The Case

Corgi announced on 28 May a $106M Series B1 led by TCV at a $2.6B valuation, three weeks after the same investor led its $160M Series B at $1.3B on 6 May. Total funding stands at $378M for a company founded in 2024 that came out of stealth in January with $108M at a reported $630M. Founder-CEO Nico Laqua told TechCrunch the company was profitable last month and will expand from start-ups into trucking, small business and sports. It writes general liability, cyber and tech-and-AI liability as a full-stack carrier, not a broker.

Insurance is on the grid, but thinly. Orion180, a Florida homeowners insurer, has been on the Haas car since October 2024; Aon is at Ferrari as a broker. Fintech is crowded: Visa and Cash App at Racing Bulls, Revolut at Audi, Mobilize Financial Services at Alpine. Cadillac, the grid's new American entrant, carries none of it, and the executive who runs the team also runs an insurance group.

Why Now

Two rounds in three weeks doubled the valuation by 28 May, and brand budgets are set in the quarter after a raise. The British GP (3-5 July) falls inside the window, and the United States GP in Austin (23-25 October) is the natural announcement stage.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac's inaugural roster is TWG AI, IFS, Core Scientific, Tenneco, Claro, Jim Beam, Tommy Hilfiger, Pirelli and Alpinestars: no insurer and no financial-services brand. Its chief executive, Dan Towriss, also runs Group 1001, a US insurance and financial-services group, so an AI-native carrier is a product he can judge. The team is building a 450,000 sq ft base in Fishers, Indiana while racing from Silverstone; Corgi's start-up customers and Cadillac's American story meet at Austin.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A first-year team stands up US entities, a factory, supplier contracts and a growing data estate, with general liability, cyber and tech-and-AI liability exposure from day one. Corgi underwrites those lines in-house with models that quote in minutes, so it can cover the team's US operations, within its licensed footprint, and make the account its first sports reference. Cadillac gains a partner that pays partly in cover and brings founders to Austin.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Insurance Partner of the Cadillac F1 Team. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, with an option to start at Austin in 2026. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in underwritten cover. Scope: commercial-insurance exclusivity, sidepod or halo placement, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for founder customers, a content series on insuring a start-up team.

Risks & Counters

YOUNG BOOK  Corgi is two years old and its valuation runs far ahead of its premium base; a multi-year fee could look reckless to investors. Counter: Pay part of the fee in cover, tie the balance to premium milestones, keep year one at Austin scale.
REGULATED SPEND  A carrier's capital is watched by regulators and rating agencies; sponsorship can read as surplus leaving the balance sheet. Counter: Fund it from the growth capital just raised, with the sports vertical as the stated commercial purpose.
