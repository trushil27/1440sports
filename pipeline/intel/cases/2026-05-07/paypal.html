The Case

On 29 April PayPal announced a reorganisation into three operating units, Checkout Solutions & PayPal, Consumer Financial Services & Venmo, and Payment Services & Crypto, effective 2 June, with Antonio Lucio joining from HP as Chief Marketing & Corporate Affairs Officer and Diego Scotti departing. It is the first structural move by Enrique Lores, President and CEO since 1 March after six years running HP. The base is large: 2025 revenue of $33.2B, total payment volume of $1.79T and net income of $5.2B, reported on 3 February.

The pair have done this before. HP announced its multi-year title partnership with Ferrari on 24 April 2024 with Lores as CEO and Lucio as chief marketing and corporate affairs officer; the HP livery debuted at the Miami GP weeks later. PayPal already buys sports, a Phoenix Suns jersey patch since 2018 and the PayPal Park naming rights in San Jose, yet has nothing in Formula 1 while Visa, Mastercard and American Express do.

Why Now

Lucio takes the chair on 2 June with a mandate to rebuild the brand under a new CEO; first-hundred-day platform decisions are made before the summer. The European rounds from Monaco, then the US GP and Las Vegas, sit inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY SCUDERIA FERRARI HP

Ferrari's roster has no payments partner: the finance names are UniCredit, BingX, Vantage and AON, and HP is the title partner that Lores and Lucio signed from the other side of the table. That relationship is the warm path: the two executives who put HP on the car know Maranello's commercial team, its hospitality and its e-commerce audience. Ferrari's global fan base and merchandise business give a checkout and wallet brand a real transaction surface.

VALUE TO SCUDERIA FERRARI HP  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. PayPal and Venmo as the checkout and wallet layer for Ferrari's fan commerce: the online store, ticketing and hospitality bookings, Esports and Driver Academy programmes, with Pay Later at checkout for high-ticket merchandise and Venmo for the US fan base at the US GP and Las Vegas. In return: Official Payments Partner designation, paddock hospitality for merchant and enterprise clients, and content alongside the HP programme.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Payments Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, matching Lucio's brand-rebuild horizon. Estimated $8-12M a year, part in kind via checkout integration. Scope: rear-wing endplate or halo placement, checkout and wallet integration across store and ticketing, US-round hospitality, and a co-branded Pay Later and Venmo campaign around the US rounds.
