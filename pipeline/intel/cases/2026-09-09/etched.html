WHY AUDI

Audi's F1 programme has bolted on ElevenLabs for voice AI but carries no rack-scale inference-compute partner, leaving trackside AI processing — strategy simulation, pit-wall modelling, race analytics — outside any named technical alliance. Audi's German engineering identity and Volkswagen Group's compute ambitions make an AI-silicon alliance a natural extension of its brand-technology narrative, not a bolt-on sponsorship. As a debut-season grid entrant, Audi has open technical-partner inventory that longer-established teams have already allocated.

VALUE TO AUDI  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

Etched's rack-scale inference silicon maps to Audi's race-weekend compute stack — strategy simulation, pit-wall decision modelling and live race analytics that currently run on generic cloud infrastructure. A trackside inference-rack deployment becomes a live demonstration surface for Etched's hardware under real-time, high-stakes load. Deployment surface: pit-wall strategy engine, factory simulation cluster, garage telemetry processing, broadcast-facing performance-analytics graphics.
