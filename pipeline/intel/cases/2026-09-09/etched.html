The Case

The round brought in Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Tiger Global, Bain Capital Ventures and Blackstone behind Jane Street, which took delivery of the first Sohu rack in July and is deploying it into its own workloads; the company reports more than $1B in customer contracts across frontier AI labs and clouds. It follows a $300M Series C at $10.3B in July led by Sequoia. A $500M Stripes-led raise at $5B closed in December 2025. Sohu is built on TSMC's 4-nanometre process; founders Gavin Uberti, Chris Zhu and Robert Wachen started the company in 2022.

Inference silicon has already bought a Formula 1 seat: Groq is on a McLaren, and eight AI partnerships were signed on the grid in the six months to May, with Anthropic at Williams, Oracle's strategy agent at Red Bull and TWG AI exclusive at Cadillac. Every one of those sells a model or a cloud; Etched sells the chip underneath, and it has a customer running it.

Why Now

Two price marks in a month, a first rack in a customer's hands and a $1B backlog to deliver: the company has to be known before the racks are. Teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter; Austin in late October, Mexico City and Las Vegas sit inside the window.

WHY AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM

Audi Revolut F1 Team is a debut works entry under Mattia Binotto, and its technical roster carries NinjaOne, Extreme Networks and ElevenLabs for voice AI, but no inference or compute partner: the lane is taken at Cadillac (TWG AI, exclusive), McLaren (Groq), Mercedes (AMD, Snapdragon, Meta AI), Aston Martin (Cognition, CoreWeave), Red Bull (Oracle) and Williams (Anthropic). A German engineering brand in its first season buys technology stories; a chip company with one customer needs one.

VALUE TO AUDI REVOLUT F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A, narrowly. Sohu runs transformer inference only, so the workstream is the team's language-model layer: race-engineering assistants, telemetry and document summarisation, and simulator and strategy agents built on transformers, on a rack at Hinwil and trackside. In return: Official Inference Partner designation, sidepod and garage presence, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality, and an engineering content series.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Inference Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, through Sohu's first production generation and its successor. Estimated $5-8M a year, part-payable in Sohu racks the team would otherwise lease as cloud inference. Scope: sidepod placement, naming of the team's inference cluster, Austin, Mexico City and Las Vegas hospitality, and a content series on the cost of a token.

Risks & Counters

TRANSFORMER-ONLY SILICON  Sohu runs one model family; a team's strategy and vision models need not be transformers, so the workstream is the language-model layer, not the pit wall. Counter: Sell that layer honestly and let Jane Street's deployment carry the proof.
A PRICE THAT DOUBLED IN A MONTH  Four times the valuation in eight months on one named paying customer; a re-rating that fast can reverse and freeze spend. Counter: Anchor on the $1B of contracts and the shipped rack, not the mark.
