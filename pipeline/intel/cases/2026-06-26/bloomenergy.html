The Case

On 13 April Bloom and Oracle announced a master services agreement under which Oracle intends to procure up to 2.8 GW of Bloom fuel-cell systems, 1.2 GW contracted. Two weeks later Bloom reported first-quarter revenue of $751.1M, up 130.4%, and raised 2026 guidance to $3.4-3.8B; total backlog is about $20B. American Electric Power exercised a $2.65B option for 900 MW in January, and Brookfield committed $5B in October 2025 to finance Bloom-powered AI data centres. A niche fuel-cell company has become the power supplier of the AI build-out, and its brand has not caught up.

The grid sells energy at scale, but every energy partner on it is a fuel or lubricant brand: Aramco at Aston Martin, Petronas at Mercedes, Shell at Ferrari, Eni at Alpine, Mobil 1 and Esso at Red Bull. No team carries an on-site power generation partner, even as factories add simulators, data halls and wind-tunnel hours that local grids were never sized for. Bloom's product is that: baseload power delivered in months, not years.

Why Now

Bloom's 2026 guidance of $3.4-3.8B implies roughly 80% growth, raised on 28 April; the budget and the motive to be known beyond energy circles crest together. Austin (late October) and Las Vegas (November) are the US stages left in 2026, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams is rebuilding Grove: a phased modernisation adds larger factory, technology and testing facilities. The roster carries Atlassian as title partner, Gulf in fuel, Duracell in consumer batteries, Komatsu, VAST and Keeper, but no power-generation or energy-infrastructure partner, so Official Power Partner is a clean category. Dorilton's investment since 2020 and a data-led revival under James Vowles give a US industrial brand a team that will use the product as well as show it.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Grove's expansion adds simulator, data-hall and wind-tunnel load. Bloom Energy Servers generate power on site from natural gas, biogas or hydrogen without combustion; Oracle took its first system in 55 days, and that speed applies to a factory too. New buildings come online without waiting on a grid upgrade, with a lower-carbon footprint the team can report. In return: Official Power Partner status, rear-wing placement, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for Bloom's customers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Power Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the Grove build-out and the first Oracle gigawatts. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-deliverable as installed Energy Server capacity at Grove. Scope: rear-wing or engine-cover placement, naming of the factory's power plant, US-race hospitality, content on powering the campus.
