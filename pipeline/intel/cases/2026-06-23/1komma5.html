The Case

On 17 June 1KOMMA5° announced, with European investors and banks, a financing programme of up to one billion euros so German homeowners can buy solar, storage, heat pumps and wallboxes on instalments of up to 25 years at 5.99%. Next day manager magazin reported IPO preparations, the CEO on a roadshow, and 2026 revenue projected around €660M with EBITDA profitability. The base is real: revenue of €520M in 2024, up from €450M, 2,500 staff, 170,000 systems installed, and €150M of pre-IPO financing led by CalSTRS in December 2024.

Formula E's team rosters carry energy majors and battery makers, not the home. Shell sits with Lola Yamaha ABT and owns sonnen, a direct rival in German home storage; TotalEnergies sits with DS Penske; Envision Group, a wind and battery group, owns Envision Racing; Castrol, a bp brand, is at Jaguar TCS Racing. No team carries a residential energy brand, yet Berlin, Zandvoort and Madrid are raced in countries where 1KOMMA5° sells.

Why Now

A pre-IPO consumer brand needs European awareness in the quarters before it lists, and the €1bn programme is its biggest customer push yet. Formula E's GEN4 era opens in Jeddah on 18 December; partner deals for the debut season close this autumn, and Berlin, Zandvoort and Madrid follow in 2027.

WHY MAHINDRA RACING

Mahindra Racing is a founding Formula E team, based in Banbury, that committed in November 2025 to race as a manufacturer through the GEN4 era to 2030. Its roster is the shortest on the grid: Mahindra & Mahindra as title and Tech Mahindra, no energy, battery or home-electrification brand. Unlike Porsche and Cupra Kiro, it has no group energy arm competing with Heartbeat AI, and it races Berlin, Zandvoort and Madrid, 1KOMMA5°'s markets.

VALUE TO MAHINDRA RACING  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo with mechanics. Heartbeat AI pools 40,000 networked home systems into Europe's largest residential virtual power plant; Formula E's energy management, harvesting and deploying a fixed battery over a race, is the same story at car scale. Mechanics: 1KOMMA5° on car and race suits at the European rounds; a Heartbeat-led energy series in Mahindra's race content; showroom activations in Berlin and Madrid on race weekends.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Home Energy Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15 (2026/27 to 2028/29), covering the GEN4 debut and the listing window. Estimated €1.5-2.5M a year in cash and media. Scope: car and race-suit placement, a Heartbeat AI content series, Berlin and Zandvoort hospitality, showroom activations.

Risks & Counters

HALO, NOT WORKSTREAM  A racing team does not buy home solar; the value is audience and story, and Mahindra has been rebuilding from the back of the field. Counter: Price it as media and hospitality; tie renewals to reach in Germany, Spain and the Netherlands.
IPO TIMING HAS SLIPPED  A 2026 listing was the hope when the pre-IPO financing closed in December 2024; a delayed IPO can freeze marketing spend. Counter: Structure year one as GEN4-launch activation with an IPO-year option.
