The Case

Also was spun out of Rivian in March 2025 by RJ Scaringe and Chris Yu, Rivian's former head of future programs and before that chief technology officer at Specialized. It has raised $455M in under two years. The $150M Series D announced on 19 August 2026, led by Prysm Capital with Eclipse, Greenoaks and MVP Ventures returning, funds autonomous development across several form factors for moving goods and passengers through cities.

The category has just been priced on a grid. Specialized became Audi's official bicycle and e-bike partner in Formula 1 in May 2026, supplying bikes for team track rides, and Porsche closed its own e-bike division the same month. Nothing of that kind sits anywhere on the Formula E grid, and the championship races where Also sells.

Why Now

The Series D closed on 19 August and the money is going into autonomy. Season 13 opens at the Jeddah E-Prix on 18-19 December; Berlin, Monaco, London and Madrid follow in May and June 2027, and teams close their rosters before the season starts.

WHY JAGUAR TCS RACING

Jaguar TCS Racing carries TCS as title partner, with Castrol, Wolfspeed, OpenText, Dow, Schaeffler, Chase and Reflo behind it: an engineering roster with no bicycle, e-bike or mobility brand anywhere on it. Jaguar is a premium marque rebuilding itself around electric design, which is the company a design-led e-bike wants to stand beside, and the London E-Prix on 29-30 May 2027 is the team's home round.

VALUE TO JAGUAR TCS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational, in the paddock rather than on the car. A TM-B fleet for crew movement across street circuits and driver track rides, the 808 Wh removable battery doubling as a USB-C power bank for trackside kit, and the TM-Q quad for paddock logistics. In return: Official Mobility Partner designation, livery and kit presence, London and Berlin hospitality, and drivers on the bikes on camera.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Mobility Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, so the term covers the autonomy programme reaching product. Estimated $1.5-3M a year, part-payable in vehicles and service. Scope: sidepod placement, team and crew bikes at every round, naming of the paddock mobility fleet, London and Berlin hospitality, and content on city electrification.

Chris Yu

Opening Angle

Risks & Counters

NOTHING FOR THE CAR  Also's series-hybrid drive, its removable pack and its consumer regen have no application to a GEN4 car; this is not an engineering partnership. Counter: Sell the paddock and the audience, not the powertrain. The Specialized deal at Audi is the same shape and it was bought.
SECOND TO SPECIALIZED  A rival claimed the category in Formula 1 first, so Also can look like the follower. Counter: Formula E is the unclaimed series and the one that races in Also's cities; first there beats second in F1.
