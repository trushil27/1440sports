The Case

Oura filed its public S-1 on 3 September for a Nasdaq listing under the ticker OURA, led by Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Allen & Company and Jefferies. The filing shows $1.21B of revenue for the nine months to 30 June 2026, up 74% from $697.6M, five million paid members against 2.5 million a year earlier, and $1.4B of trailing-twelve-month revenue at $59M of net profit. TechCrunch reported on 24 August that the listing could value the company above $16B, against $11B at last October's Series E.

Human performance is already a priced category in motorsport: WHOOP has joined Scuderia Ferrari's roster and Eight Sleep has joined Aston Martin Aramco's. Oura has been buying elite sport at pace - Official Wearable of Team USA and the LA28 Games in February, of U.S. Soccer and its 27 national teams in April, and of the US Open on a five-year USTA agreement. Motorsport is the one arena it does not hold.

Why Now

The S-1 went public on 3 September and pricing follows within weeks; a newly listed company sets its marketing plan in the first cycle after the bell. Formula E opens Season 13 at the Jeddah E-Prix in December, and the Austin E-Prix and the Miami E-Prix land in February 2027.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

The sponsor table shows no wearable or health-technology partner anywhere on the 2026 Formula E grid: the designation is open across the series, at Formula E prices rather than Formula 1 ones. Andretti Formula E is the American entry, owned by TWG Global. Its roster runs TWG AI, Quest Global, Crowe UK and Reflo. Season 13 gives it two home rounds, in Austin and Miami, inside the LA28 build-up Oura has already bought.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Season 13 runs 21 races in 13 cities; what limits drivers, engineers and travelling crew across it is circadian, not physical. Oura Ring puts readiness, sleep and heart-rate-variability data in front of the team's performance staff - the programme it runs for Team USA and ran for Red Bull Racing's pit crew from 2020. In return: Official Health Technology Partner status, livery and race-suit presence, US-round hospitality.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Health Technology Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, carrying through to the LA28 Games. Estimated $2-4M a year, part-payable in product. Scope: sidepod and race-suit placement, naming of the team's readiness programme, US-round hospitality, performance content.

Primary Decision-Maker

Doug Sweeny
Chief Marketing Officer, Oura Health · verified
Sweeny owns the sports portfolio that bought Team USA, U.S. Soccer and the US Open; he came from Levi's, Nest and One Medical. Path: Tom Hale, chief executive since March 2022, who took Momentive public in 2018, and Sean Brecker, CFO. No president is listed.

Risks & Counters

A LIVE OFFERING  The S-1 is on file and pricing is weeks away; new multi-year commitments are hard to sign while an offering is in the market. Counter: Use the window to agree heads of terms for announcement after the bell - the filing is the reason to call now.
A FULL SPORTS SHELF, AND A LAPSED F1 DEAL  Team USA, LA28, U.S. Soccer and a five-year USTA agreement are already bought, and the 2020 Red Bull Racing partnership is not on Red Bull's 2026 roster. Counter: Pitch motorsport as the gap in the shelf, not a fifth of the same; the lapsed deal proves a top team deployed the product, and Formula E costs a fraction.
