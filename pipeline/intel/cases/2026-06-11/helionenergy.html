The Case

On 4 June Helion announced a $465M Series G led by Thrive Capital at a $15.5B post-money valuation, with Lux Capital, Peak XV, BoxGroup and Bill Ford joining Lightspeed, SoftBank Vision Fund 2 and Good Ventures; total raised is $1.5B, nearly triple the $5.425B set by the $425M Series F in January 2025. The story is physical: Orion, the first fusion power plant, has been under construction in Malaga, Washington since July 2025 to supply Microsoft by 2028, and in February Polaris became the first private machine to demonstrate deuterium-tritium fusion at 150 million degrees.

Fusion is the definitive clean-energy story and Formula E is the championship built to prove clean mobility; pairing the two is the most on-message sustainability partnership on any grid, and Helion has never touched motorsport. The energy lane belongs to incumbents: Shell sits with Lola Yamaha ABT, TotalEnergies with DS Penske, Castrol with Jaguar. The counterweight: Helion is pre-revenue and pouring capital into reactors, so this is a measured brand and talent platform.

Why Now

The round closed on 4 June and Helion is at peak visibility as it moves from Polaris to a licensed construction site. Season 13, the GEN4 era, opens in Jeddah in December and the Austin E-Prix in February 2027 brings Formula E to a new US city; rosters are set now.

WHY ANDRETTI FORMULA E

Andretti is the American team on the grid, with TWG AI as primary partner, Quest Global on engineering, Crowe UK and Reflo alongside; it carries no energy, power or fuel partner, so a clean-energy lane is open outright. Envision Racing is owned by an energy group that would not cede its own story; Jaguar, DS Penske and Lola Yamaha ABT carry oil majors. Andretti gives Helion the two US rounds, Austin and Miami, and a parent that also owns the Cadillac F1 entry.

VALUE TO ANDRETTI FORMULA E  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, with concrete mechanics. Fusion does not power a Formula E car, so the substance is narrative, audience and talent: co-produced content pairing the first fusion plant with the first GEN4 season; hospitality at Austin and Miami for the utilities, hyperscalers and policymakers Helion must win; a joint STEM recruiting platform as Helion hires physicists and engineers. In return: Official Clean Energy Partner status, livery presence, driver access.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Clean Energy Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, spanning Orion's construction and its 2028 delivery to Microsoft. Estimated $1.5-3M a year, sized to a pre-revenue company and stepped up at first power. Scope: energy-category exclusivity, GEN4 content, Austin and Miami hospitality, a talent programme.
