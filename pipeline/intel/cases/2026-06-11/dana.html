The Case

Eaton and Dana signed a definitive agreement on 11 June 2026 to separate Eaton's Mobility Group and combine it with Dana in a Reverse Morris Trust, creating a company valued at more than $10 billion. The transaction values Mobility at about $5.1 billion and the combined business at roughly $11 billion of sales and $1.7 billion of adjusted EBITDA on a fully synergised pro forma 2026 basis, with $250 million of run-rate synergies inside 24 months. Eaton shareholders take at least 50.1 per cent. Closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

Dana closed 2025 with $7.5 billion of sales, $610 million of adjusted EBITDA and $85 million of net income after divesting Off-Highway, a supplier that has already chosen its ground in light and commercial vehicles. Byron Foster became chief executive on 1 July 2026. He joined Dana in 2021 to run global commercial, marketing and communications, so brand investment is his own discipline rather than a delegated one.

Why Now

The combination closes in the first quarter of 2027 and the new company puts its name into the market then. Formula E's GEN4 era begins the same season, and teams settle their partner tables through the autumn.

WHY MAHINDRA RACING

Mahindra Racing carries only Mahindra & Mahindra and Tech Mahindra on its partner table, so the driveline, e-propulsion and thermal lane sits open at a works team committed to the GEN4 era. Dana's India footprint is real: eighteen facilities, and a Pune plant building Dana TM4 electric motors, inverters and vehicle control units in the city Mahindra & Mahindra calls home. The commercial conversation starts before the sponsorship one.

VALUE TO MAHINDRA RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Dana TM4 builds electric motors, power inverters and control systems; Dana engineers thermal management, sealing and driveline for light and commercial vehicles. That gives a works team's powertrain group a real supplier bench to work against, with Mahindra's road-car programme behind it. In return Dana gets a named technical partnership, livery presence, and a paddock of manufacturers that is its own customer base.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Driveline & e-Propulsion Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, so the term opens with the combined company's first year and the GEN4 era together. Estimated $1.5-3M a year, part-payable in engineering support. Scope: sidepod and halo placement, an engineering programme with the powertrain group, and hospitality at the European and Asian rounds.

Risks & Counters

THE COMPANY CLOSES IN 2027  The combined business does not exist until the Reverse Morris Trust closes, expected in the first quarter of 2027 and subject to Dana shareholder approval. Counter: Contract now on a term starting at close, so the brand launch and Season 13 land together.
A SUPPLIER, NOT A CONSUMER BRAND  Dana sells to vehicle makers, so a livery has no retail job to do and the board will ask what it buys. Counter: Price it as customer marketing against a trade budget: the paddock is manufacturers, not shoppers.
