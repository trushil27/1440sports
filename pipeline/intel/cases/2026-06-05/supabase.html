The Case

Supabase announced on 4 June a $500M Series F led by GIC, with Accel, Y Combinator, Craft, Felicis, Peak XV and Coatue returning, a second investment from Stripe and a first from Salesforce Ventures. The post-money valuation is $10.5B, up from $5B at the October 2025 Series E and $2B at the April 2025 Series D; total capital raised now exceeds $1B. The company reports more than 250,000 customers and over 9 million developers, and says database launches grew 600% in the past year, with over 60% created by AI coding tools.

The grid has already priced developer infrastructure: Atlassian holds the Williams title, Oracle sits on the Red Bull, Google Cloud and Dell on the McLaren. What none of them owns is the open-source Postgres layer beneath the AI-coding boom. Supabase says Claude Code has been the largest single creator of new databases on its platform this year, and Claude already races on the Williams. That is a warm path, not a cold call.

Why Now

The round closed on 4 June; brand budgets reset in the quarter after a raise, and this is the largest in the company's history. The British GP on 5 July is Williams' home race and falls inside the window; teams settle their 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING

Williams carries the most developer-native roster in Formula 1: Atlassian as title partner, VAST for data infrastructure, Keeper for identity security and, since February, Claude as Official Thinking Partner. There is no database or backend-platform partner on the car. Supabase's own release credits Claude Code as the top creator of new databases this year, so the introduction runs through a relationship Williams already has.

VALUE TO ATLASSIAN WILLIAMS RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Supabase is the Postgres, authentication and realtime backend for the software Williams ships around the car: fan and ticketing apps, partner portals, and the internal tools engineers build with Claude. Multigres, previewed with the round, gives those systems scale on race weekends. In return: Official Developer Platform designation, sidepod placement, British GP hospitality and a co-produced engineering series with the Claude programme.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Developer Platform Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the next raise or a listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in platform credits. Scope: livery placement, developer activations at the British GP and the US rounds, content with the Claude programme, first refusal on a database category in 2030.

Risks & Counters

DEVELOPER-LED BRAND  Supabase grew on open source and community with no chief marketing officer; a Formula 1 spend is off-pattern. Counter: Frame it as developer reach, not a billboard: Claude, Atlassian and the paddock as a developer stage, paid partly in credits.
VALUATION PACE  A valuation doubled in eight months invites scrutiny; a visible sponsorship can read as excess to investors. Counter: Size the entry modestly, tie it to measurable developer sign-ups, and announce it alongside product news such as Multigres.
