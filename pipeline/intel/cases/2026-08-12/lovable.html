The Case

Lovable confirmed on 12 August a $400M Series C at $13.3B, led by Menlo Ventures and co-led by the Scaleup Europe Fund managed by EQT, with Balderton, Kaszek, Tencent and Regent joining and Accel, CapitalG, DST and Salesforce Ventures returning. The price has doubled since the $330M Series B at $6.6B on 18 December 2025. The company says revenue run rate passed $500M in June and should reach about $600M by end-August; more than 60 million projects have been built on the platform and its apps draw over 900 million visits a month.

The money is earmarked for scale and for America: Stockholm stays headquarters while offices open in San Francisco, New York and Boston, with about 450 hires planned this year and a chief marketing officer already based in San Francisco. A brand whose customers are founders and business owners in 150 countries now needs a stage those customers watch. It has never bought sport; the first seat is the one that defines the category.

Why Now

The round closed on 12 August and a US marketing build-out follows it. The Dutch and Italian GPs open the run-in; the United States GP in October and Las Vegas in November are the American stages, and teams settle 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY TGR HAAS F1 TEAM

Haas is the American team and the leanest on the grid: Toyota Gazoo Racing as title partner, Haas Automation selling to small manufacturers, Mphasis for digital services, Infobip for fan messaging, CommScope and Ruckus for networks. No software-creation, developer-platform or AI-application partner sits on the car. The category is open at a mid-grid price, in front of the Austin, Miami and Las Vegas crowds Lovable's US offices are opening to reach.

VALUE TO TGR HAAS F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A small team runs on tools its engineers never have time to build: hospitality guest lists, freight and travel trackers, partner microsites, driver-content pages. Lovable lets commercial and operations staff build them in days on the enterprise tier. At the US rounds, a build-your-race-app activation puts fans on the platform. In return: Official Software Creation Partner, sidepod or mirror placement, US-race hospitality, founder content.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Software Creation Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the US build-out and a probable listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in enterprise seats. Scope: sidepod or mirror placement, the team's internal-tools programme, Austin, Miami and Las Vegas hospitality, founder-and-driver content.

Risks & Counters
