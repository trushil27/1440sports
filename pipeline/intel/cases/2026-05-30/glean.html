The Case

CEO Arvind Jain announced on 28 May that ARR has passed $300M, five months after $200M and roughly three times the level of fifteen months earlier. The company raised $150M at $7.2B in June 2025 in a Series F led by Wellington Management, taking total funding to about $768M, and bought document-intelligence start-up Aryn on 11 March 2026. There has been no new round in 2026: this is a demand inflection, not a capital one, and the score says so. The buyers Glean must now win are enterprise CIOs, Formula E's paddock audience.

The enterprise-AI archetype is already on the grid. TWG AI is Official Artificial Intelligence partner of Andretti's programme; Sand Technologies backs Envision; Google Cloud is the championship's cloud partner. None of them sells a Work AI platform to end users, so a Glean-class brand would arrive first in its category. Mahindra Racing, committed as a manufacturer to the Gen4 era through 2030, pairs an Indian engineering parent with Glean's biggest office beyond America.

Why Now

The $300M mark landed on 28 May, and brand budgets reset in the quarter after a milestone like it. Season 12 closes at the London E-Prix on 15-16 August and the Gen4 era opens at the Jeddah E-Prix in December, so Season 13 rosters are settled over the summer.

WHY MAHINDRA RACING

Mahindra Racing's roster is Mahindra & Mahindra as parent and title and Tech Mahindra as IT-services partner; there is no enterprise-software brand. Its Gen4 manufacturer commitment through 2030, made on 26 November 2025, de-risks a three-year term. Glean's largest base outside the US is its Bengaluru centre of more than 450 people, and the Mahindra Group is the multi-business enterprise it sells into. Exclusivity is scoped to the Work AI platform, not to AI, since Tech Mahindra builds AI tooling through the team.

VALUE TO MAHINDRA RACING  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A Formula E team and its parent run on scattered knowledge: homologation and regulation documents, supplier and freight data, simulation reports, sponsor contracts. Glean indexes it all with permissions intact and puts an assistant and agents on top for engineers, operations and commercial staff. A team deployment, extended to chosen Mahindra Group functions, gives Mahindra measurable hours back and Glean a referenceable flagship inside one of India's largest industrial groups.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Work AI Partner. THREE YEARS, Season 13 to Season 15 (2026-27 to 2028-29), inside the Gen4 commitment. Estimated $2-3M a year, part-payable in seats. Scope: Work AI platform exclusivity, car and garage branding, a working team deployment, London and Bengaluru hospitality, engineering content.
