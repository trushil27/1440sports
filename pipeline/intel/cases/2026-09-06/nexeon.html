The Case

The National Wealth Fund committed £52.6 million on 31 August 2026, completing a £100 million round that also brought in the Korea Development Bank and Honda Xcelerator Ventures. Nexeon has raised roughly $400 million since 2006. The money is for UK expansion and commercialisation, and it lands on a business that is already shipping: the world's first commercial-scale silicon anode plant, at Gunsan in South Korea next to OCI's monosilane supply, came online in December 2025 against a binding long-term agreement with Panasonic.

Silicon anodes raise energy density and cut charging time, which is the specification the electric-vehicle argument still turns on. A state investor putting money behind that in Britain is a policy statement as much as a financing, and policy statements want an audience. Nexeon has a Japanese engineering centre, a Korean plant and a British head office; what it does not yet have is a public face.

Why Now

The round closed on 31 August. Formula E's GEN4 era opens at the Jeddah E-Prix in December, and the London E-Prix moves to Brands Hatch next season, giving a British company a home round again. Partner tables settle before the opener.

WHY JAGUAR TCS RACING

Jaguar TCS Racing runs with TCS as title partner and Castrol alongside a Jaguar powertrain, and carries no battery, cell-chemistry or materials partner. Jaguar is one of the manufacturers committed to GEN4, so the engineering audience is a works one. British company, British team, and from next season a British home round at Brands Hatch. For a materials business that sells to cell makers and the carmakers behind them, that is the right room.

VALUE TO JAGUAR TCS RACING  — MODE B (tech serves the team operation)

MODE B - halo, with concrete mechanics. Formula E's battery is a spec part, so Nexeon cannot supply the car and this brief will not pretend otherwise. What it can do: a named partnership on a works electric programme, garage and livery presence, hospitality for cell makers and carmakers at the Middle East and British rounds, joint fast-charge content with the team's engineers, and a recruiting platform for British battery talent.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Battery Materials Partner. THREE YEARS, Seasons 13-15, matching the GEN4 cycle and the ramp of the Gunsan plant. Estimated £0.8-1.5M a year, part-payable in engineering and content support. Scope: garage and sidepod placement, a technical-content series with the team, and hospitality at the European and Middle East rounds.

Risks & Counters

THE RACE BATTERY IS A SPEC PART  Formula E teams do not choose their cell chemistry, so a materials partner puts no component on the car and the technical story has to be told honestly. Counter: Sell the room, not the part: the paddock holds the cell makers and carmakers Nexeon needs in front of it.
THE MONEY IS EARMARKED  A round led by a state investor is committed to UK expansion and commercialisation, so a sponsorship line competes with plant spend. Counter: Price at Formula E team rates and stage it, weighting year one to content and recruitment rather than rights.
