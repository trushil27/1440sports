The Case

ThreatLocker announced a $190M Series F on 29 July 2026, led by Elephant, with D. E. Shaw Ventures and Arthur Ventures returning and Koch Disruptive Technologies new. It follows a $20M Series B, $100M Series C, $115M Series D (April 2024) and $60M Series E (April 2025): nearly $500M in total. SecurityWeek puts the pre-round valuation at $1.6B (reported); the company has not stated a new one and publishes no revenue. The platform, allowlisting and Ringfencing that block whatever is not explicitly permitted, protects more than 70,000 organisations. The round funds AI-risk controls and a Reading office.

Cybersecurity is the most-sold technology category on the grid: CrowdStrike at Mercedes, Bitdefender at Ferrari, Keeper at Williams, 1Password (Red Bull) and Cato Networks at Alpine. Each vendor bought the same thing, a paddock full of the enterprise buyers it sells to. ThreatLocker reaches the mid-market through managed service providers and has just committed to Europe. What it lacks is a brand stage for those partners and their customers.

Why Now

The round closed on 29 July and the Reading office is the first spend against it; marketing budgets are set in the quarter after a raise. The United States GP in October and the Las Vegas GP in November sit inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY CADILLAC F1 TEAM

Cadillac is the grid's first greenfield IT estate in a decade: a debut team operating from Fishers, Indiana, Charlotte and Silverstone, with Core Scientific building its Indianapolis data hub, IFS running operations, and no cybersecurity partner on the roster. The team wants fewer, bigger partners rather than a title sponsor. An Orlando company on an American car gets three US rounds a year at home; Cadillac gets a US security brand from launch.

VALUE TO CADILLAC F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A new team writes its security baseline from nothing: engineering workstations, machining and additive cells, the Indianapolis data hub, and trackside laptops that join a different network 24 weekends a year. ThreatLocker's allowlisting and Ringfencing deny anything not explicitly permitted, which is how a cost-capped team stops ransomware without a large security operations centre. Deployment across Fishers, Charlotte and Silverstone, a published case study, a race-weekend response desk.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Zero Trust Cybersecurity Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the Reading launch and any listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in product and services. Scope: halo or sidepod placement, deployment across the team's estate, US-race hospitality for MSP partners, factory content.
