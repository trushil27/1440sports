WHY AUDI

Audi's current partner roster includes ElevenLabs for voice AI but no broader AI-orchestration or agent-workflow partner — leaving the category that Wonderful occupies entirely open on the team's technical partner grid. As Audi scales its factory-to-track engineering pipeline ahead of its full works-team F1 entry, an orchestration layer coordinating simulation, strategy and factory workflows fills a documented gap in Audi's AI stack, positioned as the natural next partner beyond voice AI.

VALUE TO AUDI  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

Wonderful's agent-orchestration layer maps to Audi's engineering-ops stack: coordinating simulation workflows, strategy-model outputs and factory-floor data pipelines into a single automated decision layer. Deployment surface: race-engineering war-room dashboards, factory telemetry coordination, and pit-wall strategy tooling that synthesizes simulation and live-session data. The platform sits alongside ElevenLabs' voice interface, giving Audi's technical stack both a conversational layer and an orchestration layer.

Deal Architecture

A THREE YEARS entry-tier technical partnership, structured at roughly $8-12M annually, positions Wonderful as Audi's AI-orchestration partner through the 2027-2029 seasons. Activation anchors: Las Vegas Grand Prix digital showcase, Munich factory technology days, and paddock enterprise-briefing suites at flagship European rounds. Renewal checkpoints align with Audi's full works-team F1 entry, option to scale into a broader technical alliance.
