The Case

TwelveLabs announced on 1 July 2026 a $100M Series B co-led by NEA and NAVER Ventures, with Amazon, Radical Ventures, Korea Investment Partners, Index Ventures, Quadrille Capital and Red Bull Ventures, taking total funding to roughly $150M. Amazon made AWS its preferred cloud in a multi-year commitment built on Trainium chips. The money opens offices in New York and London alongside San Francisco and Seoul. Its Marengo and Pegasus models already serve NFL Media and Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment, which use them to find exact moments in game footage and package content faster.

Red Bull Ventures, the group's corporate venture arm launched in 2025 with a €200M fund and led by Nai-Tseng Chen, Red Bull's Global Head of Corporate Finance, calls TwelveLabs its most prominent investment. That is a structural introduction to both Red Bull teams, not a sponsorship. The grid's nearest incumbents are cloud and data platforms, which TwelveLabs runs on rather than against, so the category is a new line on a roster, not a displacement.

Why Now

The round closed on 1 July, and the New York and London offices it funds are a brand programme in the two cities where sports media buys technology. Teams set 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter; the United States GP (Austin, October) and Las Vegas GP (November) are the US stages.

WHY VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS

Racing Bulls is the grid's content team: CEO Peter Bayer told Sector that 60 per cent of fans rated its content excellent in F1's latest monitoring report, clear of the field, and its Creator Platform puts young fans to work inside the team. The technology roster is data and observability, Dynatrace and Confluent, with no video-AI partner. Red Bull Ventures already holds TwelveLabs equity, so the introduction exists, and the entry price fits a company that has raised $150M.

VALUE TO VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. Every session produces hours of onboard, garage and broadcast video nobody can search. TwelveLabs indexes it: the content team finds Lindblad's debut points or every Lawson pit stop from one camera in seconds, cuts clips while the session runs, and hands the Creator Platform tagged footage. Engineers get the same search over pit-stop and incident video. In return: a named partnership, livery presence, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official Video Intelligence Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, covering the New York and London build-out and the next raise. Estimated $1.5-2.5M a year, part-payable in platform usage. Scope: nose or endplate placement, naming of the video-search environment, Creator Platform integration, US hospitality.
