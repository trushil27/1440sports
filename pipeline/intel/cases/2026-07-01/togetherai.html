The Case

Together AI announced on 1 July 2026 an $800M Series C at an $8.3B post-money valuation, led by Aramco Ventures with Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, Emergence Capital, Nvidia, March Capital, Pegatron and S Ventures. Annual bookings topped $1.15B in the last quarter, with thousands of paying customers including Cursor, Cognition and Decagon. The $305M Series B at $3.3B, led by General Catalyst, closed only in February 2025. The company says its infrastructure footprint will grow roughly 50-fold over five years: for an infrastructure business, the brand-building moment arrives with the build-out.

The grid has already priced the neocloud archetype: CoreWeave became Aston Martin Aramco's Official AI Cloud Computing Partner in May 2025, Core Scientific and TWG AI opened on Cadillac's first roster, and Groq holds inference silicon on McLaren's. Aramco, the lead investor here, is both F1's Global Partner and the Aston Martin title, so the ecosystem knows the name; the seat, though, has to be one CoreWeave does not already hold.

Why Now

The round closed on 1 July; brand budgets are set in the quarter after a raise, and a 50-fold capacity plan needs a name enterprises recognise. Teams fix 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter; the United States GP (Austin, October) and Las Vegas GP (November) are the stages for a US announcement.

WHY TGR HAAS F1 TEAM

Haas is the American team, run from Kannapolis, North Carolina, racing in 2026 as TGR Haas F1 Team under Toyota Gazoo Racing's title partnership. Its technology roster is IT services, networking and communications: Mphasis, CommScope, Ruckus, Infobip. There is no cloud, GPU or AI-compute partner, so Official AI Cloud Partner is a clean category at the grid's most accessible price. A San Francisco developer brand gets a US home team, three US rounds and engineers who will use the product.

VALUE TO TGR HAAS F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. A team without a hyperscaler partner buys compute at list price. Together's self-service GPU clusters spin up in minutes for machine-learning surrogates, strategy and driver-in-loop simulation, and telemetry analytics between sessions; private open-model inference gives engineers assistants that keep team data in-house. In return: a named compute partnership, sidepod presence, and Miami, Austin and Las Vegas hospitality for Together's enterprise buyers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Cloud Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the 50-fold build-out and a probable listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in compute credits. Scope: sidepod or engine-cover placement, naming of the team's compute environment, US-round hospitality, engineering content.

Risks & Counters

CROWDED ARCHETYPE  CoreWeave, Core Scientific and Groq already hold grid positions; a fourth AI-cloud logo can read as a follower. Counter: Make Haas the first team whose compute story is open-source inference, and price it as a product deal.
INVESTOR TIES, NOT PARTNER TIES  Aramco's F1 money sits at Aston Martin, where CoreWeave holds the lane; the warm path leads to the ecosystem, not to Haas. Counter: Use Aramco for the F1 introduction; sell Haas on product use and the US rounds.
