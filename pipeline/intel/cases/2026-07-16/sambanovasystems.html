The Case

General Atlantic led the $1B first close, with Seligman Ventures, T. Rowe Price Associates and Capital Group, alongside BlackRock, Intel Capital, the Qatar Investment Authority, Battery Ventures and Vista Equity Partners; a second close was expected within weeks. Five months earlier SambaNova raised a $350M Series E with Intel after Bloomberg-reported takeover talks near $1.6B stalled, so the price has risen roughly fivefold since February. Proceeds expand production of the SN40L and the SN50, shipping in the second half of 2026 with SoftBank first. Liang says a 2027 IPO is under consideration (reported).

The grid has a precedent: McLaren named Groq an Official Partner in September 2025, putting an inference chip on the car from the Singapore GP with a pitch of fast, cost-efficient intelligence for analysis and development. SambaNova is the other US inference-silicon company at scale, now with JPMorganChase running its systems on-premises. It has no seat, and it is the one with a public-market clock starting to run.

Why Now

The first close landed on 8 July with a second expected within weeks, and the SN50 ships in the second half of 2026. The United States GP in October and the Las Vegas GP in November fall inside the window, and a 2027 listing makes 2027 the brand-build year.

WHY TGR HAAS F1 TEAM

Haas is the American team with no compute, AI or chip partner: Toyota Gazoo Racing as title and technical partner, Mphasis for digital services, CommScope and Ruckus for connectivity, no silicon. It is the lean privateer, so a partner that installs capability the team could not buy is worth more here than anywhere. Kannapolis and Banbury give SambaNova a US and a UK site; three home races put the brand in front of its buyers.

VALUE TO TGR HAAS F1 TEAM  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. An SN40L system at Banbury running open-weight models on the team's own data: strategy post-processing between sessions, telemetry anomaly detection, engineering-document and simulation copilots, none of it leaving the building. JPMorganChase chose SambaNova for that private, on-premises inference. The hardware can form part of the consideration for a cost-capped team; for SambaNova, a reference deployment to show every sovereign and enterprise buyer.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Inference Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the SN50 ramp and a possible 2027 listing. Estimated $4-7M a year, part-payable in systems and support. Scope: sidepod or rear-wing placement, naming of the team's inference environment, US-race hospitality, engineering content from Banbury.
