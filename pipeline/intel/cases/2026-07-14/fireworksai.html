The Case

Bloomberg reported on 27 May that Fireworks was in talks at $15B with Index co-leading; the round priced above that on 16 July at $17.5B, led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures and TCV, with Nvidia, Lightspeed, Evantic, Bessemer, Menlo, Insight, Ontario Teachers' and Lone Pine participating. It follows a $250M Series C at $4B in October 2025, a fourfold step in nine months; total funding is about $1.8B (reported). The company says annualised revenue passed $1B, up fivefold, and tokens served rose from 15 to over 40 trillion a day. Headcount goes from about 200 to 600 by year-end.

Inference has been sold to the grid twice: Groq at McLaren since September 2025 and CoreWeave at Aston Martin since May 2025, each signed in the year the vendor needed a public story. Fireworks is the inference company whose customers, Uber, DoorDash, Notion, Samsung and Verizon, are names a paddock recognises, and it has no seat. A $17.5B private company with $1B of revenue has the public market as its next audience.

Why Now

The round closed on 16 July and headcount is set to triple by December; brand budgets follow the hiring plan. The United States GP in October and the Las Vegas GP in November fall inside the window, and teams close 2027 rosters in the fourth quarter.

WHY VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS

Racing Bulls has built the grid's most explicit real-time data stack without an inference layer: Confluent streams more than a million data points a second between car, pit wall and factory, Dynatrace is Official Observability and Performance Analytics partner, RebelDot builds software, and there is no AI-inference, cloud or model partner. Faenza and the Milton Keynes aero site are fed by that stream. A challenger team with a younger audience suits a developer-first brand, priced below the works teams.

VALUE TO VISA CASH APP RACING BULLS  — MODE A (tech in the car / championship)

MODE A - operational. The inference layer on the Confluent stream: open-weight models fine-tuned on the team's own telemetry and strategy history, served at low latency for engineer copilots during sessions and for post-session analysis, hosted by Fireworks so no cost-cap capital is spent. Fireworks runs this shape for Uber and DoorDash. Part of the consideration in inference credits; for Fireworks, a reference deployment to show enterprise buyers.

Deal Architecture

Entry as Official AI Inference Partner. THREE YEARS, 2027-2029, spanning the hiring plan and any listing window. Estimated $3-5M a year, part-payable in inference credits. Scope: sidepod or halo placement, naming of the team's model environment, US-race hospitality for enterprise accounts, engineering content from Faenza.
