FACT QUALITY DISCIPLINE — PREMIUM STANDARD:

This brief lands on the desks of motorsport commercial directors, F1 team principals, and chief sponsorship officers at companies running $100M+ marketing budgets. The reader's first instinct on every claim is: "is this real, or is this consultant-speak?" Every fact must survive that scrutiny.

Strong: "a $14B Series C closed 11 March 2026" or "a $14B post-money valuation per the S-1/A filing". Undated figures read as guesswork — assume the reader cross-checks every number against the source URL.
- ANCHORED PERCENTAGES. Every percentage must carry the base and time period. Weak: "revenue up 76%". Strong: "revenue up 76% year-on-year, $510M FY2025". Unmoored percentages tell the reader nothing actionable.

Replace with the underlying figure, or delete the sentence. The deck and THE CASE in particular must read as facts the reader could verify in 30 seconds — not as marketing prose.
- QUANTIFIED REACH. When describing audience or scale, use specifics from the signal data. Weak: "large enterprise base". Weak: "key race weekends". Strong: "Miami, Austin, Las Vegas — US-market grands prix aligned to enterprise audience density".

Weak: "a seasoned commercial leader". Strong: "CMO since November 2025; previously VP Marketing at Snowflake where she built the enterprise sponsorship programme that included F1 paddock activation."
